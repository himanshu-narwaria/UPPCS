@ 20 | Order of Precedence
## Meaning
- **Order of precedence** = protocol list recording functionaries and authorities by rank in the Central and State Governments, including foreign diplomats and awardees.
- It is meant for **state and ceremonial occasions**; it has no application in the day-to-day functioning of the Government.
- The notification on it was released on **26 July 1979**.
- The President of India is at the highest place in the order of precedence.
- Sequence: President → Vice-President → Prime Minister → Speaker (Lok Sabha) → Members of Parliament. {1}
- Sequence: Chief Justice of India → Union Cabinet Ministers → Chief Election Commissioner → Cabinet Secretary. {2}
- Chief Justice of India and Speaker of Lok Sabha are **equal** (both rank 6) in the table of precedence.
- Chairman UPSC, Chief Election Commissioner and CAG are all at **9A**; Chief Justice of a High Court is at **14**. {5}

## Cabinet Secretary
- **Cabinet Secretary** = topmost (highest ranking) civilian officer in the Government of India. {6,7}
- Cabinet Secretary is ex-officio head of the Civil Services Board, Cabinet Secretariat and Indian Administrative Service (IAS).
- Cabinet Secretary is head of all Civil Services under the Rules of Business of the Government of India.
