@ 6 | Articles of the Constitution
## Number of Articles
- The original Constitution had **22 Parts, 395 Articles and 8 Schedules**. {2,4}
- Many Articles were added and removed later through amendments.
- At present there are around **468 Articles** (more than 400), added with alphanumeric suffixes like 21A, 43B to the original 395. {1}
- There are **12 Schedules** at present. {5}

- Article 117 has three clauses giving conditions for Financial Bills.
