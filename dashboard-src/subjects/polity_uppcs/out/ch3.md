## Drafting Committee
- The **Drafting Committee** was formed by a resolution on **29 August 1947** under Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. {12}
- Its task: discuss and decide the form and structure of the Constitution.
- It had **7 members** including the chairman = **6 other members**. {8}
- Original other members: Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar, N. Gopalaswamy Ayyangar, **K.M. Munshi**, Mohammad Saadullah, B.L. Mitter, D.P. Khaitan; Nehru and Sardar Patel were not members. {9,10,11}
- **N. Madhav Rao** later replaced B.L. Mitter.
- **T.T. Krishnamachari** was included after the death of D.P. Khaitan in **1948**.
- K.M. Munshi's full name: Kanhaiyalal Maneklal Munshi.
## National Flag committees
- The Constituent Assembly appointed the **Ad hoc Committee on the National Flag** on **23 June 1947**; chairman **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**. {13}
- Its members: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (Chairman), Abul Kalam Azad, Sarojini Naidu, C. Rajagopalachari, K.M. Munshi, Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar.
- An earlier Flag Committee was formed on **2 April 1931** at the Karachi Session under **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**.
- J.B. Kripalani was neither chairman nor member of any Flag Committee.
