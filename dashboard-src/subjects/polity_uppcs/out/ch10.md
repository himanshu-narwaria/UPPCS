## National Flag
- National Flag (Tricolour) is rectangular and has three colours; ratio of length to width (height) = **3:2** (width to length = 2:3).
- Top band = Indian saffron (Kesariya): symbol of strength and courage.
- Middle band = white: symbol of peace and truth.
- Bottom band = Indian green: symbol of fertility, growth and auspiciousness of the land.
- Centre of the white band has a navy blue wheel representing the Ashoka Chakra, with **24 spokes**. {11}
- Wheel design is that of the wheel on the abacus of the Sarnath lion capital of Ashoka.
- The wheel is a depiction of the Dharma Chakra (Wheel of the Law); it symbolizes **Justice**. {15}
- National flag was designed by **Pingali Venkayya**.
- Present design of the National Flag was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on **22 July, 1947**, during its fourth session. {5,6,7,8,9}
- Dr. S. Radhakrishnan's description of the importance of the three colours and the wheel is reproduced in the Flag Code of India.
## Flag Code of India, 2002
- Flag Code of India 2002 allowed unrestricted display of the Tricolour as long as the honour and dignity of the flag are respected.
- Flag Code of India 2002 took effect from 26 January, 2002.
- The Code has three parts: (1) general description of the tricolour, (2) rules on display by public and private bodies and educational institutions, (3) rules on display by governments and government bodies.
- Code: the National Flag shall be rectangular and the ratio of length to height (width) shall be 3:2.
- 600 mm × 400 mm is not one of the nine standard sizes of the National Flag. {10}

## State Emblem
- State Emblem is an adaptation of the Sarnath lion capital of Ashoka (Ashoka pillar at Sarnath, Varanasi).
- State Emblem was adopted by the Government of India on **26 January, 1950**. {4}
- 'Satyameva Jayate' (national motto) from the **Mundaka Upanishad** is inscribed at the base of the emblem in Devanagari script.
## National Anthem and National Song
- National Anthem 'Jana Gana Mana' was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on **24 January, 1950**. {1}
- 'Jana Gana Mana' was composed originally in Bengali by Rabindranath Tagore; its Hindi version was adopted as the National Anthem on 24 January, 1950. {2}
- 'Jana Gana Mana' was first sung on 27 December, 1911 at the Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress (President: Pt. Bishan Narayan Dar).
- Playing time of the full (formal) version of the anthem is approximately **52 seconds**. {3}
- Shortened version of the anthem (first and last lines) takes 20 seconds.
- 'Vande Mataram' was adopted as the National Song by the Constituent Assembly on 24 January, 1950.
- 'Vande Mataram' is taken from the novel 'Anand Math' by Bankimchandra Chatterjee.
- 'Vande Mataram' was first sung at the 1896 session of the Indian National Congress (President: Rahimatullah Sayani).
## National Calendar
- National Calendar is based on the **Saka era** (first month Chaitra, last month Falgun); adopted on **22 March, 1957** along with the Gregorian Calendar. {16}
- Chaitra generally begins on 22 March; in leap years it begins on 21 March.
