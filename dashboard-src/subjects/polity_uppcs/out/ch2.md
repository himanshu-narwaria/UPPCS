@ 2 | Constituent Assembly and making of the Constitution
## Demand for a Constituent Assembly
- First formal demand for a Constituent Assembly: **Swaraj Party**, at its Ranchi conference, **May 1934**; first time Indians demanded it. {1}
- The Swaraj Party wanted it composed of Indian representatives elected on adult suffrage.
- The idea of a Constituent Assembly was first put forward by **M.N. Roy** in **1934**. {2}
- Indian National Congress first officially demanded a Constituent Assembly (after the Swaraj Party) in May 1934, at **Patna**.
- In **1938**, Jawaharlal Nehru, for the INC, declared: free India's constitution must be framed without outside interference by a Constituent Assembly elected on adult franchise.
- Mahatma Gandhi: "Constituent Assembly alone can produce a Constitution indigenous to the country and truly and fully representing the will of the people". {3}
- Gandhi wrote this in his article **"The Only Way"** in the **Harijan** of **19 November 1939**.
- The demand was finally accepted in principle in the **August Offer of 1940**.
- **Cripps Mission (1942)** proposed that an elected Constituent Assembly frame India's Constitution after the Second World War. {4}
- Cripps Mission was sent by the British government to India in **March 1942**.
- Cripps proposal: setting up Dominion status.
- Cripps proposal: any province unwilling to join the Indian dominion could form a separate union with a separate constitution. {5}
- Cripps proposal: the Constituent Assembly would have members **elected** by provincial assemblies and **nominated** by princely states.
- **Third Round Table Conference** discussions led to the Government of India Act 1935, which provided an All India Federation of British Indian provinces and princely states. {66}
- Third and last Round Table Conference: **November–December 1932**, London; Congress did not take part.
## Wavell Plan
- **Lord Wavell** succeeded Lord Linlithgow as Viceroy in **October 1943** and tried to end the deadlock in India.
- He went to England for consultation in **March 1945**; his proposals were made public in **June 1945** as the Wavell Plan.
- Wavell Plan proposed equal Hindu and Muslim members in the Executive Council; it was not meant to avoid partition. {14}
- Wavell Plan: restructure the Governor-General's Executive Council with balanced representation of main communities.
- All members except the Viceroy and Commander-in-Chief were to be Indian.
- All portfolios except Defence, including the foreign department, were to be held by Indian members.
- A conference at **Shimla** was to be called at the earliest.
- It proposed that after the Second World War, Indians would make a Constitution for India.
- Aim of the Wavell Plan: remove the deadlock on constitutional and political issues and promote self-governance for Indians.
## Cabinet Mission and composition
- The Constituent Assembly was constituted under the **Cabinet Mission Plan 1946**. {8,9}
- Cabinet Mission arrived in India on **24 March 1946**.
- Cabinet Mission members: **Sir Pethick-Lawrence** (Secretary for India), **Stafford Cripps** (Chairman, Board of Trade), **A.V. Alexander** (Navy Minister); William Wood was not a member. {7}
- Provinces' seats were based on population: about **one representative per 10 lakh** (a million) people. {6}
- Seats: **296** to British India and **93** to Princely States (a minimum of 93).
- Of 296: **292** from the eleven Governor's provinces and **4** from the four Chief Commissioners' provinces (one each).
- Princely states' representatives were to be nominated by the heads of the princely states.
- The Assembly was partly elected and partly nominated.
- Members from provinces were elected by the **Provincial Legislative Assemblies**, which acted as the electoral college. {18,19,20}
- The Assembly came from indirect election, not adult franchise; it was a multi-party body and worked through committees. {15,16,17}
- Provincial legislature members were themselves elected on limited franchise based on tax, education and property (Government of India Act 1935, sixth schedule).
- Universal adult franchise lets all citizens join in governance on the basis of adult suffrage.
- Congress, Muslim League and some independent members got representation in the Assembly.
- Minority communities like Christians, Anglo-Indians and Parsis were adequately represented.
- The Assembly had many committees: **8 major** committees, others minor; the Drafting Committee was the most important.
- Elections for the 296 seats were held in **July–August 1946**.
- Results: Indian National Congress **208** seats, Muslim League **73**, small groups and independents **15**.
- Princely states did not take part at first; their representatives joined gradually from the third session starting **28 April 1947**.
- Members of the Assembly: H.C. Mukherjee = first Vice-President; K.M. Munshi = originally the only Congress member of the Drafting Committee; V.T. Krishnamachari = representative of Rajasthan's princely state; Jawaharlal Nehru = Chairman, Union Constitution Committee. {47}
- **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** was elected to the Constituent Assembly from the **Pune seat of Bombay Presidency**. {64}
- In the 1946 preliminary election Ambedkar was first elected from the eastern part of undivided Bengal (East Bengal), which later went to Pakistan.
- The Pune seat had been vacated by the resignation of **M.R. Jaikar** (of Congress).
- Ambedkar was born on **14 April 1891** at Dr. Ambedkar Nagar (then Mhow), Madhya Pradesh; died on **6 December 1956** at Delhi. {65}
- Women members in the Constituent Assembly = **15**. {73}
- The 15 women: Vijayalakshmi Pandit, Rajkumari Amrit Kaur, Sarojini Naidu, Sucheta Kripalani, Purnima Banerjee, Leela Roy, G. Durgabai (Deshmukh), Hansa Mehta, Kamala Choudhry, Renuka Ray, Malati Choudhury, Dakshayani Velayudhan, Begum Aizaz Rasul, Annie Mascarene, Ammu Swaminathan.
## Interim Government 1946
- The first Interim National Government was announced on **24 August 1946**, to take charge by 2 September 1946. {13}
- Interim Government was formed on **2 September 1946** from the newly elected Constituent Assembly, as per the Cabinet Mission's declaration.
- At first it had no Muslim League member; on **26 October 1946**, 5 Muslim League members joined the Cabinet.
- The Viceroy was ex-officio President of the Executive Council.
- **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan** was not a member of the Interim Government. {11}

## First meeting and office bearers
- The Constituent Assembly was established with its first meeting on **9 December 1946**; the Viceroy invited the elected members. {31,32,33,34,35}
- First session: 9 December 1946 to 23 December 1946; **207 members** attended (Rajya Sabha website).
- It met in **Constitution Hall, New Delhi**, now the Central Hall of old Parliament House.
- Nehru was a member of the Assembly from the very first day of its proceedings. {27}
- The **Muslim League boycotted** the first meeting; Dr. Sachchidanand Sinha was not its first permanent President. {26}
- **Dr. Sachchidanand Sinha**, the oldest member, chaired the first meeting as temporary (provisional) President. {21,23,24}
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was unanimously elected permanent President (Chairman) of the Assembly on **11 December 1946**. {22,25,28,29}
- Dr. Rajendra Prasad's name was proposed by **J.B. Kripalani**.
- **Frank Anthony** was the first nominated (temporary) Deputy Chairman, nominated on 9 December 1946 by Dr. Sachchidanand Sinha. {30}
- Frank Anthony founded the **All India Anglo-Indian Association** in 1942.
- Frank Anthony was a nominated Lok Sabha member for **seven terms** between 1950 and 1992.
- **H.C. Mukherjee** was the first permanent Vice-President of the Constituent Assembly.
- **B.N. Rau** (Sir Benegal Narsing Rau) was the Constitutional Advisor to the Assembly. {48}
- The first draft of the Constitution was prepared by B.N. Rau and put before the Drafting Committee for opinion, discussion and changes. {49}
- Rau's original draft had **243 Articles and 13 Schedules**.
## Objectives Resolution
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** moved the **Objectives Resolution** on **13 December 1946**, in the fifth meeting of the first session. {36,37,39,40}
- The Assembly unanimously adopted the Objectives Resolution on **22 January 1947**. {38}
- It gave the philosophy and guiding principles for constitution-making and shaped all later stages.
- Its amended form became the **Preamble** of the Constitution.
- Nehru's Objectives Resolution affected constitution-making; the Preamble fulfils important aims; the people of India ordained the Constitution; the President is not directly elected. {41}
- The President is elected by elected members of both Houses of Parliament and of State Legislative Assemblies of all States including Union Territories.
## Making and adoption of the Constitution
- The **Drafting Committee** was set up on **29 August 1947**: seven members, chairman **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**; task = prepare the draft Constitution.
- Constitution-making took **2 years, 11 months and 17 days** (Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha websites); exam answers accept 2 years, 11 months, 18 days. {44,45}
- **11 sessions** covering **165 days** were held; one more meeting on 24 January 1950 makes 12 the nearest exam answer. {42}
- Third reading of the Constitution began on **17 November 1949** and ended on **26 November 1949**.
- The **Constituent Assembly** adopted and enacted the Constitution on **26 November 1949**, the last day of its 11th session. {51,52,53,54,60,61,62}
- The Preamble itself says: "We the People of India enact, adopt and give to ourselves this Constitution on 26 November 1949".
- 26 November 1949 = date of adoption, completion and passing; the Constitution was signed by President Rajendra Prasad.
- Provisions on Citizenship, Interim Parliament, and Temporary, Transitional and special provisions came into force on 26 November 1949 itself.
- The Constitution came fully into force on **26 January 1950** = its commencement (enforcement) date. {55,56}
- 26 January was chosen because Congress had observed it as **Independence Day** since 1930. {57}
- The **Purna Swaraj** declaration was made at the Lahore Session of Congress (December 1929); it asked people to observe 26 January as Independence Day.
- Order of events: Assembly first met 9 December 1946 → Drafting Committee appointed 29 August 1947 → Constitution adopted 26 November 1949 → commenced 26 January 1950. {63}
- The last (final) meeting of the Assembly was on **24 January 1950**, when members signed the Constitution. {43}
- On 24 January 1950 the Assembly adopted the national anthem and national song and elected Dr. Rajendra Prasad the first President of India. {46}
- **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas)** = **26 November** every year, to promote constitutional values among citizens. {58,59}
- Constitution Day was announced by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** on **19 November 2015**.
- The Constitution provides a parliamentary form of government, federal in structure with a certain unitary leaning.
- The Constitution provides for all necessities of the country and has amendment provisions for the future.
- It is called an **"Adopted Constitution"** because it takes views and principles from other countries' constitutions; this does not explain why it meets all needs. {67}
## Views and sayings
- **Maulana Azad** pleaded in the Assembly to defer adult franchise for **15 years**. {68}
- Dr. Rajendra Prasad and Jawaharlal Nehru strongly supported adoption of adult franchise.
- **Mahatma Gandhi** advised that the Indian National Congress, formed only to win independence, be disbanded as a political party after independence. {69}
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**: "A Constitution, like a machine, is a lifeless thing… India needs today nothing more than a set of honest men"; said on 26 November 1949. {70}
- **Sir Ivor Jennings**: the Founding Fathers "tended to minimize the importance of minority interests and emotions" (on minority protection provisions). {71}
- **Granville Austin**: "Constituent Assembly was Congress and Congress was India", in his book "The Indian Constitution". {72}
- Granville Austin was a famous historian and Padma Shri award holder.
