@ 5 | Parts of the Indian Constitution
## Number of Parts
- The Constitution was originally divided into **22 Parts**, with 395 Articles and 8 Schedules. {1}
- At present there are a total of **25 Parts**.
- **Part IV-A** and **Part XIV-A** were added by the **42nd Amendment Act 1976**.
- **Part IX-A** was added by the **74th Amendment Act 1992** (with effect from 1-6-1993).
- **Part IX-B** was added by the **97th Amendment Act 2011**.

## Details of some Parts
- Part IV-A (Fundamental Duties) was inserted in **1976**.
- **Attorney General of India** is in **Part V (Article 76)**, not Part XIII. {17}
- Part VI covers the Governor, Council of Ministers, State Legislature, High Court.
- **Part XI, Chapter I** = distribution of legislative powers between Union and States; **Chapter II** = administrative relations. {6}
- Subject → Part: abolition of titles = Fundamental Rights; village panchayats = Directive Principles; education for child 6–14 years = Fundamental Duties; "India, that is Bharat, is the Union of States" = Union and its Territories. {7}
- Tribunals were inserted by the 42nd Amendment Act 1976: **Article 323-A** = Administrative Tribunals; **Article 323-B** = tribunals for other matters.
- Part XV covers the Election Commission's establishment and powers, electoral rolls, and elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and offices of President and Vice-President.
- **Article 243-W** (Part IX-A) = powers, authority and responsibilities of Municipalities.
- **Article 243-X** = power of Municipalities to impose taxes.
## Panchayats and the 73rd and 74th Amendments
- The **73rd and 74th Amendments** were passed by Parliament in **1992**.
- 73rd Amendment = rural local government (Panchayati Raj Institutions); 74th Amendment = urban local government (Nagarpalikas).
- The 73rd Amendment put the **three-tier Panchayat system** in Part IX.
- Base level: **Gram Panchayat**, covering a village or group of villages.
- Intermediate level: Block, Mandal or Taluka Panchayat.
- The intermediate level need not be set up in smaller states with population below **20 lakhs**.
- Apex level: **Zila Panchayat**, covering the whole rural area of the district.
