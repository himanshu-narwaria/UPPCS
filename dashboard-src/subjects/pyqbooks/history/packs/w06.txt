Q: In which of the following sessions of Indian National Congress, the word ‘Swaraj’ was uttered for the first time?
Options: (a) Benaras Session, 1905 (b) Calcutta Session, 1906 (c) Surat Session, 1907 (d) None of the above
Answer: (b) Calcutta Session, 1906
Explanation: At the Calcutta Congress session, ‘Swaraj’ was uttered as the goal of the Indian people in December, 1906 A.D. In his presidential address, Naoroji declared that the goal of the Indian National Congress ‘Self-Government or Swaraj, was same as of United Kingdom’.
