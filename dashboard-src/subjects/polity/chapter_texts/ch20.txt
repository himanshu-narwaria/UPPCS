Order of Precedence
*The order of precedence is a protocol list in which the functionaries and authorities are recorded by the rank in the Central and
State Governments, including foreign diplomats and awardees.
*It is meant for state and ceremonial occasions and has no
application in the day-to-day functioning of the Government.
*The notification regarding this was released on 26 July, 1979.

1. Arrange the following words in a logical and
meaningful order -
1. Vice President 2. President
3. Speaker 4. Prime Minister
5. Member of Parliament
(a) 5, 1, 2, 3, 4
(b) 4, 2, 1, 3, 5
(c) 2, 1, 4, 3, 5
(d) More than one of the above
(d) None of the above
68th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2022
Ans. (c)
In the Indian Political System under the constitution, the
President of India is at the highest order, according to the
order of precedence of top-officials of the country. The
posts mentioned in the question follow as- Vice-President,
Prime Minister, Speaker (Lok Sabha) and then members of
parliament. Therefore, the correct answer is option (c).

6. Who is the topmost civilian officer in Government of
India?
(a) Home Secretary (b) Defence Secretary
(c) Cabinet Secretary (d) Lt. Governor of Delhi
M.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1998
Ans. (c)
The Cabinet Secretary is the topmost civilian officer in the
Government of India. The Cabinet Secretary is the ex-officio
head of the Civil Services Board, Cabinet Secretariat, Indian
Administrative Service (IAS) and head of all Civil Services
under the Rules of Business of the Government of India.
