Legislative Assembly
*Articles 168 to 212 in Part VI of the Indian Constitution deal 
with the State Legislature. *According to Article 168 the State 
legislature, in specified States, consists of Legislative Council, 
Legislative Assembly and governor of the state, and in the other 
States it consists of Legislative Assembly and the Governor 
of the State. *The Legislative Assembly of each state shall be 
composed of Members elected by direct election on the basis 
of Universal Adult Suffrage from territorial constituencies.
*The maximum number of members of the Assembly shall not 
be more than 500 nor less than 60 members, generally. *The 
smallest assembly among Union territories is Puducherry (33) 
and in states it is Sikkim (32). *Uttar Pradesh has the largest 
Legislative Assembly with 403 members. *The Election 
Commission of India conducts the elections for the state 
legislatures. *According to Article 170 (2) Each state shall 
be divided into territorial constituencies in such a manner that 
the ratio between the Population of each constituency and the 
number of seats allotted to it shall, so far as practicable, be 
the same throughout the state. *The Speaker of the assembly 
exercises a casting vote in case of equality of votes. *The 
speaker establishes the contact between the house and the 
Governor. *In the absence of a speaker, the deputy speaker 
works as the speaker of the Assembly. *According to the 
84th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2001, the number of 
members in State Assemblies will remain the same until the 
relevant figures for the first census taken after the year 2026 
have been published. *Under section 14(3) of the Jammu and 
Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 the number of a ssembly 
members in Jammu & Kashmir UT is 90 elected and 5 
nominated [plus 24 vacant election constituencies fall in areas 
occupied by Pakistan (POK) ]. *Only three Union Territories 
(Delhi, Puducherry and Jammu & Kashmir) have legislative 
assemblies. Article 173 lays down the qualifications of a person 
to be a member of the legislative assembly (a) He must be a 
citizen of India, and (b) He must not be less than 25 years of 
age. *According to Article 332 the Constitution provides for the 
reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes 
in the legislative assembly in proportion to their population. 
*The Rights and Functions of the Speaker of the Legislative 
Assembly are the same as those of the Speaker of the lower 
House of the Parliament.
