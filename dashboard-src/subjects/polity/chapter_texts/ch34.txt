Lokpal and Important 
Commissions
*The earliest democratic institution created in the world for the 
redressal of citizens' grievances is the Scandinavian institution 
of Ombudsman. *The institution of the Ombudsman was first 
created in Sweden in 1809. In order to meet the long-standing 
demand to establish a mechanism for dealing with complaints 
on corruption against certain public functionaries, the Indian 
Democratic system proposed the establishment of Lokpal at the 
centre and Lokayukta at the State level. *In its interim report 
dated 20th October, 1966, under the Chairmanship of Morarji 
Desai, the First Administrative Reforms Commission (1966-
1970) recommended the setting up of Lokpal and Lokayuktas. 
Later in the year 1967, K. Hanumanthaiah became its Chairman. 
*Bills regarding Lokpal were first introduced in the year 1968, 
in the Parliament (in the 4 th Lok Sabha). *However, on 20 th 
August, 1969, the first Jan Lokpal Bill was passed in the Fourth 
Lok Sabha. In the year 1972, Maharashtra became the first 
State to establish the institution of Lokayukta. The Lokayukta 
Act of Maharashtra was enacted on 30 November, 1971 and it 
came into effect on 25th October, 1972. *Although Odisha had 
passed the Act in this regard in 1970, it came into force in 1983. 
*According to the Uttar Pradesh Lokayukta Act 1975, the 
Lokayukta submits his report to the Governor. *The Governor 
presents this report in both Houses of the State Legislature. 
Institution of Lokpal has been established for the first time in 
India by 'The Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013'. The Lokpal 
consists of a chairperson and not more than eight members. 
Former Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose was appointed India's 
first Lokpal. *In the year 1993, the Vohra Committee was setup 
to study the problem of the criminalisation of Politicians and 
bureaucrats in India. *Rajamannar Committee recommended 
the abolition of Indian Administrative Services and Indian 
Police Services. In the year 1993, the then- President exercised 
his powers under Article 123 and promulgated the Protection 
of Human Rights Ordinance. *As a result, the Protection of 
Human Rights Act came into force on 28th September 1993. 
*The National Human Rights Commission is a statutory (non-
constitutional) body. This organization was established on 12th 
October 1993. Its Chairman and members are appointed by the 
President, on the recommendations of a 6-member committee 
consisting of the Prime Minister as its head, the speaker of 
the Lok Sabha, the Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha, the 
Leader of the Opposition in both Houses of the Parliament and 
the Home Minister. *The Function of the Commission is mainly 
recommendatory in nature. The Protection of Human Rights 
(Amendment) Act, 2019 amended the Protection of Human 
Rights Act, 1993. Some major amendments are -
(i) The Act provides that a person who has been the Chief 
Justice of India, or a judge of the Supreme Court will be the 
chairperson of the NHRC.
(ii) The Act provides for three persons (of which at least one will 
be a woman) having knowledge of or practical experience 
in matters relating to human rights to be appointed as the 
members of the NHRC.



(iii) The Act reduces the term of office of the chairman and 
members of the NHRC and SHRC to three years or till the 
age of 70 years, whichever is earlier.
*The chairperson and members of the State Finance Commission 
are appointed by the Governor. *The Central Information 
Commission was established by an official Gazette Notification 
under the provisions of the Right to Information Act 2005. 
According to the Right to Information Act, 2005 the term of the 
Chief Information Commissioner was up to 5 years or 65 years 
of age. According to the Right to Information (Amendment) 
Act, 2019 the tenure of the Chief Information Commissioner 
and other Information Commissioners will be determined by 
the Central Government. In addition to the central and state 
level, Chief Information Commissioner and other information 
commissioners will receive the same salary, allowance and 
other facilities as the Central Government determines.  *In 
the year 1979, under the Chairmanship of Bindeshwari Prasad 
Mandal, the Mandal Commission was established. *The main 
task of this Commission was to identify and recommend steps 
for the advancement of the socially and educationally backward 
classes of India. *Article 315 of the Indian Constitution deals 
with the establishment of Public Service Commission for the 
Union and for the States.
*The Union Public Service Commission consists of a 
Chairman and other Members appointed by the President 
of India, whereas the Chairman and Members of the State 
Public Service Commission are appointed by the Governor 
of the State. *According to Article 323, the UPSC presents 
annually, to the President, a report on its performance, whereas 
the SPSCs present annually, to the Governor a report on their 
performance. *According to Article 322, the expenses of the 
Union or a State Public Service Commission shall be charged 
on the Consolidated Fund of India or the Consolidated Fund 
of State (in case of SPSCs).
1. According to the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 
the Lokpal would be appointed by the President 
after obtaining the recommendations of a Selection 
Committee, with the Prime Minister as chairperson 
and consisting, among others, of the following :
 1. Chief Justice of India or a Supreme Court Judge 
nominated by him.
 2. Chairman of the Rajya Sabha
 3. Speaker of the Lok Sabha
 4. Leader of Opposition in the Lok Sabha
 Select the correct answer from the codes given below :
 (a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2, 3 and 4
 (c) 1, 2, 3 and 4 (d) 1, 3 and 4
U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
Ans. (d)
According to the section 4(1) of the Lokpal and Lokayuktas 
Act, 2013, The Chairperson and the members of the Lokpal 
shall be appointed by the President after obtaining the 
recommendations of a selection committee consisting of-
(a) the Prime Minister - Chairperson;
(b) the Speaker of the House of the people - Member; 
(c) the Leader of Opposition in the House of the People - 
     Member;
(d) the Chief Justice of India or a judge of the Supreme 
Court nominated by him- Member; 
(e) One eminent jurist, as recommended by the Chairperson 
and members referred to in clauses (a) to (d) above, to be 
nominated by the President - Member.
Hence, option (d) is the correct answer.
2. Consider the following statements regarding Lokpal 
and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 : 
  1.  The Selection Committee of Lokpal consists of 
Chairperson of Rajya Sabha. 
  2.  It shall apply to Indian public servants both within 
and outside India. 
 Which of the above statements is/are correct ?   
 (a) Only 1  (b) Only 2
 (c) 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2 
Uttarakhand P.C.S. (Pre) 2025
Ans. (b)
Statement 1 is incorrect, because the Selection Committee

within India and outside India.
3.  Consider the following statements about Lokpal :
 I. The power of Lokpal applies to public servants of 
India, but not to the Indian public servants posted 
outside India.
 II. The Chairperson or a Member shall not be a 
Member of the Parliament or a Member of the 
Legislature of any State or Union Territory, and only 
the Chief Justice of India, whether incumbent or 
retired, has to be its Chairperson.



 III. The Chairperson or a Member shall not be a 
person of less than forty-five years of age on the date 
of assuming office as the Chairperson or Member, as 
the case may be.
 IV . Lokpal cannot inquire into the allegations of 
corruption against a sitting Prime Minister of India.
 Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
 (a) III only
 (b) II and III
 (c) I and IV
 (d) None of the above statements is correct
I.A.S. (Pre) 2025
Ans. (a)
Lokpal’s jurisdiction applies to Indian public servants 
both within India and outside India. Therefore, Statement 
I is incorrect. The Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 does 
not say that only the Chief Justice of India (CJI) can be 
the Chairperson; apart from the CJI, other Judges of the 
Supreme Court or a qualified ‘eminent person’ can also be 
the Chairperson. A member of parliament or a member of 
the legislature of any state or union territory shall not be a 
member/chairperson of the Lokpal. Therefore, Statement 
II is incorrect. The minimum age for the Chairperson or a 
Member is fixed at 45 years, and this age requirement applies 
on the date the person assumes office as Chairperson/Member. 
The Prime Minister is included within the jurisdiction of the 
Lokpal. Therefore, Statement IV is incorrect.

21. Under Section 7 of the Rajasthan Lokayukta 
and Deputy Lokayukta Act, 1973, the Lokayukta 
isempowered to investigate allegations against 
ministers and public servants in certain cases. 
 Which of the following subject is not part of those 
investigations?
 (a) Unreasonable harm or suffering caused by public 
servants.
 (b) Abusing his official position as a public servant to 
obtain illegal benefits for himself or any other person.
 (c) Sexual harassment of women, caste discrimination 
and violence against children.
 (d) Can be related to being guilty of corruption or lack of 
transparency in the capacity of a public servant.
 (e) Question not attempted
Raj. P.C.S. (Pre) 2024

The Section 7 of the Rajasthan Lokayukta and Deputy

24.  Consider the following statements about the minorities 
in India:
 1. The Government of India has notified five 
communities, namely, Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, 
Buddhists and Zoroastrians as minorities.
 2. The National Commission for Minorities was given 
statutory status in 1993.
 3. The smallest religious minority in India are the 
Zoroastrians.
 4.  The Constitution of India recognizes and protects

 Which of these statements are correct ?
 (a) 2 and 3 (b)  1 and 4
 (c) 2, 3 and 4 (d)  1, 2 and 4

Minorities Act, 1992 (came into force on 17 th May, 1993) 
the minority commission became a statutory body and was

32. Other than the Fundamental Rights, which of the 
following parts of the Constitution of India reflect/
reflects the principles and provisions of the Universal 
Declaration of Human Rights (1948)?
 1. Preamble
 2. Directive Principles of State Policy
 3. Fundamental Duties
 Select the correct answer using the code given below:
 Code :
 (a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 only
 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
I.A.S. (Pre) 2020
Ans. (d)



The Following Parts of the Indian Constitution reflect the 
Principles and Provisions of the Universal Declaration of 
Human Rights, 1948.
According to Article 3 of UDHR (1948), everyone has the 
right to life, liberty and the security of a person. It is reflected 
in the Preamble and the Fundamental Rights of the Indian 
Constitution.
According to Article 26 of the UDHR (1948), everyone has 
the right to education. Education shall be free, at least in the 
elementary and fundamental stages. Elementary education 
shall be compulsory. Technical and professional education 
shall be made generally available and higher education shall 
be equally accessible to all on the basis of merit. It is reflected 
in Article 45 of the Directive Principles of State Policy of  
the Indian Constitution.
According to Article 29 of the UDHR everyone has 
duties to the community in which alone the free and full 
development of their personality is possible. It is reflected in 
the fundamental duties of the Indian Constitution.
Thus, all the statements are correct. Hence, the correct answer 
is option (d).
