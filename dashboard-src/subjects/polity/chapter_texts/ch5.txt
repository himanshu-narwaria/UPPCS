Parts of the Indian Constitution
The Indian Constitution is divided into 22 parts. (at present,
a total of 25 parts)* Part IV-A and Part XIV-A were added by
the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976.*Part IX-A was added by the
74th Amendment Act, 1992.*Part IX-B was added by the 97th
Amendment Act, 2011.

5. Which part of our Constitution envisages a three tier
system of Panchayats?
(a) Part IX (b) Part X
(c) Part XI (d) Part XII
U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2013
Ans. (a)
In 1992, the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments
were passed by the Parliament. The 73rd Amendment is
about Rural Local Government. (known as Panchayati Raj
Institution). The 74th Amendment is related to Urban Local
Government (Nagarpalikas). 73rd Amendment introduces
three-tier Panchayat System in Part 9 of the Constitution. At
the base is Gram Panchayat, which covers a village or group
of villages. The Intermediary level is the Block, Mandal or
Taluka Panchayat. The intermediary level body need not be
constituted in smaller states having a population of less than
20 lakhs. At the Apex is the Zila Panchayat, which covers
the entire rural area of the District.
6. Which part and chapter of the Indian Constitution deal
with the legislative relation between the Union and the
States?
(a) Part XI and Chapter I (b) Part XI and Chapter II
(c) Part XII and Chapter I (d) Part XII and Chapter II
U.P. P.C.S. (Mains) 2012
Ans. (a)
Part XI of the Indian Constitution deals with the relation
between the Union and the states. Chapter 1 of Part XI is
about the distribution of legislative power between the Union
and the states, whereas chapter 2 deals with administrative
relations between them. Part XII deals with Finance, Property,
Contract and Suit.

Part XV of the Constitution (Articles 324 to 329) is titled

establishment and powers of the Election Commission,
preparation of electoral rolls, and conduct of elections to

President and Vice-President.

20. Which of the following statements are correct about
the Constitution of India?
1. Powers of the Municipalities are given in Part IX A
of the Constitution.
2. Emergency provisions are given in Part XVIII of
the Constitution.
3. Provisions related to the amendment of the
Constitution are given in Part XX the Constitution.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
I.A.S. (Pre) 2024
Ans. (d)
Part IXA (Article 243-P to 243-ZG) of the Constitution
of India was inserted by the Constitution (Seventy-fourth
Amendment) Act, 1992 (w.e.f. 1-6-1993). It contains
provisions related to the Municipalities. Article 243-W under
Part IXA deals with powers, authority and responsibilities of
Municipalities, etc., while the power to impose taxes by the
Municipalities is given in Article 243-X. Hence, statement
1 is correct.
Emergency provisions are given in Part XVIII (Articles 352
to 360) of the Constitution of India. Hence, statement 2 is
correct.
Provisions related to the amendment of the Constitution are
given in Part XX (Article 368) of the Constitution of India.
Hence, statement 3 is also correct.
