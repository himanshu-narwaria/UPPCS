*The Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954 was 
introduced in 1954 and came into force on 1 June, 1955 
(Repealed w.e.f. 5 August, 2011). *All the punishable offences 
under the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 are cognizable 
and triable summarily.
*The trials of crime under the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 
1955 are done by a First Class Judicial Magistrate  and in 
the case of Metropolis, it is done by Metropolitan Magistrate. 
*The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005 
was brought into force on 26 October, 2006. *The Criminal 
Tribes Act was first enacted in 1871. *The Scheduled Castes 
and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 
1989 has been applicable in India since 30 January 1990. To 
implement the objectives of The Scheduled Castes and The 
Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, the 
central government has the power to make rules by notification 
in the Official Gazette. *Section 18 of The Scheduled Castes 
and The Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 
prohibits anticipatory bail to the accused. *According to Rule 
7(1) of the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995, an 
offence committed under this Act shall be investigated by a 
police officer not below the rank of Deputy Superintendent of 
Police. *Under section 14 of this Act, the State Government, 
for the purpose of providing speedy trial, is empowered to 
constitute a court of session, with the concurrence of the Chief 
Justice of the High Court, to be a special court for each district 
to try the offence under the SC & ST Act. *Hindu Marriage 
Act, Hindu Succession Act and The Commission of SATI 
(Prevention) Act were enforced in 1955, 1956 and 1988, 
respectively. *Right to Information came fully into force on 
12 October 2005. The Right to Information is a legal right. 
According to the Supreme Court, it is also a fundamental 
right. The use of the Right to Information Act is to obtain 
Information from the public authority. *Namit sharma V/s 
Union of India case is related to the Right to Information 
Act 2005. The Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan is an Indian 
social movement and grassroots organization best known for 
its demand for the Right to Information Act. The Right to 
Information (Amendment) Act, 2019 removed the previous 
provision i.e., the Chief Information  Commissioner (CIC) and 
Information Commissioner (IC) at the Central and State levels 
will hold office for a term of five years and substituted it by 
term as may be prescribed by the Central Government. *The 
National Green Tribunal was established on 18 October, 2010, 
under the National Green Tribunal Act, 2010. *The provision 
of the Panchayats (extension to Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996 
or PESA is a law enacted by the Parliament of India ensuring 
self-governance through traditional gram sabhas for people 
living in the Scheduled Areas of India.

82. The Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023

The Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023, was 
published in the Official Gazette on August 3, 2023, after

83. Consider the following statements :
 Once the Central Government notifies an area as a 
'Community Reserve'
 1. The Chief Wildlife Warden of the State becomes the 
governing authority of such forest
 2. Hunting is not allowed in such area
 3. People of such area are allowed to collect non-timber 
forest produce
 4. People of such area are allowed traditional 
agricultural practices
 How many of the above statements are correct?
 (a) Only one (b) Only two
 (c) Only three (d) All four
U.P.S.C. (Pre) 2023
Ans. (b)
Statement 1 : Once the Central Government notifies an area 
as a 'Community Reserve', the Chief Wildlife Warden of the 
State becomes the governing authority of such forest. 
Incorrect. According to Section 36C of the Wildlife 
(Protection) Act, 1972, the State government, not the 
Central Government, has the authority to declare an area as 
a Community Reserve. Furthermore, Section 36D specifies 
that the management of such reserves is entrusted to a 
Community Reserve Management Committee, not the Chief 
Wildlife Warden.
Statement 2 : Hunting is not allowed in such an area.
Correct. The Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, prohibits 
hunting in protected areas, including community Reserves, 
to ensure the protection of wildlife.
Statement 3 : People of such an area are allowed to collect 
non-timber forest produce.
Correct. Community reserves aim to involve local 
communities in conservation efforts. While the Act does not 
explicitly mention the collection of non-timber forest produce 
(NTFP), sustainable use of resources by local communities is 
generally permitted under the supervision of the community 
Reserve Management Committee.
Statement 4 : People of such an area are allowed traditional 
agricultural practices.
Incorrect. Traditional agricultural practices, especially those 
that may alter the habitat, are typically restricted within 
community Reserves to maintain the area's conservation 
objectives. The Act emphasizes protecting flora, fauna and  
traditional conservation values, which may limit certain 
land-use practices.
Hence correct answer in option (6).

92. With reference to the Consumer Disputes Redressal at 
the district level in India, which one of the following 
statements is not correct?
 (a) The District Forum entertains the complaints where 
the value of goods or services does not exceed rupees 
fifty lakhs.
 (b) The State Government can establish more than one 
District Forum in a district if it deems fit.
 (c) One of the members of the District Forum shall be a 
woman.
 (d) More than one of the above
 (e) None of the above
68th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2022
Ans. (e)
The correct answer is Option(e)- None of the above. The 
Central Government notified the Consumer Protection 
(Jurisdiction of the District Commission, the State 
Commission, and the National Commission) Rules, 2021.
According to this rule, District Commissions shall have 
jurisdiction to entertain complaints where the value of the 
goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed 50 
lakh rupees. Hence, option (a) is correct.

jurisdiction to entertain complaints where the value of the 
goods or services paid as consideration exceeds 50 lakh

consumer disputes. According to the provisions of Section

more than one district commission in a district. Hence, option

is or has been or is eligible to be appointed as a District Judge 
and each State Commission is led by a person who is or has 
been a Judge of the High Court. It is important to mention

whom shall be a woman. Hence, option (c) is also correct.

(e) is the accurate answer.
