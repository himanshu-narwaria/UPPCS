The Planning Commission /
NITI AAYOG
*The Planning Commission was established by an executive
resolution of the Government of India on 15 March, 1950. *It
was neither a Constitutional body nor a statutory body. *It
was established on the recommendation of an advisory planning
board chaired by K.C. Neogy. Prime Minister used to be its
ex-officio chairman. *First Deputy Chairman of the Planning
Commission was Guljari Lal Nanda. *Its main function was
to assess the resources of the country and prepare five-year
plans for the central government for balanced utilisation of
those resources. *The Planning Commission was replaced by
'NITI Aayog' on 1 January, 2015. *NITI refers to the National Institution for Transforming India. *The current Vice
Chairman of NITI Aayog is Dr. Suman K. Bery. *Its Chief
Executive Officer is B.V.R. Subrahmanyam. *The rank and
status of its vice-chairperson is equivalent to a Cabinet Minister. *The National Development Council was established on
6 August, 1952.
1. The Planning Commission has been abolished by Prime
Minister
(a) Narendra Modi (b) Morarji Desai
(c) Atal Bihari Vajpayee (d) I.K. Gujral
U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2015
Ans. (a)
The Planning Commission was set up in March 1950 by the
first Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru to optimize scarce
resources in newly-born Nation. Prime Minister Narendra
Modi announced on August 15, 2014, from the Red Fort to
abolish the old Planning Commission. In accordance with a
key announcement made by Prime Minister Narendra Modi
on Independence Day, the Union Government established
NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India),
as a replacement for the Planning Commission on January 1,
2015. NITI Aayog will seek to provide a critical directional
and strategic input into the developmental process.

4. Who appoints the Vice Chairman of NITI Aayog?
(a) The President of India.
(b) The Prime Minister of India.
(c) The Vice President of India.
(d) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha.
M.P.P.C.S. (Pre.) 2022
Ans. (b)
On January 1, 2015, the NITI Aayog (National Institution
for Transforming India) was established as the successor to
the Planning Commission. It is a non-constitutional or extraconstitutional body (i.e., not created by the Constitution) and
a non-statutory body (not created by an Act of the Parliament).
It was created by an executive resolution of the Government
of India. The Prime Minister is the ex-officio chairman of
the NITI Aayog. A Vice-Chairperson is appointed by the
Prime Minister. He enjoys the rank of a Cabinet Minister.
Former World Bank economist, Suman Bery is the current
vice-chairperson of the NITI Aayog. Dr. Arvind Pangariya
served as the first vice-chairman of the NITI Aayog, from
January, 2015 to August, 2017.

7. When was the Planning Commission established?
(a) 10th March, 1950 (b) 15th March, 1950
(c) 16th March, 1951 (d) 20th March, 1950
47th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2005
Ans. (b)
The Planning Commission was a non-Constitutional
institution. It is not mentioned in the Constitution. The
Commission was formed by a resolution of the Cabinet on
March 15, 1950. Prime Minister used to be the ex-officio
Chairman of this Commission. Its main function was to
prepare a 5-year plan for the Union and to give suggestions
on the annual plans of the States. Almost 70 percent of grants
to the States by the Centre were given on the recommendation
of the Commission. NITI Aayog has replaced the Planning
Commission. It is also an extra-Constitutional body. It was
formed by a resolution of the Union Cabinet on 1 January, 2015.

19. Which one of the following is not correct about NITI
Aayog?
(a) It was set up to replace the Planning Commission
(b) It has a full-time Chairman
(c) It was set up in January 2015
(d) It is based on the principle of Cooperative Federalism
U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2015
Ans. (b)
NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India
Aayog) is a Government of India policy think-tank, which
came into being on 1 January, 2015 to replace the Planning
Commission (Yojna Aayog). Like the Planning Commission,
NITI Aayog is an extra-Constitutional body. This Aayog
works on the principle of Co-operative federalism. Thus,
all the above statements are true except that it has a fulltime Chairman, because the Prime Minister is the ex-officio
Chairperson of NITI Aayog, which changes with the
completion of the term of his office.

the Union but the States also.

resources.
In context of the above statements, which one of the
following is correct?
Code :

28. The National Development Council (NDC) –
1. Discusses the progress of the National Plan.
2. Suggests the ways to achieve the goals of the National
Plan.
3. Gives guidelines to formulate the National Plan.
4. Suggests allocation of money in Planning.
Considering the above statements, select the correct
answer from the code given below :
Codes :
(a) 1, 2, and 3 (b) 2, 3 and 4
(c) 1, 2 and 4 (d) All of the above
U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2010
U.P. P.C.S. (Mains) 2005
Ans. (a)
The National Development Council (NDC) was set up on 6
August, 1952 by an executive order of the Government. Its
main functions are:
(a) To prescribe guidelines for the formulation of the
National Plan, including the assessment of resources for
the plan;
(b) To consider the National Plan as formulated by the
Planning Commission;
(c) To consider important questions of social and economic
policy affecting National development;
(d) To review the working of the Plan from time to time
and to recommend such measures that are necessary for
achieving the aims and targets set out in the National
Plan. The Planning Commission suggested ways for the
allocation of money to the National Plan, not the National
Development Council.

31. National Development Council is primarily related to –
(a) Approval of five-year plans
(b) Execution of village development plans
(c) Formation of development plans
(d) The financial relation between States and Union.
U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2002
Ans. (a)
To ensure the participation of States in the formation of
five-year plans, the National Development Council (NDC)
was set up on 6 August, 1952 by an executive order of the
Government. The Prime Minister is the Chairman of the
National Development Council and all the Chief Ministers
of the States are its members. The Secretary of the Planning
Commission (now, NITI Aayog) is also the Ex-officio
Secretary of the National Development Council. NDC
finally approves five-year plans. NDC acts as a coordinator
between the Planning Commission (now, NITI Aayog) and
State Governments.
