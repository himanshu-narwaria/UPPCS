## Provenance and study behavior

The former claims of 500 UPPSC questions and 120 repeated facts were not supported by verified papers. They are replaced by 40 editorial source-practice questions and 80 editorial priority facts, with zero verified UPPSC PYQs and zero verified repeat counts. No official exam keys or exam predictions are asserted. Both full and quick TOCs and the dashboard metadata agree on those counts.

The 72-hour plan maps all 238 content pages once across 54 distinct learning ranges, then adds ten revision sessions, four drills and four timed mocks. Each mock has ten questions; the four mocks partition the 40-question bank. Mocks use fresh independent answer sheets and reveal keys only after submission; learning-bank answers remain separate. Feedback links from the plan open the matching full-note anchor. Malformed/null saved answers are discarded safely.

- Chapter 1, PDF pp. 6: The ₹2,584.60 crore answer follows a question saying only 'this scheme', after an unrelated euthanasia item. The answer is preserved in the source evidence, but is not assigned to a scheme in the guide or tested.
- Chapter 1, PDF pp. 5, 145: The one-liner dates Surha Tal's 100th-site news to June 2026, while the map prints 19 January 2025. Keep both source dates visible; no current Ramsar-date verification is claimed.
- Chapter 2, PDF pp. 18, 47: The issue discusses a Modified UDAN launch on 4 July 2026 and Cabinet approval on 25 March 2026. These are kept as distinct milestones; the date is not silently replaced by one undifferentiated 'launch' date.
- Chapter 3, PDF pp. 86: The international rankings table has visibly overlapping rows and awkward publisher/date entries. Its text is retained as source evidence; the guide uses only clearly readable HDR/GII rows and does not certify every rank or publisher as current.
- Chapter 4, PDF pp. 91: The same page carries 7.7% provisional FY 2025-26 real GDP growth and 7.8% in a revised-estimates table under the new GDP series. Both are retained with their labels; no latest official estimate is asserted.
- Chapter 6, PDF pp. 144, 145: The prose says Sauj Lake and Sukhna were added on 15 October 2026, but the map labels Sauj 18 June 2026 and Sukhna 16 March 2026. The map also labels Surha Tal 19 January 2025 while p. 5 describes its 100th-site news in June 2026. These dates are preserved and not used as unqualified test keys.
- Chapter 7, PDF pp. 164, 165: The UP budget table's row labels and prose do not align cleanly: ₹1,77,744.12 crore appears next to 'Interest Payments' in the table, while prose describes about ₹1,77,744 crore as capital outlay. - Chapter 8, PDF pp. 169, 174: MSME incentive details retain the source's 'July 2026 draft' label. Bihar budget totals retain their including/excluding debt-repayment and gross-appropriation definitions; they are not treated as interchangeable totals.
- Chapter 9, PDF pp. 213: Some rows in the exercise table overlap in the PDF. The guide uses the clearly legible SIMBEX, VARUNA and SLINEX rows; consult the original page before learning other row-to-location matches.
- Chapter 10, PDF pp. 239: The Messi item reports an international-retirement announcement on 31 August 2026 and a last match in October 2026. Both source statements are retained; the guide does not turn them into a single independently verified retirement date.

## Verification and limits

`rebuild_all_current_affairs.py` rebuilds the three study pages and runs source-retention, metadata/count, chapter-range, omission-regression, security, anchor and dashboard-contract checks. `critic_verify.py` is a deterministic regression script; it is not the separately requested fresh critic review. `coverage_ledger.json` records the reference-level scope and cautions; `page_ledger.json` records all 241 pages and per-content-page preservation checks.

No browser was available for visual HTML or end-to-end interaction testing. JavaScript syntax and focused state/feedback behavior checks supplement the static HTML audits. Source PDF visual inspection is a sample, not certification of all figures, maps or tables. The guide follows the supplied 2026 (2) issue; it does not claim that the issue's later dates, office-holders, results, rankings or legal statements were independently verified against current primary sources.
