CHAPTER 20: Lodi Dynasty

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Lodi Dynasty was established by Bahlul Khan Lodi in 1451 AD.
* He sat on the throne of Delhi as ‘Bahlul Shah Ghazi’ and got Khutba proclaimed in his name.
* His most important victory was over Jaunpur. Bahlul Lodi did not treat Hindus with religious fanaticism. Many reputed Hindu Sardars were in his court; Rai Karan Singh, Rai Pratap Singh, Rai Nar Singh, Rai Trilokchandra and Rai Dadu. He introduced ‘Bahlul coin’ which was the main means ofexchange prior to Akbar.
* Bahlul Lodi appointed his third son, Nizam Khan, as his successor prior to his death, but some of the chiefs opposed him because his mother (Jaiband/Bibi Ambha) was the daughter of a goldsmith.
* Some powerful warlords joined Nizam Khan and he sat on the throne as Sultan Sikandarshah on 17 July, 1489 AD. He was the greatest ruler of the Lodi dynasty.
* Sikandar Lodi, in 1504 AD, established a new city called Agra along the Yamuna River to command eastern Rajasthan and routes towards Malwa/Gujarat and made Agra his capital. Sikandar Lodi was a very hard- working, generous but religiously orthodox.
* He worked for the improvement and advancement of agriculture and trade.
* The measurement scale used for land ‘Gaj-i-Sikandari’ was started at that time. He arranged free meals for the poor.
* He prohibited ‘Tazia’ in Muharram and banned Muslim women from visiting ‘Peers’and ‘Mazaars’.
* During his reign, a list was made ofqualified and skilled persons and was presented every six months before him.
* He was an educated and learned person.
* He ordered an Ayurvedic treatise to be translated into Persian and named it ‘Farhange Sikandari’.
* Lajjat-i-Sikandarshahi’, a masterpiece of singing, was composed during his reign.
* Sikandar Lodi was very fond of hearing Shehnai.
* He used to write poems under the nickname ‘Gulrukhi’.
* Sultan Sikandar abolished the octrai duty on grain.
* There was a battle between Maharana Sanga and Ibrahim Lodi at Khatauli in 1518 AD.
* Ibrahim Lodi was defeated in this battle.
* The first battle of Panipat took place between Babur and Ibrahim Lodi on 21 April, 1526 A.D.
* Ibrahim was defeated and killed on the battle- field.
* According to Nayamatullah (Niyamatullah), besides Sultan Ibrahim, no other Sultan of India was killed on the battlefield.

QUESTIONS
1. Who among the following Sultanate rulers was of Afghan origin?

(a) Khalji
(b) Tughluq
(c) Sayyid
(d) Lodi
U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
Ans. (d)
Lodi Dynasty (1451-1526 A.D.) was the last ruling family of the Delhi Sultanate of India. The dynasty was of Afghan Origin. Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat (1526 A.D.), which ended the Lodi dynasty.

2. Which one of the following is the correct chronological order of the Afghan rulers to the throne of Delhi?

(a) Sikandar Shah-Ibrahim Lodi-Bahlul Khan Lodi
(b) Sikandar Shah-Bahlul Khan Lodi or Lodi Ibrahim.
(c) Bahlul Khan Lodi or Lodi Sikandar Shah-Ibrahim Lodi.
(d) Bahlul Khan Lodi or Ibrahim Lodi or Lodi Sikandar Shah.
I.A.S. (Pre) 2006
Ans. (c)
Bahlul Lodi was the founder of Lodi dynasty. The correct chronological order of the Afghan rulers to the throne of Delhi is Bahlul Khan Lodi (1451-1489 A.D.), Sikandar Shah (1489-1517 A.D.), Ibrahim Lodi (1517-1526 A.D.). Hence, option (c) is the correct answer.

Sikandar Lodi is especially remembered for founding the city of Agra in 1504 A.D. with the purpose to command over eastern Rajasthan. His administration was noted for its efficiency. Roads were made safe and irrigation facilities were provided for the benefit ofthe peasantry. He had a knowledge ofPersian poetry. He used to write poems under the nickname 'Gulrukhi'. 10. Which one ofthe following Sultans is known for having abolished the tax on grains?

(a) Alauddin Khalji
(b) Ghiyasuddin Tughluq
(c) Firuz Tughluq
(d) Sikandar Lodi
U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Spl) (Pre) 2010
Ans. (d)
The rule of Lodi dynasty persisted till the coronation of Sikandar Lodi. He took keen interest in land revenue management. He ordered his nobles to present the details of their expenditure and cases of embezzlement were severely punished. He introduced “Gaj-i-Sikandari for measurement of land which was later used by Sher Shah Suri and Akbar. Several grain levies were abolished by him.

12. Who among these was not from the Slave dynasty?

(a) Balban
(b) Iltutmish
(c) Qutbuddin Aybak
(d) Ibrahim Lodi
Uttarakhand Lower Sub. (Pre) 2010
Ans. (d)
The rulers of Delhi Sultanate during (1206-90 AD) were famed as Slave dynasty. Qutbuddin Aibak (1206-10 AD), Iltutmish (1211-36 AD), and Balban (1266-86 AD) were the eminent ruler ofthis dynasty while Ibrahim Lodi was related to Lodi dynasty. Thus, option (d) is the correct answer.
