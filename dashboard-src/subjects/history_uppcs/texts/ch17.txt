CHAPTER 17: Delhi Sultanate: Slave Dynasty

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Incumbents ofthe Delhi Sultanate from 1206 AD to 1290 AD are popularly known as the sultans ofthe Slave dynasty, whose founder was Qutbuddin Aybak.
* They were all Turks, and their houses/lines were separate.
* So it is more appropriate to call them the slave dynasty or Mamluk Sultan ofDelhi, instead of calling these sultans as the sultans of the Slave dynasty.
* The first ruler of the Slave Dynasty in India was Qutbuddin Aybak (1206 - 1210 AD).
* He was from a Turk tribe called Aybak.
* In childhood, he was bought by the Qazi of Nishapur Fakhruddin Abdul Aziz Kufi as a slave.
* From childhood he used to read the Quran in a very melodious tone, which made him famous as the Quran Khwan (the one who recites the Quran).
* Later, he was brought from Nishapur to Ghazni where Ghori bought him.
* On the basis of his talent, passion and honesty, he gained the confidence of the Ghori.
* Ghori promoted him to the post of Amir-i-Akhur. His coronation was held in June 1206 AD, three months after the death of Ghori.
* Aybak’s capital was Lahore.
* Aybak never held the title of ‘Sultan’.
* He kept himself satisfied only with the titles of‘Malik’ and ‘Sipahasalaar’.
* After obtaining the liberation letter from Ghori’s successor Ghiyasuddin Mahmud, Aybak got rid of slavery in 1208 AD. and ruled as an independent sovereign.
* Due to his generosity, Qutbuddin Aybak used to donate so much money that he was called by the ‘Lakh Bakhsh’ (Giver of lakhs).
* Aibak had built mosques known as ‘Quwwat-ul-Islam’ in Delhi and ‘Adhai Din Ka Jhopra’ in Ajmer (in 1192 AD).
* He started the construction of‘Qutub Minar’ in Delhi, which was completed by Iltutmish.
* The fourth Storey was damaged by lightning which was rebuilt by Firuz Shah Tughluq, also spelt Feroz Shah Tughluq. He rebuilt the fourth floor of Qutb Minar as well as built the fifth floor.
* Sultan Qutbuddin Aibak died in 1210 AD when he fell from a horse while playing Chaugan (a game like modern polo).
* He was buried in Lahore.
* Iltutmish received the approval as Sultan not from any Ghor ruler, but from the Khalifa.
* Khalifa recognised the rule of Iltutmish to all those areas which he won and gave him the title of ‘Sultan-e-Azam’.
* Iltutmish was called ‘Slave of a slave’ [Aybak (who himselfwas a slave), whose slave was Iltutmish].
* Iltutmish was a Turk of‘Ilbari’tribe.
* Before becoming Sultan, he was the governor of Budaun.
* He was with Muhammad Ghori and Aybak during the campaign to suppress the Khokhar tribe rebellion in 1205 - 1206 AD.
* In the war, Ghori was so influenced by the courage and skill of Iltutmish that he freed him from slavery.
* Mongol invader Genghis Khan loitered near the Indus river for 3 months but did not cross into India during the reign of Iltutmish. Chasing the ruler of Khwarazmshah Jalaluddin Mengubirni, Genghis Khan reached the bank of Indus river. Jalal-ud-din-Mengubirni sought refuge from Iltutmish. Iltutmish dared not to risk the invasion ofa dangerous and furious invader like Genghis. So, he prudently refused to give asylum to the Khwarizm. Jalal-ud-din remained for some time in the north-west; this led to a series of Mongol attacks like seige of Multan around 1224. His departure (~ 1223-24) did not instantly end frontier troubles.
* Iltutmish defeated Sultan Ghiyas-ud-din Iwaz or Hisamuddin Awaz in mountains of Rajmahal near Teliyagarhi and annexed Bihar Sharifand Barh. Awaz had accepted the subordination to Iltutmish. Iltutmish appointed Malik-Jani as the new Subedar of Bihar.
* Iltutmish was the first to use the pure Arabic coins during the Sultanate period in India. He introduced two important coins,
* i.e. silver (Tanka) and copper (Jital) during the Sultanate period and started the tradition of inscribing the name of the Tankshal on the coins.
* Iltutmish received a certificate of ‘Khilat’ from Caliph of Baghdad in 1229 AD, which made Iltutmish a legitimate Sultan and the Delhi Sultanate an independent state.
* It is known from the Ibn Battuta’s description that Iltutmish had set up two statues of Lions of marble in front of his palace, which had two bells hanging around their neck which anyone could ring and seek justice from the Sultan.
* Iltutmish appointed the officials Qazi and Amir-i-dad in all the cities.
* According to Dr. R. P. Tripathi, the history of Muslim sovereignty in India begins with Iltutmish.
* According to Dr. Ishwari Prasad, Iltutmish was, undoubtedly, the founder of Slave Dynasty.
* Raziyya Sultan (1236-40 AD) was the first female ruler of Delhi Sultanate.
* As an individual, she violated the prevailing gender norms ofIslam for the first time in India, and politically, she emphasized the power to be in the hands of the Sultan instead ofdividing it among the Chiefs (Sardars) or the Iqtadars and thus, she tried to exercise real authority which provoked powerful Turkish slave-nobles (Chahalgani).
* Chahalgani (40 nobles) played an important role in overthrowing Raziyya Begum.
* Nobles under the leadership ofGovernor ofBhatinda, Malik Altunia, rebelled against Raziyya and overthrew her.
* The full name of Sultan Balban was Ghiyasuddin Balban.
* Balban ruled the Sultanate from 1266 to 1287 AD as Sultan.
* He was also known as Ulugh Khan.
* Like Iltutmish, he was also an Ilbari Turk.
* He laid the foundation of a new dynasty, the Balban Dynasty.
* Balban was caught by Mongols in his childhood, who sold him at Ghajini to Khwaja Jamaluddin, a resident of Basra.
* He was brought to Delhi in 1232 AD, where Iltutmish bought him in 1233 AD after Gwalior's victory.
* Impressed by his abilities, Iltutmish gave him the title of Khasdaar.
* During the reign of Raziyya, he earned the rank of Amir-i-Shikar.
* In the conspiracy against Raziyya, he was with the Turkish warlords, consequently Bahramshah became the Sultan, and Balban got the title of ‘Amir-i-Akhur’.
* Due to the grace of Badruddin Rumi, he got the Jagir of Rewari. Alauddin Masudshah became the Sultan with help of Turkish Amirs in which Balban played an important role, consequently he got the fiefofHansi.
* Balban married his daughter to Sultan Nasiruddin in 1249 AD. On this occasion, he was given the title of ‘Ulugh Khan’ and the post of ‘Nayab-i-mamlikat’.
* Balban sat on Delhi’s throne in 1266 AD.
* It is said about Balban that he adopted a policy of ‘blood and iron’.
* There were two main characteristics of the rule of Balban - First the divine right of Sultan and second the Sultan had to be autocratic.
* Balban had created his political ideology inspired by the popular heroes of Persia. The King was considered the God’s representative on earth - ‘Niyabat i-Khudai’.
* Balban projected kingship as divnely sanctioned.
* ‘Zille Allah’ or 'Zil-i-Ilahi' means Shadow of God.
* He was the first Sultan ofDelhi, who established the theory ofthe state/ kingship.
* He told his son Bughra Khan - “The post ofSultan is a living symbol ofautocracy.”
* Balban started many traditions of the Iranian emperors in his court. He started the rituals of ‘Sijada’ (saluting the Sultan in a prostrate manner) and ‘Paibos’ (kissing the feet ofSultan). In his court, he started the practice of celebrating the Persian festival ‘Nawruz’ with greatjoy every year.
* Balban established a military department ‘Diwan-i-Arz’ to look after the military organisation ofthe army.
* Balban made Imad ul-Mulk his Military minister (Arz-i-Mamalikk), who was a very honest and hardworking person.
* He kept him free from the financial control ofthe Wazir so that he did not lack wealth.

QUESTIONS
1. Who was the founder of Slave dynasty?

(a) Iltutmish
(b) Alauddin Khalji
(c) Balban
(d) Qutbuddin Aybak
U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 1990
Ans. (d)
Slave dynasty was the first of five unrelated dynasties to rule India during Sultanate period from 1206 A.D. to 1290 A.D. Qutbuddin Aibak ascended the throne of Delhi Sultanate in 1206 AD and with him started a new line of successive kings in Delhi Sultanate. He was the founder of slave dynasty. Qutbuddin Aibak and all his successors were slaves to their respective predecessors.

2. Who was the first ruler of the Slave Dynasty?

(a) Iltutmish
(b) Qutbuddin Aybak
(c) Raziyya
(d) Balban
53rd to 55th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2011; 47th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2005
Ans. (b)
The first ruler of the Slave Dynasty was Qutbuddin Aybak who ruled from 1206 A.D. to 1210 A.D. He established his capital at Lahore. Qutbuddin Aibak was born in Turkistan. Though he was ugly in appearance, he was intelligent and impressive in behaviour. He was taken as a prisoner and sold to the Qazi ofNishapur named Fakhruddin as a slave in his childhood. He was taken care and provided with proper education and military training by the kind-hearted Qazi but soon after the death of the Qazi, his sons sold Aybak to Muhammad Ghori. He was well-versed in Islamic theology, horse riding and swordsmanship. Owing to his skill and qualities, he soon attracted the attention of his master and was appointed commander of a troop. Shortly after battles; he was promoted to the post of Amir-i- Akhur, the master of the royal stable. When Ghori invaded India, Aybak came with his master and provided him with active support during battles. The successes of his master by and large depended on his military skill. Ghori was immensely pleased with him, and he appointed him his governor of the Indian empire. In 1206 A.D., on his way to Ghazni, Ghori died. His empire was inherited by his slaves, as he had no son to succeed him. Aybak, who was a viceroy of his Indian Empire was invited by the Amirs of Lahore to assume the powers. He bestowed the title of Malik and Sipahasalaar on himself. Muhammad Ghori had no male successor to inherit his empire; hence his nephew, Ghiyasuddin Mahmud ascended the throne ofGhori after his death in 1206 A.D.
