CHAPTER 65: Trade Union and Communist Party

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Madras Labour Union was formed in 1918 A.D. under the leadership of B.P. Wadia and V. Kalyanasundaram and it became the First modern labour Union. Buckingham and Carnatic Clothe Mills labourers were part of this union. Soon, two Labour Unions in Bombay, one at Calcutta (The Indian Seamen's Union) and four Labour Unions at Madras were established.
* The Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association was established on February 25, 1920, by Anasuya Sarabhai (she became president of the association) and Mahatma Gandhi. It emerged following the 1918 Ahmedabad mill strike.
* All India Trade Union Congress was founded by N.M. Joshi on 31st October, 1920 A.D. at Bombay.
* Lala Lajpat Rai was the first President ofAll India Trade Union Congress and Joseph Baptista was the Vice President. Dewan Chaman Lal was its General Secretary (they all were among the founding members) The motive behind its formation was the establishment of International Labour Organization in 1919 A.D. So Indian labourers decided to organize themselves as labour unions. This organization was divided for the first time in 1929 A.D. at Nagpur, Jawaharlal Nehru was president at that time.
* Communist International was founded in March 1919 A.D. by Vladimir Lenin and Bolshevik Party of Russia.
* M.N. Roy visited Russia on the invitation of Lenin and joined communist International becoming the first Indian to do so. To stop the growing power and influence of Communist activities, some communist members were arrested on the grounds of a conspiracy held in Kanpur. This case is popularly known as Kanpur Conspiracy.
* British government in 1924 A.D. started cases against M.N. Roy, Shripad Amrit Dange, Ghulam Hussain, Ramcharan Lal Sharma, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shaukat Usmani, Singaravelu Chettiar, Nalini Gupta, Shamuddin Hassan, M.R.S. Velayndhun, Dr. Manilal, Sampurnanand and Satyabhakta in Kanpur conspiracy. The government charged them with the conspiracy ofestablishing a revolutionary organisation in India.
* Revolutionary period of trade union is believed to be 1926- 1939 A.D.. During this M.N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shripad A. Dange, Shauqat Usmani etc. were the main leader of the Trade Union Movement.
* In 1940 AD, M.N. Roy established Radical Democratic Party.
* Saumendra nath Tagore in 1934 established Revolutionary Communist Party of India.

13. Kanpur conspiracy case was against the leaders of:

(a) Khilafat Movement
(b) Non-cooperation Movement
(c) Communist Movement
(d) Revolutionary Movement
U.P. P.S.C. (GIC) 2010; U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2001
Ans. (c)
Kanpur Conspiracy Case was against the Communists who were abhorred by the British Government. Some newly turned communists named M. N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shripad Amrit Dange, Shauqat Usmani, Ramcharan Lal Sharma, Singaravelu Chettiar, Ghulam Hussain, Nalini Gupta, Shamuddin Hassan, MRS Velayndhun, Dr. Manilal, Sampurnanand and Satyabhakt were caught by the Government and were tried for conspiring against the Government in 1924. The charge against them was “to deprive the Emperor of his sovereignty over British India, by complete separation of India from imperialistic Britain through a violent revolution.” The Meerut Conspiracy and Peshawar conspiracy cases were also against the leaders of Communist Movement.

18. Consider the following statements regarding the Workers and Peasants Party:
1. The Workers and Peasants Party was formed in 1927 and was given the form of an all India organization.
2. The aim ofthis party was to work within the Congress so that it could be made a more revolution inclined party and an organisation of the common people. Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Only 1
(d) Only 2
U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
Ans. (d)
Labour Swaraj Party was formed in Bengal in 1925 A.D. under congress, which was transformed into Workers and Peasants Party of Bengal in 1926 A.D. Thereafter, in 1927 A.D. Workers and Peasants Party of Bombay Province was formed from Congress Labour Party which emerged within Congress in Bombay Province in 1926 AD. After this, Workers and Peasants Party was formed in United Province in 1928 A.D. and in Punjab by name of Kirti Kisan Party. In the conference of these Workers and Peasant parties of different provinces held in Calcutta in December, 1928 A.D., 'All India Workers and Peasant Party' was formed as pan India organisation. Hence statement 1 is incorrect.
The purpose of this party was to work within the Congress to make it a more revolutionary party and to organize the general public. Thus, the second statement is correct. Hence, the correct answer is (d).
