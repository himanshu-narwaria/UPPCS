CHAPTER 76: Communal Award and Poona Pact (1932)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* A consensus among several parties for the separate electorate for Dalits was not reached in Second Round Table Conference. The His Majesty Government's declared position (endorsed by Parliament) that ifcommunities failed to agree the government would devise and apply a scheme. As a result British P.M. Ramsay MacDonald find a solution (although Europeans, Anglo-Indians, Indian Christians got separate electorates in 1919).
* On 16th August 1932 A.D. Ramsay MacDonald announced Communal Award. In this award, each minority group was given some reserved seats in Legislative Assemblies and elections to those seats were to be held by a separate electorate. Members ofthe depressed classes qualified to vote, will vote in a general constituency and they one also entitled to vote in a general constituency.
* Earlier Muslims and Sikhs were considered minorities but with this award the Dalit section of Hindu constituencies also got separated from it and now considered as a minority. Now Muslims, Indian Christians, Sikhs, Anglo-Indians and others including depressed classes were given separate electorates which were available in elections at Provincial Legislatures only.
* Gandhiji started his first fast unto death on 20th September, 1932 A.D. from Yerwada jail against the Communal Award which ended with the Poona Pact between B.R. Ambedkar and Gandhiji. Gandhiji didn’t sign this Pact. On 24th September, 1932 A.D. Poona Pact was signed between Dr. Ambedkar from Dalit section and Madan Mohan Malaviya from Hindu community.
* M.R. Jayakar, Devdas Gandhi, P. Baloo, Gavai, Thakkar, Solanki, T.B. Sapru, G.D. Birla, Rajagopalachari, Dr. Rajendra Prasad, Rao Bahadur Srinivas, M.C. Rajah, C.V. Mehta, Bakhale and Kamat signed this Pact too.
* 71 seats were reserved for Depressed classes in Communal Award which was increased to 148 by Poona Pact (Madras-30, Sindh with Bombay - 15, Punjab - 8, Bihar and Orissa - 18, Central Provinces - 20, Assam - 7, Bengal -30 and United Provinces 20). (This number is however 147 in some books). Also, 18% ofGeneral seats were reserved for the Dalits in central legislature. After the Poona Pact, Gandhiji didn't show more interest in the Civil Disobedience Movement and shifted his focus towards the fight against untouchability. He founded All India Anti-Untouchability League which was later renamed ‘Harijan Sevak Sangh’. Ghanshyam Das Birla was its first President.
All India Depressed Classes League was founded by Babu Jagjivan Ram. All India Scheduled Castes Federation was founded by B.R. Ambedkar in 1942 A.D., while All India Depressed Classes Association was founded in 1926 A.D. by M.C. Rajah. On 14th August, 1931 A.D., B.R. Ambedkar in a conversation with Gandhiji said that "History tells that Mahatmas, like fleeting phantoms, raise dust, but raise no level".

1. Who among the following issued the ‘Communal Award’?

(a) Ramsay Macdonald
(b) Stanley Baldwin
(c) Neville Chamberlain
(d) Winston Churchill
U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1999
Ans. (a)
No consensus was made on the subject of separate electorate for Depressed Classes and various communities in Second Round Table Conference. The His Majesty Government's declared position (endorsed by Parliament) that if communities failed to agree the government would devise and apply a scheme. As a result British PM Ramsay MacDonald found a solution. Accordingly, Ramsay Macdonald declared his “Communal Award” on 16 Aug. 1932 A.D.

Ans. (d)
British Prime Minister Ramsay MacDonald announced Communal Award on 16 August 1932 A.D. According to Legislative Assembly provisions, few seats of Legislative Assembly were reserved for minorities whose candidates were to be elected by separate electorates members of the depressed classes qualified to vote, will vote in a general constituency and they are also entitled to vote in a general constituency. Muslims and Sikhs were already considered minorities (although Europeans, Anglo-Indians, Indian Christians were provided with separate electorates in 1919). Now the Depressed Classes (untouchables) were to be declared minorities as per provisions of the new law.

11. Assertion (A): The Poona Pact defeated the purpose of Communal Award.
Reason (R): It paved the way for reservation of
seats in the Parliament and the State Assemblies for the SC and ST people. Select the correct answer from the code given below:
Code:

(c) (A) is true, but (R) is false.

Ans. (a)
On 16 August, 1932 A.D., Ramsay Macdonald announced his Communal Award. The award authorised Indian Christians, Muslims, Europeans, Sikhs, Depressed Classes and others to have separate electorates. Gandhi began fast unto death on 20th September, 1932 A.D. to protest against this decision. Finally, on 24 September, 1932 A.D. an agreement was made in Poona known as ‘Poona Pact’ in which joint electorate was accepted on the basis of two provisions. The two provisions were – Firstly, there were 148 seats reserved for depressed classes in the provincial legislature while only 71 seats were reserved according to communal award.
In Central Legislature 18 percent of the seats allotted to the general electorate were reserved for depressed classes. So reason (R) explains assertion (A) correctly, and both statements are true. So, the option (a) is correct answer.
