CHAPTER 70: Simon Commission (1927)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* The Government of India Act, 1919 had a provision that a commission would be appointed after ten years to study the progress of the government scheme and suggest new steps.
* An all White seven-membered Indian Statutory Commission popularly known as the Simon Commission was set up by the British Government under Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin in November, 1927 A.D. The Commission was to recommend to the British Government whether India was ready for further constitutional reforms.
* This commission was chaired by Sir John Simon and had 7 Members. Since none ofits members was Indian, it was opposed and boycotted by the Indian National Congress and a section of the Muslim League, headed by Muhammad Ali Jinnah and this commission was termed as 'White commission' by Indians. The commission arrived in Bombay on 3rd February, 1928 A.D. Chairman of this commission Sir John Simon was a member of Liberal Party whereas Clement Attlee who was the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom during India’s Independence was also a member of this Commission from Labour Party.
* Indian National Congress in its December 1927 A.D. session held at Madras decided to oppose Simon Commission.
* On 3rd February 1928 A.D., Simon Commission arrived in India and the countrywide strikes followed. The places approached by Commission witnessed complete strikes and slogan “Simon ! Go Back” was used as a protest in several rallies.
* Lala Lajpat Rai who was leading a rally in the protest against Simon Commission was severely injured in a Lathi charge by Police and he succumbed to his injuries.
* Lala Lajpat Rai was a major political leader ofPunjab who led the freedom struggle against the British Rule. He is also known as ‘Punjab Kesari’.
* Nehru Report (1928 A.D.) which had a provision for Dominion Status to India was prepared as an answer to the Simon Commission. It was prepared by an all-party committee headed by Motilal Nehru in which Jawaharlal Nehru was the secretary. This Committee had ten members including two Muslim Members. In this report, Dominion Status for India was demanded instead of complete freedom.
* Constituencies at places reserved for minorities were suggested instead of separate electorates (joint/mixed electorates). This report also suggested the fundamental rights should be granted to Indians such as right of free expression of opinion; right to assemble peaceably and without arms; right to form associations or unions etc.
* In 1928 A.D., Subhas Chandra Bose and Jawaharlal Nehru founded Independence for India League to demand Complete Independence instead of Dominion Status.

Montagu-Chelmsford reform (Government of India act, 1919) provided for a Constitutional Commission to review its working and operations. The Government of India Act, 1919 was passed on the basis of recommendations of Lord Chelmsford and Samuel Montagu to introduce self-governing institutions gradually to India. 13. Lala Lajpat Rai was injured-

(a) In lathi charge in protest to Simon Commission

In 1927, the British Government in England decided to send a commission headed by Sir John Simon to decide India’s political future. The commission had no Indian representative. This decision created an outrage in India. Indian National Congress in its Madras session of December, 1927 A.D. decided to boycott the Commission. When the Commission arrived, it met with demonstrations with banners saying “Simon !
