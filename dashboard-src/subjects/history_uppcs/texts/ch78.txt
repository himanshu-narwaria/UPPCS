CHAPTER 78: Provincial Election and Formation of Cabinet (1937)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* On the basis of Government of India Act, 1935, in February 1937, election results were announced of provincial legislatures in which Congress by defeating its opponents secured majority in five provinces.
1. Madras (159 seats out of total 215 seats) - Congress
2. Bihar (98 seats out of total 152 seats) - Congress
3. Central Provinces and Berar (70 out of 112) - Congress
4. United Provinces (134 seats out of 228) - Congress
5. Orissa (36 out of 60 seats) - Congress
* Congress emerged as the single largest party in Bombay (86 seats out of 175), Assam and North-West Frontier Province. Only in Bengal, Punjab and Sindh did Congress not secure enough support from masses to form the government on its own. The cabinets formed by Congress after 1936-37 A.D. elections only lasted for 28 months. In July 1937 A.D., Congress formed cabinets in 6 provinces - Madras, Bombay, Central Province, Orissa, Bihar, and United Province and with the support of other parties Congress formed a coalition government in Assam (a coalition government was formed by Muhammad Saadulla in 1937, but later resigned and Congress government led by Gopinath Bordoloi took office in September 1938) and North-West Frontier Provinces. Based on 1937 results, Sir Sahibzada Abdul Qayyum Khan, formed a coalition government although Indian National Congress was the single largest party, the Congress formed the government there by moving no-confidence motion against Qayyum Khan. The inclusion of Indians into the Second World War without consultation led to the resignation of all Congress cabinets. On 22nd October, 1939 A.D. Congress cabinet ofall provinces resigned as an act of protest. Congress coalition government in Assam under Gopinath Bordoloi was resigned from the government in November, 1939 and the NWFP government was also resigned in November, 1939. On the resignation ofthe Congress cabinets, Jinnah called Indian Muslims to celebrate 22nd December, 1939 A.D. as a Day of Deliverance.
* Working Committee ofIndian National Congress decided for abolition of feudal intermediaries/peasants proprietorship in 1937 A.D. as a result ofwhich Congress governments in U.P. and Bihar passed tenancy bills. Congress secured 134 seats out of 228 seats in United Provinces and formed government on its own.
* Govind Ballabh Pant was the Prime Minister (Premier) while Kailash Nath Katju was Law and Justice Minister and Revenue/Home/ Jail Ministry was given to Rafi Ahmed Kidwai.
* In Congress ruled states the reports related to Muslims grievances were in the following chronological order.
- Pirpur Report - 1938
- Sharif Report - March, 1939
- Fazlul Haq Report (Muslims Sufferings Under Congress Rule Report) - December, 1939

13. The elected Government of Bihar formed by Shri Krishna Singh resigned in February 1938 for which reason?

(a) Satyagraha against the British at the call of Gandhiji
(b) Participation in the Freedom Struggle of India
(c) For the release of political prisoners
(d) More than one of the above
(e) None of the above
68th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2022
Ans. (c)
In 1937, when the Congress came to power in Bihar, Shri Krishna Singh became the province's Premier. He formed his cabinet in Patna on 20 July 1937 A.D. under Government of IndiaAct 1935. However his ministry resigned in 1938AD to put pressure on the Governor for the release of political prisoners. Governor later conceded to the demands and ministry resumed offices but in October 1939 A.D., his ministry again resigned on the issue of India's participation in World War-II.
