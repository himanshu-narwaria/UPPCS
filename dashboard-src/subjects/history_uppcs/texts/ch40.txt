CHAPTER 40: Disintegration of the Mughal Empire

EXPLANATION SECTION
* After the death of Aurangzeb in 1707 A.D. his 63-year-old son Muazzam (Shah Alam) ascended the throne adopting the name of ‘Bahadur Shah I’. He ruled during the period from 1707-1712 A.D.
* He was given the title of ‘Shah-e-Bekhabar’ by Khafi Khan.
* Jahandar Shah was the first emperor in the Mughal dynasty, who proved to be weak and highly disqualified for governance. He ascended the throne with the help of Zulfiqar Khan. He was defeated and later executed by his nephew Farrukh Siyar in a battle in 1713 A.D.
* Farrukh Siyar issued a decree (farman) in which he granted duty - free trading concessions to the British in Bengal in 1717 A.D. It was mentioned in the contract that the East India Company could trade without duty (charge) after paying the annual tax of Rs. 3000
* Muhammad Shah (1719 - 1748 A.D.) was the last Mughal emperor to sit on Peacock throne (built by Shah Jahan). During his reign, Nadir Shah attacked India in 1739 A.D. and defeated Mughals in the battle of Karnal. The contemporary historian Anand Ram Mukhlis wrote, "Nadir Shah took sixty thousand rupees, many thousands of gold coins, gold of one crore rupees, jewels of fifty crores, Kohinoor and peacock throne with him to Iran." and remarked that "the accumulated wealth of 348 years changed owners in a moment."
* Muhammad Shah (1719-1748 A.D.) spent most of his time in watching animal fight. People called him Rangeela due to his obsession towards wine and beauty and apathy towards administration. A group of eunuchs and ladies dominated in Mughal Darbar during the regime of Muhammad Shah.
* The tenure of Shah Alam-II was from 1759 to 1806 A.D. His real name was Ali Gauhar. English captured Delhi in 1803 A.D. during his tenure.
* The last Mughal emperor was Bahadur Shah Zafar / Bahadur Shah II (reigned 1837 -1857 A.D.). His father’s name was Akbar -II (1806 -1837 A.D.). He was called as the "Emperor without Empire".
* Ibrahim Zauq and Asad Ullah Khan Ghalib taught poetry to Bahadur Shah Zafar. Hasan Askari was sufi saint and Pir to Zafar.
* Bahadur Shah Zafar got Rs 1 lakh per month from the East India Company as pension, which consisted of Rs. 99000 given in Delhi and one thousand rupees for regular family expenses in Lucknow. He died in 1862 A.D.
* Chin Qilich Khan aka Nizam-ul-Mulk founded the independent Kingdom of Hyderabad in 1724. Nizam became the real king after the death of the Mughal governor Mubariz Khan in the battle of Sakhar - Kheda of Deccan, 1724 A.D..
* Sawai Jai Singh (Jai Singh II) (1688 - 1743 A.D.) ofAmber was a Rajput king ofthe 18th century.
* He established ‘Jaipur’ city and made it the centre of science and art.
* He built well equipped observatories in Delhi, Jaipur, Ujjain, Mathura and Varanasi. He patronised the translation of‘‘Euclid's Geometry” in Sanskrit.
* He organized two Ashvamedh sacrifices during his reign.

After that puppet king Rafi-ud-darjat, Rafi-ud-daula and Muhammad Shah sat on throne with their help. They lost power during Muhammad Shah's regin.

(b) 1737 A.D.
(c) 1738 A.D.
(d) 1739 A.D.
M.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
