CHAPTER 71: Lahore Session of Congress, Proposal of Complete Independence (1929)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* At Ahmedabad Session (1921 A.D.) of Congress, Maulana Hasrat Mohani defined Swaraj as complete independence from all type of foreign control, demanding Congress to adopt complete independence as its goal.
* C.R. Das was elected as the President of Congress's Ahmedabad session of 1921 A.D. but the session was presided over by Hakim Ajmal Khan (served as acting President) as C.R. Das was in jail.
* At Calcutta Session of Congress (1928 A.D.), Congress gave ultimatum to British Government either to accept the recommendations of Nehru Report or face a mass movement.
* Inability of the government to take any action on the Nehru Report compelled the Congress to announce ‘Purna Swaraj’ as its goal in the historic Lahore Session, 1929 A.D. headed by Jawahar Lal Nehru.
* On 31st December, 1929 A.D. with the stroke of midnight Congress President Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru hoisted the Indian flag of Independence at the bank of Ravi River. Congress working committee at the sitting of 2nd January, 1930 decided to celebrate 26th January, 1930 A.D. as ‘Purna Swaraj Day (Complete Independence day)’ and to celebrate 26th January, every year as ‘Purna Swadhinta Divas’. In this Session during his presidential address, Nehru said, ‘we have but one goal today that ofindependence'. Congress on 31st December, 1929 A.D. at Lahore Session announced resolutions which included:
(1) There is no benefit from Round Table Conference.
(2) Renunciation of plan of Dominion Status in Nehru Report. (3) Word Swaraj means Complete Independence.
(4) All India Congress Committee (AICC) will start Civil Disobedience Movement when deemed necessary.

15. The Lahore Session of the Indian National Congress (1929) is very important in history, because –
1. The Congress passed a resolution demanding complete independence.
2. The rift between the extremists and moderates was resolved in that Session.
3. A resolution was passed rejecting the two-nation theory in that session.
Which of the statement (s) given above is/are correct?
(a) Only 1
(b) 2 and 3
(c) 1 and 3
(d) None of these
I.A.S. (Pre) 2012
Ans. (a)
The historic Lahore session of Indian National Congress in the chairmanship ofJawaharlal Nehru was held in December, 1929 A.D. and the proposal to India’s complete independence (Purna Swaraj) had been declared by him for the first time. The rift between extremists and moderates was resolved in 1916 A.D. Lucknow session of INC. Hence statement (2) is incorrect. The two-nation theory was never opposed during Lahore Congress Session, though it was opposed by the Congress as a political party. Hence, statement (3) is also incorrect. Therefore, option (a) is the correct answer.

21. Which of the following was not included in the resolutions of Lahore Session of Congress in 1929?

(a) Declaration of India’s foreign policy
(b) Declaration of complete independence
(c) Preparing to launch civil disobedience movement
(d) Abolition of untouchability
U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Spl) (Mains) 2010
Ans. (d)
The resolutions proposed by Indian National Congress on December 31, 1929 AD consisted of following points- (1) There is no benefit from Round Table Conference. (2) The plan ofDominion status ofIndia has been renunciated. (3) The word ‘Swaraj’ means Complete Independence. (4) All India Congress whenever it considers appropriate can start Civil-Disobedience Movement.
Abolition of Untouchability was not mentioned in the proposal. The Round Table Conference can be considered as India’s foreign policy as the British Government only had the right to make a foreign policy of India. It was primarily passed in Congress session of 1921 A.D. that there must be a foreign policy for India.
