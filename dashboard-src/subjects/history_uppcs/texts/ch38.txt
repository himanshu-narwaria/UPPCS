CHAPTER 38: Sikh Sect

EXPLANATION SECTION
* The founder of Sikhism and the first Guru of Sikhs was Guru Nanak. There are total of 10 gurus in Sikhism. They are Guru Nanak, Guru Angad, Amar Das, Ramdas, Arjan Dev, Hargobind, Har Rai, Har Krishan, Tegh Bahadur and Guru Gobind Singh.
* Guru Angad is considered as the progenitor of‘Gurumukhi script’.
* Gazetteer ofAmritsar district, mentions that the fourth Guru Ramdas obtained from Akbar the grant of a piece of land (around 500 bighas) on the spot where now stands the city of Amritsar, and dug the holy tank, and commenced the erection of a temple in its midst. Some sources also state that it was gifted to Bibi Bhani (wife of Guru Ramdas) by Akbar. Ramdas died in 1581 A.D. and his son and successor, Guru Arjun completed the temple. Some other sources mention that Ramdas purchased land from the zamindars of the villages.
* Guru Amardas divided the religious empire into 22 parts called Manji. Each Manji was under the authority of a Sikh.
* Guru Arjan Dev constructed ‘Sri Harmandir Sahib’ in the middle of the Amritsar pond. He established two cities called Tarn Taran and Kartarpur (in Jalandhar Doab). Guru Ram Das organised a network ofSikhs to collect offerings, called Masand. Dasvandh is another practise being following by Sikhs, according to which the Sikhs had to pay one-tenth of their income to Guru.
* Adi Granth or Guru Granth Sahib was compiled in 1604 A.D. by Arjan Dev, the fifth Sikh guru (1581A.D.- 1606A.D.). It contains preachings of six Sikh Gurus, many Hindu/muslim saints like Kabir, Baba Farid, Namdev and Raidas. Among the earliest compositions in the Adi Granth was written by Vaishnav poet Jaydev and the last compositions added were of Guru Teg Bahadur. Guru Arjan Dev was sentenced to death by Jahangir and Guru Teg Bahadur was sentenced to death by Aurangzeb.
* The tenth and the last Guru of Sikhs, Guru Gobind Singh, converted Sikhs into a military sect ‘Khalsa Panth’. It was established on 13th April, 1699 in ‘Anandpur Sahib’ on the day of Baisakhi. He called the whole Sikh community as ‘Khalsa’. He ordered Sikhs that each Sikh should have the surname of Singh (male) and Kaur (female) and hold 5 K's-Kesh, Kangha, Kachha, Kripan and Kada.
* The tenth and the last Sikh Guru Gobind Singh, was assassinated in Nanded, Maharashtra by an Afghan noble in 1708 A.D. Nanded Gurudwara (Huzur Sahib) is situated at his cremation site
* The another name of Banda Bahadur was Lachman Dev or Lakshan Dev. This name was given to him by Sikh Guru Gobind Singh.

U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2002
Ans. (a)
Guru Arjan Dev and Guru Tegh Bahadur were awarded death sentence by their contemporary rulers, Jahangir andAurangzeb respectively.
GuruArjan Dev (1581-1606A.D.)- He was the 5th Sikh Guru. After the revolt against Jahangir, his rebellious son Prince Khusrau fled from Agra to Afghanistan. During his escape Khusrau met Guru Arjan Dev at Tarn Taran city. Guru gave him support and shelter due to which Jahangir got angry with Guru Arjan Dev. Jahangir accused him of conspiracy and sentenced him to death sentence in 1606 AD. Guru Arjan Dev established Taran Taran and Kartarpur towns. His major work was a compilation of Guru Granth Sahib. Guru Tegh Bahadur (1664-1675 A.D.): Revered as the ninth Nanak, he was the ninth of ten Gurus of the Sikh religion. According to the records written by his son Guru Gobind Singh, the Guru had resisted persecution, adopted and promised to protect Kashmiri Hindus. The Guru was summoned to Delhi by Aurangzeb on the protest but when he arrived, he was offered “to abandon his faith and convert to Islam.” Guru Tegh Bahadur refused, he and his associates were arrested. He was executed on November, 1675 before the public in Chandni Chowk, Delhi.
