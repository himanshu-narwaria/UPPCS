CHAPTER 69: Formation of Swaraj Party (1923)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Central Legislature (Council of States and the Legislative Assembly) and Provincial Legislative Councils, which were established by the Government of India Act, 1919 were boycotted by the Congress on the directions of Gandhiji and they didn't contest the elections of 1920. An atmosphere of hopelessness was created with the withdrawal of Non Cooperation Movement and the arrest of Gandhiji.
* C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru formulated a new ideology. They proposed that Congress should fight elections to enter the councils be a part of Government and should enter the Legislative Assemblies and the struggle should be continued by being within the Government. But this plan was rejected by the majority of congressmen. C.R. Das (then President of Congress) resigned from the President's post and organised a party within the Congress in January, 1923 A.D. with Motilal Nehru and named it Swaraj Party. The mandate of this party was to become a part of Councils and hinder their working through agitation.
* C.R. Das became the President of the Party and Motilal Nehru became its General Secretary. S. Srinivasa Iyengar (founder of the Swaraj Party at Madras) and N.C. Kelkar were the other main leaders of Swaraj Party.
* The Election of Vithalbhai Patel as the Speaker or President of Legislative Assembly in 1925 A.D. is one of the major achievements of the Swaraj Party.
* Chittaranjan Das was also known as ‘Desh Bandhu’. The meaning of the term ‘Desh Bandhu’ was ‘Friend of the Country’. Chittaranjan Das studied law in London and became an advocate in India.
* One of his most important achievements was that he helped in the acquittal of Aurobindo Ghose from the Alipore Bomb Conspiracy Case. He said Swaraj should be for common people not only for some sections ofthe society. Madan Mohan Malviya was the founder member of Hindu Mahasabha.

Ans. (b)
Central Legislature (Council of States and the Legislative Assembly) and Provincial Legislative Councils, which were established by the Government of India Act, 1919 were boycotted by the Congress on the directions of Gandhiji and they didn't contest the elections of 1920. An atmosphere of hopelessness was created with the withdrawal of Non Cooperation Movement and the arrest of Gandhiji.
Suspension of the Non-Cooperation Movement led to a split within Congress in the Gaya session of the Congress in December, 1922 A.D. Leaders like Motilal Nehru and Chittaranjan Das formed a separate group within the Congress known as the Swaraj Party in January, 1923A.D. The Swarajists wanted to contest/fight the Council elections and wreck the Government from within. Elections to Legislative Councils were held in November, 1923A.D. The Swaraj Party marked its significant presence in the Legislative Council. It demanded the setting up ofresponsible Government in India with the necessary changes in the Government of India Act of 1919 A.D.

15. Who of the following is known as ‘Deshbandhu’?

(a) Chandra Shekhar
(b) Chitranjan Das
(c) A.O. Hume
(d) Annie Besant
U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2006
Ans. (b)
Chittaranjan Das, a revolutionary freedom fighter, was endearingly called ‘Deshabandhu’ (Friend of the Country). Das was a lawyer by profession and made a name for himself when he returned to India after finishing his studies abroad, took up a law practice and defended Sri Aurobindo Ghosh in a court suit filed against him. After the failure of the Non-Cooperation Movement, he became disillusioned and proposed a strategy to end dyarchy, but Congress did not accept it. He formed his own party Swaraj Party along with Motilal Nehru.

17. When Congress leaders condemned the Montagu Chelmsford Report, many moderates left the party to form the:

(a) Swarajya Party
(b) Indian Freedom Party
(c) Independence Federation of India
(d) Indian Liberal Federation
I.A.S. (Pre) 2003
Ans. (d)
At the end ofWorld War I, the British Government formulated a scheme of reforms which was known as the Montague Chelmsford Reforms. It was embodied in the Government of India Act, 1919. Surendranath Banerjee and other Liberal leaders of congress welcomed Montague-Chelmsford reforms. The Liberal leaders disassociated from congress and created All Indian Liberal Federation.
