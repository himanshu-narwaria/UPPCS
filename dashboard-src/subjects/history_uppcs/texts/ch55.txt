CHAPTER 55: Revolutionary Activities Outside India

EXPLANATION SECTION
* In February 1905 AD, Shyamji Krishna Verma established the Indian Home Rule Society (IHRS) at London. IHRS established nationalist student hosted, named as 'India House' (1905-10) to foster radical Indian nationalist thoughts. The aim of this society was to establish self-rule by promoting nationalistic ideas/propaganda among Indians in Britain. From here a monthly journal ‘The Indian Sociologist’ was started in Jan 1905.
* The Ghadar Party was founded on 21st April, 1913 (at Oregon) with its headquarters at San Francisco (USA) with an objective to free India from British rule. Indians residing in Canada and the USA founded this party.
* In 1913, Sohan Singh Bhakna founded a society named ‘Hindustan Association of the Pacific Coast’. This society published a newspaper named Ghadar, that is why this party became famous as Ghadar Party. Lala Hardayal was the torchbearer of this party.
* Barakatullah, Rash Behari Bose, Raja Mahendra Pratap, Abdul Rahman, Madam Bhikaji Cama, Bhai Parmanand, Kartar Singh Sarabha and Pt. Kashiram were prominent members of the party.
* Raja Mahendra Pratap with Barakatullah Khan founded the first provincial government ofIndia in Afghanistan (Kabul) during the first world war on December 1, 1915. Raja Mahendra Pratap himself became President and Baraktullah Khan was the Prime Minister.
* Madam Cama was born on 24th September, 1861. Her parents were Parsi. She served as the personal secretary of Dadabhai Naoroji in Indian Nationalist Movements. She was married to Rustomji who was an advocate and a social worker.
* Bhikaiji Rustom Cama was a supporter of Revolutionary Nationalism. She operated revolutionary activities from Europe and America. She participated in International Socialist Congress held at Stuttgart (Germany) where she was the first person to hoist Indian Flag on foreign land, which was designed jointly by V.D. Savarkar, Shyamji Krishna Verma and Bhikaiji Rustom. She is famous as the ‘Mother of the Indian Revolution’.
* Madanlal Dhingra and Udham Singh were hanged for the assassination of British officials in England. Madanlal Dhingra on 1st July, 1909 A.D., shot and killed Curzon Wyllie who was the political aide-de-camp to the Secretary of State for India and Cowasji Lalkaka during a meeting of National Indian Association. Udham Singh killed Michael O' Dwyer (Former Lieutenant Governor ofPunjab) in London in March 1940 who was responsible for endorsing and upholding the actions taken during Jallianwala Bagh Massacre. The Kamagata Maru was a Japanese steamliner chartered by an affluent Punjabi businessman, Gurdit Singh to bring Indian immigrants to Canada. The ship departed in 1914, taking 376 Indians for Canada’s Vancouver port, but the Canadian authorities refused their entry in Canada. A Shore Committee was constituted to protect the rights of Indian travellers under the leadership of Hussain Rahim, Balwant Singh, and Sohan Lal Pathak. Indian, Bhagwan Singh, Baraktullah, Ramchand and Sohan Singh also carried out a movement in favour of Indian emigrants. When Kamagatamaru reached Budge Budge port at Calcutta, a clash occurred between angered passengers and British Police. In firing by the Police, 20 of the passenger were killed, one drowned and 202 passengers were sent to Jail.
* Rash Bihari Bose was an Indian National living in Japan. He organized a conference in Tokyo on 28th-30th March 1942 A.D. to discuss political problems of India.
* This conference was given an organized form in June, 1942 A.D. and Subhas Chandra Bose was invited. Ras Bihari Bose founded Indian Independence league in this conference and unanimously elected as President of the league.
