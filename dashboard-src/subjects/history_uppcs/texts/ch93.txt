CHAPTER 93: Magazines, Books and Their Authors

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Annie Besant founded Central Hindu College in 1898 AD and was elected as the President (International) of the Theosophical Society in 1907 AD. She founded a weekly newspaper 'Commonweal' in January 1914 for her political work. In June 1914 she purchased the 'Madras Standard' and renamed it 'New India', which thereafter became her chosen organ for her tempestuous propaganda for India's freedom.
* Lieutenant Colonel James Tod in his book 'Annals and Antiquities of Rajasthan' wrote about the feudal system of Rajasthan.
* Shyamji Krishna Verma was an Indian revolutionary andjournalist who publishedjournal ‘The Indian Sociologist'.
* Amar Sonar Bangla was composed in 1905 during the partition of Bengal by Rabindranath Tagore. The melody (tune) of this song were inspired by Harkara's ‘Ami Kothay Pabo Tare’ song of Gagan Harkara. First ten lines of the song Amar Sonar Bangla were adopted as National Anthem of Bangladesh officially in 1972 AD. This song was translated into English by ‘Syed Ali Ahsan’. Rabindranath Tagore composed Jan-Gan-Mana too. He is the only poet credited with authoring the national anthems of two countries. The Bengali edition of Gitanjali was published on 14 August 1910 (31 Shravan 1317). Its English edition was published for the first time in November 1912 AD. Rabindranath Tagore won the 1913 Nobel Prize in literature; Gitanjali: Song Offerings was the english book chiefly associated with the award, and it was Tagore'sown English selection/recasting of the Bengali Gitanjali (version) alone (not direct translation of the book only). Deliberative book “Hind Swaraj” was firstly published in Gujarati in 1909 AD. British Government censored this book. In March 1910, the Bombay Government proscribed its circulation; Gandhi then published his own English rendering, Indian Home Rule, in reply. In this book, Gandhiji compared British Parliament to a sterile women and a prostitute and commented on working processes of the British Government. "Gokhale: My Political Guru, a book was first published in February, 1955 by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad. The book is an English compilation ofGandhi's earlier writings and speeches on Gokhale".
* Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar (1820-1891 AD) was a great social reformer and educationist of the 19th century. Primarily by his efforts the Hindu Widows Remarriage Act, 1856 was passed. He wrote ‘Bahuvivah’ and ‘Balya Vivaher Dosh’.
* ‘Das Kapital’ was written by famous German socialist Karl Marx. In this book he straight away criticized the capitalism and presented his Socialist principles which are also known as Marxism (like inquiry into the laws of motion of modern society). First part ofthe book was published during the lifetime of Marx whereas the second and third part of the book was edited and published by his friend and associate Friedrich Engels.
* ‘Anand Math’ of Bankimchandra Chatterjee is considered as the Bible of Bengali patriotism. This Book is based on the Sanyasi Revolt (1763-1800 AD).
* Kesari Singh Barhath was poet and freedom fighter ofRajasthan. In 1903, he wrote ‘Chetavani-Ra-Chungatya’ having 13 soratha/couplets.
* Maithili Sharan Gupta is a famous poet adorned with the title of‘National Poet’. He is the writer of'Bharat Bharti'. His other works include Panchavati, Saket, Yashodhara, Jayadrath Vadh etc.
* 'Ae Mere Watan ke logo' a patriotic song is written by Pradeep. Born in a small town Badnagar with in the Ujjain district, Madhya Pradesh the real name of Pradeep was Ram Chandra Narayanji Dwivedi. He wrote a condolence song on the martyrs of 1962 AD Indo-China war. The music of this song was composed by C. Ramchandra.
* Md. Iqbal was born on 9 November 1877 AD at Sialkot in Punjab of British India. The famous poet of Urdu and advocate by profession Iqbal was a nationalist from the beginning. He wrote - ‘Sare Jahan se Accha Hindostan Hamara’ but later on he joined Muslim League. ‘Majhab Nahi Sikhata apas me bair Rakhna’ is a line from the Song Sare Jahan se Accha by Iqbal.
* ‘Landmarks in Indian Constitutional and National Development’was written by writer Gurumukh Nihal Singh. He was the Governor of Rajasthan from 1956 to 1962 AD.
* G.A. Natesan was the editor ofthe book 'Congress Presidential Addresses' (1934-35 AD). This book was published in two parts. The first one contained the congress Presidential Addresses from 1885 to 1910, and the other one contained the same from 1911 AD to 1934 AD.
* Jawaharlal Nehru when he was jailed at Ahmednagar Fort (1942-1945 AD) wrote a book named The Discovery of India. Also, in his jail term, Nehru wrote an Autobiography (also known as Toward Freedom) and Glimpses of World History (in 1930s).
* Sachindranath Sanyal wrote ‘Bandi Jeevan’.
* ‘A Journey through the Kingdom of Oude in 1849-50’ report was written by W.H. Sleeman and published in two volumes in London.
* ‘JP Movement-Emergency and India' Second Freedom' was a book written by M.G. Devasahayam.
* Lieutenant General Brij Mohan Kaul wrote the book ‘The Untold Story’. This book was published in 1967 AD and throws light on India-China war of 1962 AD.
* Hans Christian Andersen in his writings wrote about fairy tales. His important works are:
The Steadfast Tin Soldier, The Snow Queen, The Little Mermaid, Thumbelina etc.
* 'The Golden Gate' published in 1986 AD is the first novel by Vikram Seth.
* Rachel Carson wrote ‘Silent Spring’. She was a famous American Biologist. In this book, questions are raised about the use ofpesticides.
* ‘The Satanic Verses’ is the fourth novel ofSalman Rushdie. Its first publication took place in 1988 AD. This book was in the news for some time regarding various controversies. Rushdie's first published novel 'Grimus', appeared in 1975.
* 'The Namesake' was the first novel of Jhumpa Lahiri. Jhumpa Lahiri is a Pulitzer Prize winner. She received this award for the “Interpreter of Maladies” which she published in 1999 AD.
* 'Manas ka Hans' is a prestigious novel by Amritlal Nagar. Generally, the story is written around the life of Tulsidas who wrote Ramcharitmanas.
* Sumitra Nandan Pant is one ofthe prominent poets ofmodern India. He was a Chhayavaadi poet. His famous works are Chidambara, Lokayatan, Veena, Yugvani, Pallav, Bharat Mata Gram Wasini and Kala aur Boodha Chand.
* J.K. Rowling written/created Harry Potter in which Cornelius Fudge is a Minister for magic.
* ‘Mrignayani’ is a novel written by Vrindavan Lal Verma. His main novels are - Jhansi Ki Rani, BhuvanVikram, Sangam, Lagan, Ahilyabai, etc.
* ‘Bikhre Moti’ is a story collection published by Subhadra Kumari Chauhan in 1932 AD. Besides this, she wrote 'Unmadini' and 'Seedhe-Sadhe Chitra'.
* Charles Dickens wrote the novel ‘David Copperfield’ published serially in 1849-50 AD (and in book form in 1850 A.D.). The novel is based on the life of Charles Dickens semi autobiographical.
* ‘The Proudest Day’ was written by Anthony Read and David Fisher. The story of the book is related to the freedom of India.
* ‘Kamayani’ was written by famous Hindi poet Jaishankar Prasad. In this epic, the episode of Manu and Shraddha is narrated. His famous works are - Aansu, Lahar, Ajatashatru, Titli and Kankal.
* Verrier Elwin studied Baiga Tribe and wrote a book named ‘The Baiga’. He suggested making national parks for the tribes.
* ‘Ignited Minds’ is written by former President of India Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam who is also known as the Missile Man of India. He also wrote 'Wings of Fire' (An autobiography: APJ Abdul Kalam with Arun Tiwari).
* ‘The Story of the Integration of the Indian States’ was written by V.P. Menon.
