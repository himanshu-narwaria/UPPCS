CHAPTER 24: Delhi Sultanate: Literature

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Abul Hassan Yaminuddin Khusrau, often called Amir Khusrau, was born in 1253 AD at Patiyali in present-day Kasganj district in Uttar Pradesh. Khusrau was called ‘Tuti- i - Hind’.
* Some ofhis famous compositions were Miftah-ul-Futuh, Khazain ul-Futuh (Tarikh-i-Alai), Ashika, Nuh Sipihr and Tughluq namah. He referred to himself as a 'Turkish-Indian'.
* Amir Khusrau himself said that he knew, Sanskrit and other Indian languages, which are a part ofHindavi.
* In India, Amir Khusrau was the originator ofthe early form of the musical genre known as Qawwali.
* New Persian poetry style, ‘Sabk - i - Hindi’ or Hindustani style, was started by Amir Khusrau.
* Musical instruments ‘Tabla’ and ‘Sitar’ were made prevalent in the 13th/14th century by Amir Khusrau.
* The development of Persian literature in India started with the advent of Muslims.
* After the establishment ofDelhi Sultanate, the Persian language was granted the status of official language.
* Tabakat-i-Nasiri is a treatise of Minhaj-us-Siraj (Minhaj i-Siraj) dedicated to Sultan Nasiruddin Mahmud.
* Ziauddin Barani composed the ‘Tarik -i-Firozshahi’and ‘Taj-ul-Masir’ was written by Sadruddin Muhammad Hassan Nizami.
* Yahiya Bin Ahmad Sarhindi wrote ‘Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi’, which is the principal source regarding the history of Sayyid rulers under the patronage of Sayyid Mubarakshah.
* Rana Kumbha was a patron of music, literature and art. He wrote texts on music, Sangeet Raja (also known as Sangita Mimansa).
* The Tughluq dynasty ruler Firuz Shah Tughluq wrote his memoir as Futuhat-i-Firuz Shahi.

43. Which statements is/are correct regarding the book 'Mirat-e-Sikandari'?
1. There is a description of the happiness and prosperity of the Kingdom of Sultan Mahmud Begada of Gujarat.
2. There is a description of proper arrangements for the security of trade routes.
Select the correct answer from the code given below:
Code -
(a) Only 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Only 1
(d) Both 1 and 2
U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Ans. (d)
'Mirat-e-Sikandari' describes the happiness and prosperity of the kingdom of Sultan Mahmud Begada of Gujarat. It mentions the businessmen benefitting from the safety ofroads during the reign of Mahmud Begada. Hence, it is clear that both statements are true.
