CHAPTER 54: Revolutionary Movements in India

EXPLANATION SECTION
* ‘Mitra Mela’ was an organization founded by Vinayak Damodar Savarkar in 1899 AD. It was later converted into a secret organization 'Abhinav Bharat Society' in 1904 A.D. Besides Maharashtra, its branches were opened in Madhya Pradesh and Karnataka.
* On 27th September, 1925 A.D., Rashtriya Swayam Sevak Sangha was founded by Keshav Baliram Hedgewar. Its headquarter is located in Nagpur, Maharashtra.
* Jatindranath Mukherjee (1879-1915) was a Bengali Revolutionary. He was a heartened follower of Vivekananda and Aurobindo Ghosh. He was an active member in Revolutionary Committees such as Yugantar, Anushilan Samiti, Gadar Party etc. He was also known as Bagha Jatin.
* Vyayam Mandal was founded by Chapekar Brothers - Damodar Hari Chapekar and Bal Krishna Hari Chapekar and Vasudeo Hari Chapekar in 1896-97 A.D. at Poona. It was founded with clear political intentions. In June 1897 A.D., Chapekar Brothers killed President of Poona plague committee Rand and Lieutenant Ayerst. Damodar Hari Chapekar including his other brothers was hanged in this crime. They were arrested on the information given by Dravid Brothers. Britishers charged/arrested Tilak for inciting murders through writings (sedition) but they got no evidence to prove that.
* In July 1897 A.D., Tilak was charged with sedition as per Section 124-A of IPC and was sentenced for 18 months. It made Tilak an All India leader and people gave him the title of Lokmanya.
* In December 1909 A.D., Anant Laxman Kanhere a member of Karve Group shot and killed District Collector ofNasik ‘Jackson’. Kanhere, Krishnaji Gopal Karve and Vinayak Deshpande were hanged in Jackson murder case.
* V.D. Savarkar was arrested in London and brought to India with some other people who were charged in Nasik conspiracy case and was sentenced to life imprisonment.
* The place of Barra Dacoity 1908 A.D. was located in East Bengal. The group which executed Barra Dacoity was led by Pulin Bihary Das.
* On April 30th, 1908 A.D., Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chandra Chaki threw a bomb at Kingsford, but he escaped and unfortunately wife and daughter of Mr. Kennedy who was a sympathizer of National movement were killed. Prafulla Chandra Chaki committed suicide and Khudiram Bose was caught and hanged.
* In Alipore Conspiracy Case (1908 AD), Aurobindo Ghose, his brother Barindra Kumar Ghose all together 49 persons were accused for waging war/conspirary etc. 19 people were sentenced, but Aurobindo Ghosh was released. Chittaranjan Das putting a lot ofefforts saved Aurobindo Ghosh. Kanhai Lal Dutt and Satyendra Bose shot and killed Narendra Gousai (a government witness in Alipore conspiracy case) in the prison. They were sentenced to death. For operating Revolutionary movements in India, a conference of Young revolutionaries was organised at Kanpur in October 1924 A.D., and Hindustan Republican Association was founded in that conference. Its founding members were Sachindra Sanyal (President), Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee and Chandra Shekhar Azad. The organization executed robbery on 9th August, 1925 A.D. at Kakori and looted Government treasure from the train “Eight down Saharanpur - Lucknow passenger train” ofNorth railway’s Lucknow - Saharanpur Zone. Approx 40 revolutionaries were arrested among which Ram Prasad Bismil,Asfaqullah, Roshan Lal and Rajendra Lahiri were hanged. Chandra Shekhar Azad was the key member of Hindustan Republican Association who escaped. Keshab Chakravarthy and Murari Lal Gupta also escaped earlier. Asfaqullah Khan was the first recorded Muslim to be hanged for the struggle ofIndia’s Independence. He was sentenced to death in Kakori case (Kakori Train Action) and was hanged on 19th December, 1927 A.D. at the age of 27 in Faizabad Jail (Now Ayodhya Jail).
* Famous revolutionary Ram Prasad Bismil refused to drink milk when it was offered to him two days before hanging. He said, “I will drink only my mother’s milk”.
* Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA) was founded at Feroz Shah Kotla (New Delhi) in 1928 A.D. by Chandra Shekhar Azad, Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and others. Two members ofthis organization Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt on 8 April, 1929 A.D. threw Bomb at Central Legislative Assembly. The Lahore Conspiracy Case (1929-1930) was a pivotal trial where British authorities prosecuted Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA) revolutionaries namely Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru and others for killing ASP J.P. Saunders to avenge Lala Lajpat Rai's death. After this incident, 18 members of Hindustan Socialist Republican Association were arrested and charged with cases in Lahore conspiracy. Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged and secretly cremated on the bank of river Sutlej at Hussainiwala in Firozpur district on 23rd March, 1931 A.D., becoming a major turning point in India's freedom struggle.
* Chittagong Armoury Raid was an attempt to raid the armoury of the police and auxiliary forces at Chattagaon in Bengal. It was organized by Surya Sen who was also famous by the name of "Master Da".
* The Indian Republican Army (IRA) was established by Surya Sen in Bengal with the Cooperation of namely Ganesh Ghosh, Loknath, Nirmal Sen, Ambika Chakraborty, Naresh Roy, Kalpana Dutt, Ananda Gupta, Tegra, etc. On 18th April 1930 A.D., Surya Sen raided the Chittagong Armoury. Struggle took place on 22nd April, 1930 A.D. between Government forces and IRA in which 80 soldiers and 12 revolutionaries were killed. On 16th February, 1933 A.D., Surya Sen was arrested and faced legal proceedings and ultimately hanged on 12th January, 1934 A.D.
* On 14th June, 1929 A.D. Jatin was arrested on such type of charges under the supplementary Lahore Conspiracy Case. He started a hunger strike for Jail reforms, and due to this long hunger strike which lasted 63 days, Jatin died on 13th September, 1929 A.D.
* The theory of Passive resistance was formulated by Aurobindo Ghosh in newspaper Bande Mataram.
* Subhash Chandra Bose founded Forward Bloc on 3rd May, 1939 after resigning from Presidentship ofCongress.
* In December 1931 AD, Shanti Ghosh and Suniti Chowdhury two school students killed district magistrate.
* Bina Das fired gun-shots at governor sir Stanley Jackson at point-blank range at a convocation in 1932 A.D.
* Bhagat Singh once said, “Criticism and independent thinking are the two indispensable qualities of a revolutionary".
