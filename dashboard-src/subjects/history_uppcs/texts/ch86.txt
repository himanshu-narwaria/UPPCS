CHAPTER 86: Subhas Chandra Bose and Azad Hind Fauj

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose was born on 23rd January, 1897 A.D. at Cuttack, Orissa (Odisha) division of Bengal province. He graduated from Scottish Church College (Affiliated to University ofCalcutta) in 1919 A.D. and passed the Indian Civil Services exam in 1920 A.D. He became president of Congress in the session of 1938 A.D. and 1939 A.D. In 1939 AD, he resigned from Congress presidentship and founded Forward Bloc. He went to Germany in the year 1941 A.D. He gained control of Azad Hind Fauz in the sessions of 1943 A.D. He is often remembered as Netaji.
* Gurudev Rabindra Nath Tagore gave him the title of Desh Nayak.
* Mahatma Gandhi called him as Patriot of Patriots.
* In Germany, with the help ofGerman Government he founded “Free India Centre” where he printed several pamphlets for India's Independence and gave many speeches. Starting from In the year 1941 A.D., Subhas Chandra Bose recruited thousands of Indian war prisoners from General Erwin Rommel ofNorth Africa, and formed an army of thousands of soldiers (war prisoners), this was called as “The Free Indian Legion”. The provisional government of free India (Arzi-Hukumat-e-Azad Hind) was formally proclaimed by Subhas Chandra Bose in October 1943 in Singapore.
* Azad Hind Fauz was basically founded by Capt. Mohan Singh who was British Indian Army Officer. When the British Army was retreating in the war with Japan, Mohan Singh joined hand with Japanese. After this Japanese gave Indian soldiers to Mohan Singh and he started recruiting them in Azad Hind Fauz.
* In December 1941, in Malay Forests, the Punjab battalion led by Mohan Singh was defeated by the Japanese army. Lt. Colonel L.V. Fitz Patrick (English Origin) was the prisoner ofwar but Bangkok resident Indian Giani Pritam Singh took their responsibility and became successful in establishing friendship between the Japanese and Mohan Singh with other Indian soldiers.
* Japanese Major Iwaichi Fujiwara and Giani Pritam Singh encouraged Capt. Mohan Singh to lead Indian National Army. In December 1941, Capt. Mohan Singh agreed to it and in February 1942 under his leadership Indian National Army was founded in which the defeated Indian soldiers of the British Army who went on Malay expedition were recruited. It should be made clear here that the brainchild of INA was given by Mohan Singh, Giani Pritam Singh and Iwaichi Fujiwara.
* Mohan Singh did the brave task ofproviding leadership to it.
* Azad Hind Fauz (I.N.A.) was constituted officially in September, 1942 and Captain Mohan Singh became its first Commander. On 4th July, 1943 A.D., Rash Behari Bose gave the command of I.N.A. to Subhas Chandra Bose.
* On 21st October 1943 A.D., Subhas Chandra Bose established a Provisional Government of Free India (Azad Hind Sarkar) at Singapore. On August 26, 1943, Bose officially took charge of INA and renamed it as Azad Hind Fauj. Fauz was founded on the Singapore. Singapore was under Japanese occupation (1942-1945). Singapore became a part of Malaysia in 1963 A.D.
* On 9th August, 1965 A.D., Malaysia Parliament with a Agreement separated Singapore from State of Malaysia. On this day, Singapore became a free country. So, it is clear that Singapore was part of Malaya when Azad Hind Fauz was formed.
* In Burma (Myanmar), Netaji appealed to his soldiers and said, “we have sacrificed a lot but the ultimate sacrifice of our life is remaining, we need the priests who is ready to sacrifice his life to the Goddess of freedom for the Independence of his country, Give me Blood, I promise you Freedom”. The slogan 'Jai Hind' was suggested by Abid Hasan, Base's secretory, this slogan was formally adopted as the greeting in Azad Hind Fauj. It was the tradition of Salute in Azad Hind Fauz, today it is the slogan of the whole nation.
* Azad Hind Fauz Day was celebrated on 12th November, 1945 A.D. and they celebrated from 5th November to 11th November, 1945 A.D. Azad Hind Fauz week. Soldiers and officers of Azad Hind Fauz were arrested and court martial proceedings against them were filed by the British Government in November 1945 AD at Delhi Red Fort. As a result of which three convicts Prem Sahgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon and Shah Nawaz were sentenced to transportation for life and Abdul Rashid was awarded 7 years of imprisonment.
* Congress constituted an INA Defence Committee, to rescue them in which prominent advocates were - Bhulabhai Desai (leader of the Committee), Tej Bahadur Sapru, Kailash Nath Katju, AsafAli and Jawahar Lal Nehru. There were countrywide protests against the sentence ofthe court, which compelled then Commander-in-ChiefClaudeAuchinleck to commute the transportation for life sentences ofthree defendant and to free other soldiers of INA also.
* Jawahar Lal Nehru succeeded in passing a resolution of Guerrilla warfare against the Japanese in the Congress Working Committee atAllahabad (Prayagraj). He was opposed to Nazism, Fascism and Imperial Ideologies.
