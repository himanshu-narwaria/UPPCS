CHAPTER 28: Mughal Dynasty:Babur

EXPLANATION SECTION
* The Mughal dynasty was ofTurkic-Mongol origin and Babur belonged to Chagatai branch of Turks, which derived its name from the second son of Genghis Khan, ruler of central Asia and Turkistan.
* The Full name ofBabur was Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur. He was born on February 14, 1483 AD, to Umar Shaikh Mirza and Qutlugh Nigar Khanum at Ferghana. After the death of his father, he ascended the throne of Ferghana in June 1494 AD as eleven years and four months, old minor.
* Shaybani Khan invaded Ferghana in 1501 A.D. and defeated Babur at battle of Sar-e-Pul. Babur fled to central-Asia. “Tulughuma” technique ofUzbeks was used by Shaybani Khan in the Battle of Sar-e-Pul in which Babur was defeated.
* After winning Kabul in 1504 A.D. Babur renounced his ancestral title “Mirza” and assumed a new title, “Padshah”.
* Alam Khan was the uncle ofIbrahim Lodhi. He invited Babur to invade India, claiming his right to the Delhi throne.
* In 1524 A.D., during the 4th campaign of Babur, there was a bitter feud between Sultan Ibrahim Lodi of Delhi and Daulat Khan, the governor ofPunjab. Ibrahim Lodi ordered Daulat Khan to visit his capital but Daulat Khan violated his orders and sent his son Dilawar Khan to Babur with a message to help him to dethrone Ibrahim Lodi and enthrone Alam Khan in place of him. It was a golden opportunity for Babur as he had already received the invitation of Rana Sanga, the king of Mewar. He was quite assured that the opportunity to conquer India had come.
* The first Battle of Panipat was fought between Babur and Ibrahim Lodi on 21 April 1526 AD. Babur possessed specific military tactics. The vast artillery ofBabur was one ofthe main reasons behind his victory. The army of Ibrahim Lodi lost the battle despite being greater in numbers. Ibrahim Lodi was killed in this battle. As a result, the entire area up to Delhi and Agra came under Babur’s control.
* Babur declared himselfthe ruler on April 27, 1526 AD and founded the Mughal empire in India. Babur used Tulughma war technique in the first battle of Panipat. Ustad Ali Quli led the artillery and Mustafa commended the battalion ofgunners. Babur's generosity earned him the title of “Qalandar”. Cannons were used for the first time in India in the first Battle of Panipat.
* Babur declared Jehad in the Battle of Khanwa. The battle of Khanwa was fought on March 16, 1527 AD, between Babur and Rana Sanga. Babur defeated Rana Sanga in this battle and assumed the title of Ghazi. In 1528 AD, Babur took control of Chanderi’s Fort. Medini Rai was killed in the war.
* The Battle of Ghaghra took place between Babur and Mahmud Lodi in 1529 AD. Babur won the Battle. This was the last battle fought by Babur.
* He governed through allocation offiefs/jagirs and paraganas, appointing nobles/princess to territorial charges to his vast empire.
* The governance of Badakhshan was given to Humayun, The governance of Kabul, Qandahar and Multan was given to Kamran. Babur appointed different officers in the Punjab region (e.g. Lahore, Sialkot, Dipalpur had separate governors. Mir YusufAli was appointed to Lahore.
* The region of Rajasthan was not included in his empire as it was under the control of various Rajput rulers.
* The founder of Mughal Empire, Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur wrote his autobiography containing various events ofhis life. It was named Tuzuk-i-Babri or Babur Nama.
* Babur mentioned two Hindu empires in his autobiography,
* i.e., Vijayanagara and Mewar (Chittor). The autobiography written by Babur has an important place among autobiographies all over the world. Babur Nama is written in the Chagatai Turkish language.
* Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khanan translated Babur Nama in the Persian language by the order of Akbar.
* The verse compositions written by Babur, are composed in “Diwan” (Diwan-i-Babur) i.e. a collection of his poems. He wrote “Mubaiyan’' in a new verse style i.e. Masnavi, which is a book of Muslim law. The text Risal-i-Usaj (Khat-i-Baburi) written by Babur is considered the most innovative style. The Mughal Emperor Babur’s army chief Mir Baqi built the Babri Masjid in Ayodhya.
