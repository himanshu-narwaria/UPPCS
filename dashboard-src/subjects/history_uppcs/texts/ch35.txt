CHAPTER 35: Music andPaintingsof Mughal Period

EXPLANATION SECTION
* War scenes, birds, animals and scenery were illustrated in Mughal paintings. Humayun started the Mughal style of painting. He met two distinguished painters, Khwaja Abdus Samad and Mir Sayyid Ali, during the years of his exile in Persia and Afghanistan. From these artists Humayun and his son Akbar learned lessons in drawing and painting.
* Akbar got a number of texts painted. The first major project was ‘Dastan-i-Amir Hamza’. Almost 100 painters took part in its preparation, first under the supervision of Mir Sayyid Ali and later, under Abdus Samad. There were about 1400 paintings in it. This book mentioned the heroic work of the Persian hero, Amir Hamza (uncle of Prophet Muhammad).
* The art of Mughal painting reached great heights during Jahangir’s reign. Jahangir was a highly skilled connoisseur ofpainting. Abul Hasan (the title of Nadiru-z-Zaman), Ustad Mansur (Nadir-ul-Asra), Faruq Begh, Bishan Das, Aqa Reza, Muhammad Nadir, Muhammad Murad, Manohar, Madhav, Govardhan, etc were the main artists of Jahangir's court. Abul Hassan made a painting ofJahangir‘s coronation. This painting is inscribed in the Jahangir’s autobiography ‘Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri’ as frontispiece. Thus, Jahangir’s period was called ‘Golden Age of Mughal Paintings'.
* ‘Pahari School’, ’Rajput School’, ‘Mughal School’ are different styles of medieval painting.
* Aurangzeb banned the music as he found it as anti-Islamic, but during the same period, most books on Indian classical music were written in the Persian language. Despite being anti-music. Aurangzeb was a skilled instrumentalist who used to play the Indian instrument called ‘Veena'.
* ‘Todi’ Raga is used to sing early in the morning. It was sung by Bhats and Charans in the emperor’s court.
* During the reign ofAkbar, Tansen and Swami Haridas were prominent Dhrupad singers. Tansen was a famous musician of Akbar‘s court. Tansen‘s original name was Ramtanu Pandey. He was one of the Akbar’s Navratanas. Before coming to the court of Akbar, Tansen was in the court of King Ramchandra of Rewa from where Akbar summoned him to his court and gave a very high post.
* Akbar had given Tansen the title of ‘Kanthabharnvanivilas’.
* Vilas Khan was the musician of Jahangir’s court. The prominent singers of the court of Shah Jahan were Jagannath, Ramdas, Sukhsen, Sursen, Lal Khan, Durrang Khan, etc. Khyal singing became popular in the time of Muhammad Shah ‘Rangila’.
* The Kishangarh style ofRajasthan is known throughout India for its depiction ofcourtesan paintings. Raja Sawant Singh was the real inspiration behind the famous Kishangarh School of Painting of Radha-Krishna theme. The main artists of this style, were Amirchand, Chhotu, Bhawanidas, Nihalchand, Sitaram etc.
* The famous portrait of ‘Bani-Thani’ which is in Kishangarh style, was made by Nihalchand.

(d) Shah Jahan

Jahangir (1605-27) had an artistic inclination. During his reign, Mughal painting developed further as Jahangir was fascinated with art, science and architecture from a young age. The art of Mughal painting reached great heights during Jahangir’s reign. His interest in painting also served his scientific interest in nature. Jahangir maintained a huge aviary and kept a record of every specimen and organized experiments. He patronized the European and Persian arts.

25. Tappa style of music was refined and developed in which of the following Mughal Emperor's Court?

(a) Jahangir
(b) Shahjahan
(c) Muhammad Shah
(d) Akbar
U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
Ans. (c)
In the 18th century, Music in the Tappa style received great encouragement at the court of the Mughal Emperor Muhammad Shah. Sadaranga and Adaranga were two great composers of Khayal gayaki at his court. Several new forms of music such as Tarana, Dadra and Ghazal also existed at this time. Moreover, some folk forms of music were also incorporated in the country music such as Thumri and Tappa etc. Tappa is based upon a style of folk music sung by camel drivers of Punjab. Hence, option (c) is the correct answer.
