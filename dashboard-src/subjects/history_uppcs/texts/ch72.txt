CHAPTER 72: Civil Disobedience Movement

EXPLANATION SECTION
* At Lahore Session, 1929 A.D. Congress Working Committee was empowered to start Civil Disobedience Movement. In February 1930 Congress Working Committee held its second meeting at Sabarmati Ashram and Mahatma Gandhi was given leadership of this Movement.
* On 12th March, 1930 A.D. Gandhiji started his famous Dandi March. He, with his chosen companions, departed from Sabarmati to Dandi for the Satyagraha on 12th March. After 24 days of a long journey (reached Dandi on April 5, 1930) Gandhi on 6th April, 1930 A.D. at Dandi, broke Salt Law by picking up a lump of Salt at Dandi. And with this symbolic act, Gandhiji officially started the Civil Disobedience Movement.
* Subhas Chandra Bose compared this March with the March ofNapoleon from Elba to Paris.
* An English Newspaper editor - Brailsford mockingly said. “Can an Emperor be unseated by boiling sea water in a Kettle?” On this comment, Statesman newspaper replied, “Mr. Gandhi can boil the seawater till they got the Dominion Status”.
* Civil Disobedience Movement spread to the whole country in Gandhiji leadership. In Tamil Nadu, Gandhian leader C. Rajagopalachari started the salt march from Trichinopoly to Vedaranyam on the Vedaranyam beach of Nagapattinam district to break the salt law.
* On 5th April, 1930 A.D., Gandhiji reached Dandi village and addressed the foreign reporters as well as Indian reporters and said "I want world sympathy in this battle of Right against might", On 5th May, 1930 A.D., Gandhiji was arrested before a planned raid of congress workers at Dharsana salt works (godown) and was sent to Yerwada Jail. Abbas Tayabji became a leader of the Movement after Gandhiji’s arrest and Sarojini Naidu became the leader ofthe Movement after Abbas Tayyabji's arrest. Sarojini Naidu led the raid at Dharsana salt godown. This incident was elaborated by American reporter Webb Miller.
* In the North-West Frontier Province (NWFP), Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan founded an organization called Khudai Khidmatgar. They were also known as Red Shirts. This organization emphasized on the national unity also organized movements against British Colonial policies and demanded improvements in the condition of labours. Muslims of NWFR played an important role in Civil Disobedience Movement under the leadership of Khan Abdul Gaffar Khan. During this movement, Garhwal Rifles under the leadership of Chandra Singh Garhwali in Peshawar refused to fire at unarmed gathering.
* Tribals of Manipur played an active role in the Civil Disobedience Movement under the leadership of ‘Gaidinliu’ a Naga tribal woman. The movement led by her is called as Jiyatarang Movement (1930s).
* After the Civil Disobedience Movement, Gandhiji focused on creative programmes. On 17th September, 1934 Mahatma Gandhi issued a public statement announcing his resignation from Congress. In October 1934 A.D., Gandhiji officially retired from active politics to devote all his time in the upliftment of Harijans. In September 1932 A.D., Gandhiji founded an All India Anti-Untouchability League and in 1933 A.D. he started a weekly newspaper named Harijan for the betterment of Harijans.
