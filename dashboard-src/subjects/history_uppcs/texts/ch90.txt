CHAPTER 90: Partition of India and Independence

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Clement R. Attlee was the Prime Minister of Britain when India got Independence from Britishers. He was the Prime Minister from the year 1945 to 1951 AD. Clement Attlee was from the Labour Party. On 20th February, 1947 AD, Attlee in House of Commons declared that by a date before June, 1948 AD we will free India and transfer the power ofthe Government in Indian hands.
* Attlee made Lord Mountbatten the viceroy after replacing Wavell. Mountbatten came to India in March 1947 AD, and started the proceedings of transfer of power. He was given the task of the transfer of power in the manner that will best ensure the future happiness and prosperity of India and take decisions as per the need ofIndia so that Britain could depart from India with honour and with minimal damage.
* Mountbatten during the talks on the transfer ofpower reached a conclusion that partition ofIndia and formation ofPakistan is a must. He prepared a plan, keeping in mind the instructions by Attlee. The plan was prepared for the partition ofIndia, this plan is known as the Mountbatten Plan (3rd June, 1947 AD).
* As per the plan on July 1947 AD, Indian Independence Act was passed by the British Parliament in which two nations India and Pakistan were given dominion status and the deadline for the formation ofPakistan and India as two nations were fixed to 15th August, 1947 AD.
* Indian Independence Bill was presented by Attlee on 4th July, 1947 AD in the House of Commons in British Parliament. House ofCommons passed this Bill on 15th July, 1947 AD and was passed by House ofLords the next day. The Bill got Royal Assent ofhis Majesty on 18th July, 1947 AD.
* Between 24 March and 6 May 1947 AD, in series of 133 full records oftalks with Indian politicians Mountbatten reached on a conclusion that Cabinet Mission Plan had been impractical. So he prepared another substitute plan for this and termed it as “Plan Balkan”.
* Punjab Boundary Commission and Bengal Boundary Commission were formed by Mountbatten on 30th June, 1947 AD to determine the division ofland between India and Pakistan along with Punjab and Bengal. Cyril Radcliffe was the chairman ofboth the Councils. The councils was given the task to demarcate the boundaries of Punjab and Bengal on the basis of Contiguous majority areas of Muslim and Non Muslim majority population. For this task, he had to take in consideration of various other elements too. Each council had four members (judges) two from Congress and two from the Muslim League. Gandhiji, in his first meeting with Mountbatten on 31st March 1947 AD, suggested that the control ofthe Interim Government be handed completely to Md. Ali Jinnah who was the leader of the Muslim League so that these communal riots in India be stopped, but Congress leaders and Congress Working Committee didn’t support this proposal of Gandhi.
* On partition, Gandhiji said, "If Congress wishes to accept partition it will be over my dead body". I will never be a supporter of partition till my last breath. If I were to choose I will never let Congress be ready for partition. Although Gandhi accepted the partition resolution at the All India Congress Committee (AICC) meeting held on 14-15 June 1947. Though he was fundamentally he opposed the idea ofdivision ofIndia. He urged Congress to accept the proposal to avoid further communal bloodshed.
* J. B. Kripalani was the Congress President when All India Congress Committee in Delhi accepted the proposal ofpartition ofIndia on 14-15 June, 1947 AD. This proposal was presented by Govind Ballabh Pant and Maulana Abul Kalam Azad seconded this proposal.
* On November 1947, J.B. Kriplani resigned from Congress and Dr. Rajendra Prasad became the congress President. In Jaipur Session of Congress (1948 AD), Pattabhi Sitaramayya became the next Congress President. In the year 1950 AD, at Nasik session of Congress, Purushottam Das Tandon became the new Congress President. Nehru was President ofCongress from 1951 to 1954 AD.
* Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (Frontier Gandhi) voted against the proposal for India’s partition at the meeting of All India Congress Working Committee on 14/15 June 1947 AD (mention of both dates are found).
* Dr. Saifuddin Kitchlew upon the passing of this proposal said that today Nationalism surrendered to Communalism. He was President ofthe Congress Committee of Punjab Province and was against the partition. After Independence, he separated himself from Congress and later joined the Communist Party of India
* On 14/15 August 1947 AD, at midnight Constituent Assembly assumed full powers for the governance of the country. Under the Act, the existing central Legislature ceased to exist and its place was taken by Constituent Assembly which, primarily a constitution making body, was to function as the Dominion Legislature as well. To avoid confusion, the two functions of the Assembly Constitution making and legislation were clearly separated and the Constituent Assembly (Legislative) commenced functioning from November 17, 1947. The meeting ofConstituent Assembly was held at the midnight of 14 August.
* On this occasion of Independence, Jawahar Lal Nehru gave a very influential speech in the Assembly. On 14th August 1947 AD, in the midnight 'Vande Mataram', 'Jan-Gan-Man' and 'Sare-Jahan-Se Achha' song was sung by Sucheta Kripalani. Jawaharlal Nehru was appointed as Prime Minister of India. Lord Mountbatten was the First Governor General of Independent India (1947- 48 AD) whereas C. Rajagopalachari was the First Indian Governor-General of Independent India (1948-50 AD).
* C. Rajagopalachari during the years 1948-50 AD was the first and last Indian Governor-General of Independent India. He stayed on this post until 26th January, 1950 AD. He was the Chief Minister of Madras from year 1952 to 1954 AD. B.R. Ambedkar was the first Law Minister of free India. He worked as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
* Indian national leaders wanted to make 26th January a memorable day (26 January 1930 Independence day celebration), that is why our constitution came into force on 26th January 1950 AD since it was prepared on 26 November, 1949 AD (many provisions like Article 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 60, 324 etc. have come into force from November 26, 1949). 26 January was declared as ‘Republic Day’.
* R. Coupland said, "Indian nationalism in truth, was the child of the British Raj and the British authorities blessed its cradle". But he forgot that for him Indian nationalism was an undesirable child which was denied milk in the childhood, and after that, efforts were made to choke it till death.
* On the unification of India by British rule K.M. Panikkar said, “The Greatest Achievement of British Rule is the Unity of India”.

47. January 26,1950 was chosen for the enforcement of the Constitution because:

(a) This was an auspicious day.
(b) ‘Quit India Movement’was begun on this date in 1942.
(c) Congress had celebrated 26 January, 1930 as the first Independence Day.
(d) None of the above.
Jharkhand P.C.S. (Pre) 2011
Ans. (c)
In the year 1929 AD, at the annual session of Congress held at Lahore, ‘Purna Swaraj’ or Complete Independence was accepted as the goal of the Congress. On the banks of the river Ravi, at midnight on 31st December, 1929 AD, the tricolour flag of Indian Independence was unfurled amidst cheer and jubilation. 26th January, 1930 AD was marked as the first Independence Day, which was to be so celebrated every year. To commemorate this historic day, it was decided to enforce the Constitution on 26th January, 1950 AD.
