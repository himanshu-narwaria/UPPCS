CHAPTER 56: Partition of Bengal and Swadeshi Movement

EXPLANATION SECTION
* British Government announced the partition of Bengal on 20 July, 1905 A.D. (Resolution date was July 19, 1905) On 7 August 1905 A.D., a historic meeting took place at the town hall of Calcutta and the Swadeshi movement was started. The historic Boycott resolution was passed here. After this anti partition movements started in various parts ofBengal.
* On 16 October 1905 AD, the partition formally came into force and Bengal was divided. The day was observed as the 'Mourning day' in the whole Bengal. On the suggestion of Rabindranath Tagore the day was celebrated as ‘Rakhi Diwas’ amongst the people of Bengal.
* After its partition, the Bengal was divided into East and West Bengal. East Bengal was merged into Assam, and a new province was formed (named as Eastern Bengal and Assam) which included Rajshahi, Chatgaon (Chittagong), Dhaka etc. Decca (now Dhaka) was the capital ofthis province.
* Its second part included Bengal province, Orissa and Bihar.
* Bengal was divided during the tenure of Lord Curzon (1899- 1905 A.D.).
* Sir Andrew Henderson Leith Fraser was an officer of Indian Civil Service. He served as the lieutenant governor of Bengal from 1903-1908 A.D. He played an important role in the planning of Bengal Partition.
* Rabindranath Tagore was a critic ofcertain aspects ofSwadeshi movements like aggressive and exclusive nationalism and reliance solely on boycotts etc., and he emphasised on better relation between the East and West cultures. He believed that the West had committed some mistake in understanding the East, this doesn't mean East do the same with the West.
* Krishna Kumar Mitra the editor of ‘Sanjivani’, published a special edition on 13 July 1905 A.D., and for the first time suggested that people should boycott British goods, and mourn against the partition and should break all linkages from British officers and offices. All of this should be done as a protest against the partition of Bengal.
* A public gathering on 16 July 1905 A.D., at Bagerhat (then in Khulna district, Bengal Presidency, British India) supported the suggestion of Krishna Kumar Mitra.
* British Government called partition a necessity for the administrative purposes but the real reason was to weaken the growth of nationalism in Bengal. According to then viceroy Lord Curzon, “Calcutta is the centre from which the Congress Party is manipulated throughout the whole of Bengal and indeed the whole of India ..... The whole of their activity is directed to creating an agency so powerful that they may one day be able to force a weak government to give them what they desire.”
* The announcement of Partition of Bengal was the immediate cause of Swadeshi movement. Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Aurobindo Ghosh, Bipin Chandra Pal and Lala Lajpat Rai were the prominent leaders of this idea. They wanted a country wide Swadeshi movement but the moderates wanted to keep movement limited to Bengal & preferring constitutional methods.
* The Swadeshi Movement that started in Bengal was spread to throughout the country and Tilak was prominent leader in Maharashtra region. Ajit Singh and Lala Lajpat Rai led the movement in Punjab and U.P., Syed Haider Raja led this movement at Delhi and V.O. Chidambaram Pillai led the Swadeshi movement in Madras Presidency.
* To organise masses for this movement Ashwini Kumar Dutt founded “Swadesh Bandhav Samiti”. Women played an active role in this movement. However, farmers and Muslims showed limited participation in Swadeshi and Boycott movements.
* Vande Mataram became the anthem/ralley cry song ofIndian National movement during the Swadeshi movement.
* British journalist H.W. Nevinson was related to Swadeshi Movement. He travelled for four months across India and he reported for Manchester Guardian, Glasgow Herald and Daily Chronicle. Later on, he compiled his reports in a Book titled ‘The New Spirit in India’.
* In the atmosphere of increasing National Movement Abnindranath Tagore and his brother Gaganendranath Tagore founded Indian Society of Oriental Art in 1907 AD (which was initiated by E.B. Havell) to reconstruct the precious Indian Arts and Culture and to develop new consciousness among the masses.
