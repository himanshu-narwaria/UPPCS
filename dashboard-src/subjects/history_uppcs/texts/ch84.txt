CHAPTER 84: Cripps Mission (1942)

EXPLANATION SECTION
Considering the rising power ofJapan, international community, especially United States of America, China and Australia pressured Britain for political solution settlement with India. Due to urgent need for British action to solve the political problem, Stafford Cripps arrived in India in March, 1942 A.D. to negotiate with Indian politicians.
After reaching India in March, 1942 A.D., Stafford Cripps presented a proposal whose major recommendations were:
i- After Second World War India should be given a Dominion
Status which is consistent with the declaration (i.e. associated by common allegiance to the crown, while not subordinate in domestic/external affairs).
ii- Indians should be given the right to form their own Constitution for which the elected constitution making body will be initiated after the War.
iii- Any provinces not agreeing to the new Constitution would have the right to keep themselves out of the proposed Union. and to retain its present constitutional position.
* Mahatma Gandhi termed this deal as a post-dated cheque on a falling bank.
* Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru and Maulana Azad were the official negotiators from the Congress side in talks with Cripps Mission.
