(a) Delhi
(b) Gwalior
(c) Ujjain
(d) Lahore
I.A.S. (Pre) 2025
Ans. (d)
Gandharva Mahavidyalaya was one ofthe earliest institutions established for the organized training in classical music through a modern method. Pandit Vishnu Digambar Paluskar founded the first Gandharva Mahavidyalaya in Lahore in 1901, which was then a part of undivided India. Paluskar sought to take music education out ofthe exclusive hereditary domain of professional musicians and make it accessible to the wider public.

5. In the context of the cultural history of India, a pose in dance and dramatics called Tribhanga has been a favourite of Indian artists from ancient times till today. Which one of the following statements best describes this pose?

(a) One leg is bent and the body is slightly but oppositely curved at waist and neck
(b) Facial expressions, hand gestures and make-up are combined to symbolize certain epic or historical characters
(c) Movements ofbody, face and hands are used to express oneself or to tell a story
(d) A little smile, slightly curved waist and certain hand gestures are emphasized to express the feelings of love or eroticism.
I.A.S. (Pre) 2013
Ans. (a)
Tribhanga (tri-bent pose) is a standing body position or stance used in the traditional Indian sculpture, art and Indian classical dance forms. Tribhanga is literally means three parts break, consisting ofthree bends in the body; at the neck, waist and knee which gives it a gentle ‘S’ shape. Hence option (a) is the correct answer.
