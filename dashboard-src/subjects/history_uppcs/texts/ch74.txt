CHAPTER 74: Karachi Session of Congress (1931)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* The Indian National Congress held Karachi Session on 29-31 March, 1931 A.D. with Vallabh Bhai Patel as President to ratify/ endorse the Gandhi-Irwin Pact (Delhi Pact).
* In this session for the first time resolutions on Fundamental Rights and National Economic Policies were passed.
* On some people opposing this session (black-flag demonstrations against Gandhiji) Gandhiji said, “They might kill me but they cannot kill Gandhism”.
* This was the first session where Poorna Swaraj was defined.
* Resolutions on Fundamental Rights and National Economic Policies were drafted by Jawaharlal Nehru with the help of M.N. Roy.
* Subhas Chandra Bose termed this session as ‘pinnacle of Mahatma Gandhi's popularity and prestige’.

Ans. (d)
To approve the Gandhi-Irwin Pact, the Karachi Session of Congress took place under the leadership ofSardar Vallabhbhai Patel on 29-31 March, 1931 A.D. Congress passed the resolutions related to Fundamental Rights and the national economic programme for the first time in this session. While getting opposed by a few people in the session, Gandhi said “They might kill me but they cannot kill Gandhism” It was the first time when the Congress described the term ‘Complete Independence’.
