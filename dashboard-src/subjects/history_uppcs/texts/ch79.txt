CHAPTER 79: Tripuri Crisis of Congress (1939)

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Subhas Chandra Bose became the Congress President after being elected unopposed at Haripura (Gujarat) Session of Congress in the year 1938 AD.
* In the year 1939 A.D., Subhash Chandra Bose defeated Gandhiji’s candidate Pattabhi Sitaramayya and became president for a second time at the Tripuri (M.P.) Session of Congress. But he resigned due to his differences with Gandhiji on matters offormation ofthe working committee ofCongress. After that, Dr. Rajendra Prasad became Congress President. After the Tripuri crisis in the year 1939 A.D. Subhash Chandra Bose founded Forward Bloc resigning as Congress President. This organization was based on leftist ideology (it was a left wing nationalist political party).
* When the clouds of Second World War were hovering above Europe, Subhash wanted to take advantage of Britain and Germany wars (World War II) and sought freedom for India with a military blow to British Government in India. He believed in an old saying of Ireland
i.e. “England's difficulty is Ireland's opportunity” that is why he tried to persuade Congress and Gandhiji to take help of Britain's enemy to free India.
