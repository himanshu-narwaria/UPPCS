CHAPTER 51: Political Organisations Established Before Congress

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Raja Ram Mohan Roy was the guide of social reforms and political movements in Modern India. His associates in 1836 for the first time started an association called Bangabhasha Prakasika Sabha. Raja Ram Mohan Roy was the earliest individual to struggle for political reforms in India.
* Zamindari Association or Landholders Society was established at Calcutta in 1838. This society marked the beginning of an organized political activity and use ofmethods ofconstitutional agitation for the redressal ofgrievances. The aim ofthe Society was to safeguard the rights of Zamindars. This Society was founded by Dwarkanath Tagore and his fellow Zamindars.
* The Poona Sarvajanik Sabha was one of the strongest political organisation of 19th century India. It was launched on 2 April 1870 in the Pune district of Maharashtra. It was the result of the labour of many great men like G.V. Joshi, S.H. Sathe and S.H. Chiplonkar. Fresh vigour and energy were infused into the Sabha in November 1871 A.D. with the entry of M.G. Ranade. M.G. Ranade provided fantastic leadership to this Sabha. In 1875 this Sabha presented a petition in British House of Commons. The petition consisted of the demands of representatives of India.
* Indian Association was founded on 26th July, 1876 A.D. by Surendranath Banerjee with the help of Anand Mohan Bose in Calcutta. This was the most important political association before Congress with the tag of an All India Nationalist Organisation. Surendranath Banerjee passed the Civil Services exam in 1869, but as there was some trouble over his exact age he was declared disqualified. He published 'The Bengalee' newspaper for the promotion ofmass consciousness. He was Congress President twice.
* British Indian Association was started on October, 1851 A.D. in Calcutta whose founders were, Radhakant Deb (President) and Debendra Nath Tagore (General Secretary) etc.
* Syed Ameer Ali in 1877 A.D. founded Central National Muhammadan Association at Calcutta.
* Getting inspired by Rajnarayan Basu, Nabagopal Mitra in 1867 founded a Society named ‘Hindu Mela’. The main aim of this society was to promote the nationalist feelings and use of indigenous goods amongst the masses.
* In 1843 A.D., George Thompson founded Bengal British India Society.
* Bombay Presidency Association was founded in 1885 A.D. by Pherozeshah Mehta, K.T. Telang and Badruddin Tyabji. These three are known as Trimurti of Bombay.

1. The first Political Organization established in India in 1838 was known as

(a) British India Society
(b) Bengal British India Society
(c) Settlers Association
(d) Zamindari Association
Uttarakhand P.C.S. (Pre) 2010; I.A.S. (Pre) 1993
Ans. (d)
The first political organization established in India was known as the Zamindari Association or Landholders Society (1838 A.D.) in Calcutta. It was the first political organisation which introduced organized efforts and constitutional remedies for complaints. Its main purpose was to secure the interests of landlords. The founder members were Dwaraka Nath Tagore and his associate landlords.
