CHAPTER 52: Indian National Congress

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Allan Octavian Hume was a retired British officer of Indian Civil Service.
* In 1884 A.D., he established Indian National Union which was the precursor of Indian National Congress.
* Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 A.D. by A.O. Hume and its first session was held on 28-30 December, 1885 A.D. at Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College at Bombay. In this session, Dadabhai Naoroji suggested that the name Indian National Union should be changed to Indian National Congress.
* A total of 72 delegates took part in this session. It was presided over by W.C. Banerjee, and its first General Secretary was A.O. Hume.
* Lord Dufferin (1884-88 A.D.) was the Viceroy of India during the establishment of Congress. He mocked and described Congress as a microscopic minority of the Indian people. The second session of Congress was held in 1886 at Calcutta presided by Dadabhai Naoroji.
* Badruddin Tyabji is recognized as the first Muslim president of Indian National Congress. He presided over the 3rd session of Indian National Congress held at Madras on 27-30th December, 1887 A.D. A total of 607 delegates took part in this Session. For the first time in this session, working operations of Congress was handed to a committee of delegates. A subject’s Committee was appointed for the first time in this session.
* ‘George Yule’ was the first elected European President of Indian National Congress. He presided over the 4th Session of Congress held at Allahabad (Prayagraj) in 1888 AD. British Committee ofthe Indian National Congress was founded under the Presidency of Sir W. Wedderburn in London in 1889 to gather support for Congress and its cause. It was a committee of Indian National Congress. It started a monthly journal called ‘India’ to spread awareness among Britishers about the matters relating to India (which became weekly later).
* Lala Lajpat Rai often called ‘Sher-e-Panjab’, in 1920 A.D. presided over the Congress special session and resolution on the non-cooperation movement was passed by Congress in this session.
* Mrs. Annie Besant having Anglo-Ireland origin, was the President ofTheosophical Society from 1907 to 1933 A.D. In 1916 A.D., she founded Home Rule League and in 1917 A.D. she became the first women president of Congress by presiding over Indian National Congress Session of 1917 A.D.
* 27th Session of Indian National Congress (INC) was held at Bankipore (Patna, Bihar) in December, 1912 A.D. This session was presided over by R.N. Mudholkar.
* A.O. Hume was called the Father of Congress in this session.
* The Indian National Congress held its session at Lucknow in 1916 and the muslim League also held its session at Lucknow in the same year. In this session, a pact was signed between INC and Muslim League is known as Lucknow Pact. The extremist leaders of INC who were excluded 9 years ago were again included. Ambica Charan Mazumdar was the President ofthe Lucknow Session of INC.
* At the session of 1916 A.D. Tilak said, “Swaraj mera janma sidhha adhikar hai, aur mai ise lekar rahunga.” (Swaraj is my birthright, I shall have it).”
* In 1888 AD, Sir Syed Ahmed Khan and Shiv Prasad Singh (King of Banaras) founded United Indian Patriotic Association the aim of which was to restrict Congress from spreading its ideals to masses and to keep people away from Congress.
* In 1900 A.D., Curzon said that “the Congress is tottering to its fall, and one of my greatest ambitions while in India is to assist it to a peaceful demise.”
* Gandhiji recommended dissolution of Congress after Independence ofIndia.
* Lord Wellingdon took part in 30th session of INC (held in Bombay in 1915 and it was presided over by Sir Satyendra Prasanna Sinha). He was then Governor of Bombay.
* Mahatma Gandhi was president for one time in 1924 A.D. at Belgaun (Belagavi) Session of INC.
* Sarojini Naidu (1879-1949 A.D.) was a famous poet and national leader. In 1925 A.D. after presiding over 40th annual session of INC she became the first Indian female president of INC. During 1947-49 AD, she was the Governor of U.P.
* Jawahar Lal Nehru was the president of INC in 1929 A.D. at Lahore, April 1936 A.D. at Lucknow and in December 1936 A.D. at Faizpur Sessions.
* 51st session of INC was held at Haripura of Gujarat presided by Subhas Chandra Bose on 19-21st February, 1938 A.D.
* National Planning Committee was established with Jawahar Lal Nehru as its Chairman. The committee was set up in 1938 by Subhas Chandra Bose, as the president ofCongress (INC), K.T. Shah was its secratary.
* Abul Kalam Azad was president of Congress from 1940-46 AD.
* J.B. Kripalani was elected president at 54th session of Congress. He was also President of Congress during Independence.
* Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramaiyyah presided over the 55th session (December 1948 Jaipur).
* ‘Jan-Gan-Man’ composed by Rabindranath Tagore was adopted as the National Anthem of India on 24th January, 1950 A.D. It was first sung at Calcutta session on 27th December, 1911 A.D.
* Bal Gangadhar Tilak was present for the last time at Amritsar session in 1919 A.D..

1. Indian National Congress was established by-

(a) A.O. Hume
(b) Surendra Nath Banerji
(c) W.C. Banerji
(d) Annie Besant
42nd B.P.S.C. (Pre) 1997; 44th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 2000
Ans. (a)
Allan Octavian Hume (A.O. Hume) was a retired British officer of Indian Civil Service. In 1884 A.D., he established the Indian National Union. After visiting Kolkata, Mumbai and Madras, Hume took the initiative to organize the first Indian National Union to meet in Poona. Its objective was to obtain a share in Government for educated Indians, to create a platform for civil and political dialogue between educated Indians in British Raj. The first meeting was scheduled to be held in Poona, but due to an outbreak ofCholera, it was shifted to Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College in Bombay. The first session ofIndian National Union was held on 28-30 December, 1885 A.D.. In this session on the advice of Dadabhai Naoroji, the organization was renamed as ‘Indian National Congress.’

15. In the first stage of the National Movement, which one among the following was not a demand of the Congress?

(a) To increase the number ofmembers ofthe Legislative Councils
(b) To increase the number of members of the public representatives in the councils
(c) Separation of Executive and Judiciary
(d) Unification of Executive and Judiciary
M.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
Ans. (d)
During the first stage ofNational Movement, Congress demanded a greater voice for Indians in the government and in administration. It wanted the legislative councils to be made more representative, given more power and introduced in provinces where none existed. It demanded that Indians be placed in high positions in the government. Other demands included the separation of the judiciary from the executive, the repeal ofthe Arms Act and the freedom ofspeech and expression.
