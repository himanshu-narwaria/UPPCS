CHAPTER 67: Khilafat Movement

EXPLANATION SECTION
* Shaukat Ali, Muhammad Ali, Abul Kalam Azad, Hakim Ajmal Khan, Hasrat Mohani and Dr. Ansari were members of the Khilafat Committee constituted to launch Khilafat Movement. Generally, the credit for the starting of Khilafat Movement is given to Ali Brothers, Shaukat Ali and Muhammad Ali.
* Sultan of Turkey was considered as the Caliph/Khalifa of the Muslim Empire by the Indian Muslims. In the First World War, Turkey was fighting against the Allied countries. During the First World War, British politicians promised Indian Muslims not to disintegrate Turkey Empire, but as the war ended, the Turkey Empire was dismantled by treaty of severes and came to an end in 1922 (after Treaty of Lausanne, 1922), when The last Ottoman Sultan was deposed and left the capital of constantinople in a British warship.
* Indian Muslims started hating the British Empire and they started a movement to protect Ottoman Empire of Turkey and protect the Caliphate.
* Khilafat Movement which was started against the disintegration of Turkey Empire got popularity and pace when it was joined by Gandhiji.
* Mahatma Gandhi was elected the President of the All India Khilafat Committee, in the All India Khilafat conference held under chairmanship of Fazlul Haq held in Delhi on 23-24 November 1919 A.D. on 24th November.
* Khilafat Movement accelerated with the congress session of December 1919 at Amritsar. Gandhiji looked this as a Golden opportunity for Hindu-Muslim unity. Thus Gandhiji supported Khilafat Movement to get the support of Indian Muslims in the struggle against the British.
* During the Khilafat Movement Hakim Ajmal Khan renounced the title of Haziq-ul-Mulk given by British government in the year 1908.
* Md. Ali Jinnah (then a Nationalist) was against the linking of Khilafat Movement with the Nation’s freedom. He suggested Gandhiji not to bring religion in politics. He was against the Gandhiji’s participation in the Khilafat Movement.

3. Which of the following were the main objectives of the Khilafat Movement?
1. To rouse anti-British feelings among the Muslims of India.
2. To reform the Muslim society.
3. To demand separate electorates and preserve the Khilafat.
4. To save the Ottoman empire and preserve the Khilafat.
Choose the correct answer from the codes given below:
Code:
(a) 1 and 2
(b) 2 and 3
(c) 3 and 4
(d) 1 and 4
I.A.S. (Pre) 1993
Ans. (d)
The Muslims of India considered the Sultan of Turkey as Khalifa (Caliph) of the Islamic empire. In the First World War Turkey was fighting against allied countries. Before the war, the British Government had promised a fair treatment towards Turkey to win the support ofIndian Muslims. Turkey was dismembered and the Khalifa was stripped of his post. This act of the British angered Indian Muslims. As a result, they started a movement to defend the Ottoman Empire of Turkey and to retain Khalifa in his post. The Khilafat Movement started to protest against the partition of Turkey acquired an aggressive approach with the joining of Gandhi in the Movement.

4. Who was elected as President of the All India Khilafat Conference in 1919?

(b) Muhammad Ali Jinnah
(c) Maulana Shaukat Ali
(d) Motilal Nehru
U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 1993
Ans. (a)
The Indian Muslims were incensed when they discovered that their loyalty had been ruined during the war and the assurances of generous treatment to Turkey after the war, a promise made by British statesmen had no intention of fulfilling it. The Muslims like Ali brothers, Muhammad Ali Hasrat Mohani and ShaukatAli and MaulanaAbul KalamAzad had organized a Khilafat Party. Gandhi decided to extend their support to the Khilafat Movement as this was an opportunity ofuniting Hindus and Muslims. Mahatma Gandhi was elected President of the All-India Khilafat Conference held at Delhi on November 23-24, 1919 A.D. on 24th November. It was held under the chairmanship of Fazlul-Haq. The Amritsar Session of the Congress was held in December 1919, which gave a great fillip to the Khilafat agitation.

15. In which city was the conference ofKhilafat Committee in 1920 held that requested Gandhiji to assume the leadership of Non-cooperation Movement?

(a) Lucknow
(b) Lahore
(c) Allahabad
(d) Karachi
39th B.P.S.C. (Pre) 1994
Ans. (c)
In 1920 A.D., Gandhi suggested Khilafat Committee to adopt a programme of non-violent, non-cooperation to protest the Government behaviour. In June, 1920 the Khilafat Committee at Allahabad (now Prayagraj) unanimously accepted his suggestion and asked Gandhi to lead the Movement. In September, 1920, during the special Calcutta session under the presidentship of Lala Lajpat Rai, resolution of Non cooperation Movement was adopted.

17. Moplah Rebellion in 1921 was an offshoot of:

(a) Khilafat Movement
(b) Mutiny of 1857
(c) Swadeshi Movement
(d) Non-Cooperation Movement
43rd B.P.S.C. (Pre) 1999
Ans. (a)
The Moplah peasants revolted against the Hindu landlord’s oppressive policies, which were in alliance with the British. The Hindu landlords redistributed their lands and the Moplahs, who had been suffering, tempted to in revolt, a pitched battle between the British regiment and the Moplahs which killed several Europeans. On the other hand, the Khilafat Movement also turned more aggressive, and the situation had become very confusing, and it was very difficult to make a difference between the meetings of Khilafat Movement and the meeting by Moplah peasants. Thus both the Movements were merged together.
