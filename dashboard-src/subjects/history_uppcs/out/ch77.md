@ 77 | Congress Socialist Party (1934)
## Formation
- May 1934: AICC met at Patna to set up a Congress Parliamentary Board to fight elections.
- Some AICC members met separately and founded the **All India Congress Socialist Party**; its first meeting was at **Patna**. {1}
- Congress Socialist Party's first session was in 1934. {2}
- The party was officially inaugurated in October 1934 at Bombay, where its policies and working methods were decided.
- Founders: **Jayaprakash Narayan** and **Acharya Narendra Dev**; J.P. was associated with this party. {5,6,8,9}
- **Jaya Prakash Narayan** was convener of the All India Congress Socialist Party at Patna, 1934. {7}
- J.P. Narayan = General Secretary; Acharya Narendra Dev = President; Ashok Mehta was among the founder members.
- Not formed in January 1934; Jawaharlal Nehru never formally joined it. {3}
- Members: Ashok Mehta, Achyut Patwardhan, Minoo Masani, Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia, Purushottam Trikam Das, Yusuf Meherally, Ganga Sharan Sinha and Kamla Devi Chattopadhyay.
## Ideology
- Aim: push Congress towards a clear socialist agenda: full independence, abolition of landlordism, nationalisation of key industries.
- CSP was a socialist caucus within the INC; it rejected Gandhi's "anti-rational mysticism" and the CPI's sectarian attitude to Congress.
- Influenced by Fabianism and Marxism, CSP advocated armed struggle as well as non-violent resistance.
- CSP promoted decentralised socialism: cooperatives, trade unions, independent farmers and local authorities to hold much economic power.
- As secularists they hoped to overcome communal divisions through class solidarity.
- CSP did not advocate boycott of British goods and tax evasion, dictatorship of the proletariat, or separate electorates for minorities. {4}
## Bihar Socialist Party
- **Phulan Prasad Varma** formed the **Bihar Socialist Party** in 1931 at a meeting in his home; J.P. Narayan is also named as its founder. {10,11,12}
- It was formed from within the Indian National Congress; influenced by Marx, Lenin and the Fabian Society.
- Bihar Socialist Party is also given as founded by Ganga Sharan Sinha, Rambriksha Benipuri and Ramanand Mishra (in 1991).
- J.P. Narayan belonged to the Bihar Socialist Party; Ganga Sharan Sinha, Rambriksha Benipuri and Ramanand Mishra were also associated with it.
- Sri Narsingh Narayan Azad, a socialist, was an important member of the Bihar Socialist Party.
## Jayaprakash Narayan
- Jayaprakash Narayan is known as **'Loknayak'**. {13,14,15}
- In the Quit India Movement (1942) he secretly organised the masses with Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia and Aruna Asaf Ali.
- 5 June 1974: he called for 'Sampoorna Kranti' (total revolution) from Gandhi Maidan, Patna.
- He was awarded the Bharat Ratna posthumously in 1999.
- **'Jaya Prakash Day'** was celebrated in April 1946 for his release from prison. {16}
- A rally at Bankipur Maidan, Patna demanded his release and criticised the policy of arresting political persons.
## Bombay Manifesto
- **Bombay Manifesto** (May 1936), signed by 21 Bombay businessmen, openly opposed Nehru's preaching of socialist ideas at the Lucknow Session; the business community largely supported it. {17}
