@ 78 | Provincial Election and Formation of Cabinet (1937)
## Election results
- Provincial elections of 1936–37 were held under the **Government of India Act 1935**; provincial governments were formed under it. {2}
- Elections were held in eleven provinces: Madras, Central Province, Bihar, Orissa, United Province, Bombay, Assam, NWFP, Bengal, Punjab and Sindh.
- Results were declared in February 1937; Congress emerged as a power in eight provinces.
- Congress won a clear (full) majority in **five** provinces. {4}
| Province | Congress seats | Total seats |
| Madras | 159 | 215 |
| Bihar | 98 | 152 |
| Central Provinces and Berar | 70 | 112 |
| United Provinces | 134 | 228 |
| Orissa | 36 | 60 |
- Congress was the single largest party in Bombay (86 of 175 seats), Assam and NWFP, without absolute majority in Bombay and Assam.
- Only in Bengal, Punjab and Sindh did Congress lack enough support to form a government. {1,3}
## Congress ministries
- July 1937: Congress formed ministries in 6 provinces: Madras, Bombay, Central Province, Orissa, Bihar and United Province.
- Congress did not form ministries in **Bengal** and **Punjab** in 1937. {5,7,9}
- Later Congress formed coalition governments in Assam and NWFP.
- Assam: Muhammad Saadulla formed a coalition in 1937 but later resigned; Congress government under Gopinath Bordoloi took office in September 1938.
- NWFP: Sir Sahibzada Abdul Qayyum Khan formed a coalition though Congress was the largest party; Congress took over by a no-confidence motion against him.
! UPPSC did not evaluate the question on the number of provinces with Congress ministries in 1937.
- Congress ministries lasted **28 months**. {10,11}
- Congress ministries resigned on 22 October 1939 because India was drawn into the Second World War without consulting Indians.
- Assam (Gopinath Bordoloi) and NWFP governments resigned later, in November 1939.
- Jinnah asked Muslims to mark 22 December 1939 (Friday) as the **Day of Deliverance**. {12}
## Work of the ministries
- Congress Working Committee of **1937** adopted the policy of abolishing feudal intermediaries (land ownership)/peasant proprietorship. {14}
- As a result Congress governments in U.P. and Bihar passed tenancy bills.
- Each Congress province tried to improve irrigation and save farmers from the cruel policies of moneylenders.
- U.P.: Congress won 134 of 228 seats and formed government alone; **Govind Ballabh Pant** was Prime Minister (Premier).
- U.P. ministry: Kailash Nath Katju = Law and Justice; **Rafi Ahmed Kidwai** = Finance (also given as Revenue/Home/Jail). {15}
- Bihar: **Shri Krishna Singh** became Premier; formed his cabinet at Patna on 20 July 1937.
- His ministry resigned in February 1938 to press the Governor for release of political prisoners. {13}
- The Governor conceded and the ministry resumed office; it resigned again in October 1939 over India's participation in World War II.
- **Sir James David Sifton** = first Governor of Bihar after separation from Orissa; in office 1 April 1936 to 10 March 1937. {17}
- Bihar became a separate province in 1936 under the Government of India Act 1935; Sifton had been Governor of Bihar and Orissa.
