@ 71 | Lahore Session of Congress, Proposal of Complete Independence (1929)
## Earlier sessions
- Ahmedabad Session (1921): **Maulana Hasrat Mohani** proposed defining Swaraj as complete independence free from all foreign control, as the goal of Congress. {1,2}
- C.R. Das was elected President of the 1921 Ahmedabad session but was in jail; **Hakim Ajmal Khan** presided as acting President.
- Congress session of 1921 first resolved that India must have a foreign policy.
- Madras annual session (1927) was chaired by Dr. M.A. Ansari; it demanded a constitution for Swaraj, rejected the Simon Commission and passed a Hindu-Muslim unity proposal.
- Madras 1927 objected to sending Indian troops to China, Mesopotamia and Persia (not China and Japan); Purna Swaraj was adopted in 1929, not 1927. {22}

- Calcutta Session (1928): Congress gave the British an ultimatum to accept the Nehru Report within the year or face a mass movement.
- The rift between extremists and moderates was resolved at the 1916 Lucknow session.
## Lahore Session 1929
- Government inaction on the Nehru Report led Congress to declare 'Purna Swaraj' as its goal.
- **Lahore Session** (December 1929) passed the **Purna Swaraj** (complete independence) resolution for the first time. {3,4,5,7,15,17,18,19}
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** was President of the Lahore Session, 1929. {9,16,20}
- It was the first Congress session presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru. {14}
- Nehru prepared and moved the Purna Swaraj resolution and declared Purna Swaraj the aim of Congress. {10,23}
- In his presidential address Nehru said: "we have but one goal today that of independence".
- The Nehru Report, which sought only Dominion Status, was rejected at this session.
- At midnight of 31 December 1929 Nehru hoisted the tricolour flag of independence on the bank of the **Ravi** river at Lahore. {11,12,13}
- The two-nation theory was not rejected at Lahore by any resolution, though Congress as a party opposed it.
## Lahore resolutions
- Lahore resolutions (31 December 1929): no benefit from the Round Table Conference; Dominion Status plan of the Nehru Report given up; 'Swaraj' means Complete Independence; AICC may start Civil Disobedience when it thinks fit.
- Abolition of untouchability was not among the Lahore resolutions. {21}
- The Round Table Conference point can be taken as India's foreign policy, as only the British Government could make India's foreign policy.
## Purna Swaraj Day
- Congress Working Committee on 2 January 1930 fixed **26 January 1930** as 'Purna Swaraj Day' (Complete Independence Day). {8}
- 26 January was to be kept every year as 'Purna Swadhinta Divas'; till 1947 it was called Purna Swaraj Day. {6}
