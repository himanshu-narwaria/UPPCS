@ 20 | Lodi Dynasty
## Lodi dynasty and order of Delhi dynasties
- **Lodi dynasty** (1451–1526 A.D.) was of **Afghan** origin; it was the last ruling family of the Delhi Sultanate. {1}
- Order of Afghan rulers: Bahlul Khan Lodi (1451–1489 A.D.) → Sikandar Shah (1489–1517 A.D.) → Ibrahim Lodi (1517–1526 A.D.). {2}
- Order of Delhi dynasties: Slave/Ghulam/Mamluk (1206–1290 AD) → Khalji (1290–1320 AD) → Sayyid (1414–1451 AD) → Lodi (1451–1526 AD). {11}
- Slave dynasty rulers (1206–90 AD): Qutbuddin Aibak (1206–10), Iltutmish (1211–36), Balban (1266–86); **Ibrahim Lodi** belonged to the Lodi dynasty. {12}
## Bahlul Lodi
- **Bahlul Khan Lodi** founded the Lodi dynasty in 1451 AD.
- He took the throne of Delhi as 'Bahlul Shah Ghazi' and had the Khutba read in his name.
- His most important victory was over **Jaunpur**; Jaunpur was known as **Shiraz-i-Hind** in this period. {3}
- Bahlul Lodi did not treat Hindus with religious fanaticism.
- Reputed Hindu Sardars in his court: Rai Karan Singh, Rai Pratap Singh, Rai Nar Singh, Rai Trilokchandra and Rai Dadu.
- He introduced the 'Bahlul coin', the main means of exchange before Akbar.
- Before his death Bahlul named his third son, Nizam Khan, as successor; some chiefs opposed as his mother (Jaiband/Bibi Ambha) was a goldsmith's daughter.
## Sikandar Lodi
- Backed by some powerful warlords, Nizam Khan took the throne as Sultan Sikandarshah (**Sikandar Lodi**) on 17 July 1489 AD.
- Sikandar Lodi = greatest of the three Lodi rulers.
- In 1504 AD **Sikandar Lodi** founded the city of **Agra** on the Yamuna and made it his capital. {5,6,7,9}
- Agra was founded to command eastern Rajasthan and the routes towards Malwa/Gujarat.
- Sikandar Lodi was very hard-working, generous, kind to his subjects, but religiously orthodox.
- His administration was noted for efficiency; roads were made safe and irrigation facilities given to peasants.
- He worked to improve agriculture and trade and took keen interest in land revenue management.
- He ordered nobles to present details of their expenditure; embezzlement was severely punished.
- He started the land measure **'Gaj-i-Sikandari'**, later used by Sher Shah Suri and Akbar.
- Sikandar Lodi abolished the octroi duty (several levies) on **grain**. {10}
- He arranged free meals for the poor.
- He banned 'Tazia' in Muharram and stopped Muslim women from visiting 'Peers' and 'Mazaars'.
- A list of qualified and skilled persons was made and presented before him every six months.
- He was educated and learned, with knowledge of Persian poetry.
- He had an Ayurvedic treatise translated into Persian and named it **'Farhange Sikandari'**.
- **'Lajjat-i-Sikandarshahi'**, a masterpiece on singing, was composed in his reign.
- Sikandar Lodi was very fond of hearing the Shehnai.
- Sikandar Lodi wrote poems under the pen name **'Gulrukhi'**. {8}
## Ibrahim Lodi
- **Battle of Khatoli (Khatauli), 1518 AD**: Maharana Sanga badly defeated Ibrahim Lodi. {4}
- First battle of Panipat: Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi, 21 April 1526 A.D.; Ibrahim was defeated and killed on the battlefield; the Lodi dynasty ended.
- Per Nayamatullah (Niyamatullah), no Sultan of India other than Ibrahim was killed on the battlefield.
