@ 60 | Delhi Durbar and Change of Capital
## Delhi Durbar, 1911
- In December 1911 a grand **Delhi Durbar** was held to welcome British King George V and Queen Mary.
- On 12 December 1911 George V annulled the Partition of Bengal and announced the reunification of East and West Bengal.
- Calcutta (now Kolkata) was the capital of India under the British until 1911. {1}
- George V announced the transfer of the capital from Calcutta to Delhi on 12 December 1911. {3,6}
- The transfer was completed in 1912; if both 1911 and 1912 are options, 1912 is preferred. {4,5}
- The capital shifted to Delhi during the tenure of **Lord Hardinge II**. {2}
## Bihar and Orissa
- It was announced that Bengal would be reconstituted as a new province; Bengal got this identity in 1912 after East and West Bengal reunited.
- The carving out of Bihar from Bengal was announced at the Delhi Durbar on 12 December 1911.
- The province of **'Bihar and Orissa'** was proclaimed on 22 March 1912 and came into existence on 1 April 1912; Bihar was thus first separated from Bengal in 1912. {11,12}
- In 1936 Bihar became a separate province.
- The Calcutta High Court still exercised jurisdiction over the new province of Bihar and Orissa.
- Assam was restored to its previous position of 1874.
## Hardinge Bomb Case
- A bomb was thrown at Viceroy **Lord Hardinge II** on his state entry into Delhi. {7,8}
- The Delhi Conspiracy Case (Delhi-Lahore Conspiracy, Hardinge Bomb Case) was a 1912 plot to kill Hardinge on the transfer of the capital from Calcutta to Delhi.
- It was hatched by the revolutionary underground of Bengal and Punjab, headed by **Rash Behari Bose**.
- On 23 December 1912 a homemade bomb was thrown into the Viceroy's howdah as the ceremonial procession passed through Chandni Chowk, Delhi.
- **Anushilan Samiti** was responsible for throwing the bomb at Hardinge. {10}
- Fourteen persons were accused; Awadh Bihari, Master Amir Chand, Bhai Balmukund and Basant Kumar Biswas were given death sentences.
- Balraj and Harvansh Sahay got life imprisonment; Dinanath turned approver.
- Bhagwati Charan Vohra was not involved in the Hardinge bomb incident. {9}
- Rash Behari Bose, who played the main role, fled to Japan and escaped the trial.
