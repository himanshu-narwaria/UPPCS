@ 74 | Karachi Session of Congress (1931)
## Karachi Session
- **Karachi Session** of Congress was held on 29–31 March 1931 with **Vallabhbhai Patel** as President. {1}
- It was held to ratify (endorse) the Gandhi-Irwin Pact, also called the Delhi Pact.
- Resolutions on **Fundamental Rights** and National Economic Policy (programme) were passed for the first time at Karachi. {3}
- **Jawaharlal Nehru**, with the help of M.N. Roy, drafted the resolutions on Fundamental Rights and National Economic Programme. {2}
- Karachi was the first session where Poorna Swaraj (Complete Independence) was defined.
- Facing black-flag demonstrations, Gandhiji said: "They might kill me but they cannot kill Gandhism".
- **Subhas Chandra Bose** called the Karachi Session the 'pinnacle of Mahatma Gandhi's popularity and prestige'. {4}
