## Savarkar, Mitra Mela and Abhinav Bharat
- **Mitra Mela** was founded by Vinayak Damodar (V.D.) Savarkar in 1899. {3}
- In 1904 Mitra Mela was turned into the secret society **Abhinav Bharat Society**, founded by V.D. Savarkar. {1,2,5,6,7}
- Abhinav Bharat was set up at Nasik in Maharashtra; branches also opened in Madhya Pradesh and Karnataka. {4}
- Savarkar founded the Abhinav Bharat Society during his college education in Pune.
- Savarkar set up a branch of Abhinav Bharat Society in 1906 at India House, London. {8}
- Savarkar wrote the book **'The Indian War of Independence 1857'**, giving a nationalist view of the 1857 revolt; it carried his views. {9}
- In the book he analysed the circumstances of the 1857 uprising and attacked British rule as unjust and oppressive.
- Through this book Savarkar became one of the first writers to call 1857 India's 'First War for Independence'.
- Savarkar wrote the biography of **Mazzini** to inspire Indian nationalists.
- Savarkar was arrested in London and sent to India by ship; he jumped into the sea from the sailing ship to escape, but was re-arrested as the alarm was raised.
- He had planned the escape and told his friend to keep track of the ship.
- Savarkar was brought to India with others charged in the Nasik conspiracy case and sentenced to life imprisonment.
- In one matching, Abhinav Bharat → G.D. Savarkar, Mitra Mela → V.D. Savarkar, Indian Republican Army → S. Sen, Hindustan Republican Association → S.N. Sanyal. {73}
- Organisation → leader: Abhinav Bharat Society → V.D. Savarkar; Anushilan Samiti → Sri Aurobindo Ghosh; Ghadar Party → Lala Hardayal; Swaraj Party → C.R. Das. {72}
- Abhinav Bharat Society and Anushilan Samiti were revolutionary organisations. {82}
- United Indian Patriotic Association: founded by Sir Syed Ahmed Khan and Raja Shiv Prasad in 1888 to oppose the Congress.
- Kuka Movement is linked with Guru Ram Singh. {78}
## Nasik Conspiracy Case
- Anant Laxman Kanhere, of the Karve Group and a member of Abhinav Bharat, shot dead Jackson, District Collector of Nasik, on 21 December 1909; this is the **Nasik Conspiracy Case**. {64}
- Kanhere, Krishnaji Gopal Karve and Vinayak Deshpande were hanged in the Jackson murder case.
- 27 members of the Abhinav Bharat Society were convicted and punished in the Nasik case.
- Rash Behari Bose was associated with the Delhi Conspiracy Case, not the Nasik case.
## RSS
- **Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh** was founded by Keshav Baliram Hedgewar on 27 September 1925 at Nagpur. {10}
- RSS was founded as a social organisation to give training and unite the Hindu community; headquarters at Nagpur, Maharashtra.
## Chapekar brothers and Tilak
- **Vyayam Mandal** was founded at Poona in 1896-97 by the Chapekar brothers: Damodar Hari, Bal Krishna Hari and Vasudeo Hari Chapekar.
- Vyayam Mandal was founded with clear political intentions.
- In June 1897 the Chapekar brothers killed Rand, President of the Poona Plague Committee, and Lieutenant Ayerst.
- Damodar Hari Chapekar and his brothers were hanged; they were arrested on information given by the Dravid brothers.
- It was alleged that the Brahmins of Poona under Tilak had conspired in the murder of Rand and Ayerst.
- The British charged Tilak with inciting the murders through his writings but got no evidence to prove it.
- In July 1897 Tilak was arrested for sedition under Section 124-A of the IPC and sentenced to 18 months; this caused huge protests across the country.
- This made Tilak an all-India leader and the public gave him the title **Lokmanya** (during the revolutionary movement). {19}
- Tilak started the Lathi Club, Hindu Akhada, Ganapati Mahotsava and Shivaji Mahotsava. {91}
## Anushilan Samiti and Yugantar
- **Anushilan Samiti** was a revolutionary organisation that believed in the path of violence. {17}
- Anushilan Samiti was founded at Midnapur in 1902 by Gyanendra Nath Basu.
- Calcutta: first branch in March 1902 by Satish Chandra Basu; second branch in 1902 by Pramath Nath (P.) Mitra, Jatindra Nath Mukherjee (Bagha Jatin) and Barindra Nath Ghosh. {12}
- Another Anushilan Samiti was founded at Dhaka in October 1906 by Pulin Bihari Das.
- Founders of Anushilan Samiti are also given as P. Mitra and Pulin Das.
- Hemchandra Kanoongo was assistant and bomb expert of the Calcutta Anushilan Samiti.
- Sachindra Nath Sanyal set up a branch of Anushilan Samiti at Patna in 1913. {13,14}
- Barindra Ghosh was the younger brother of Sri Aurobindo Ghosh; influenced by Aurobindo, he joined the revolutionary movement.
- In 1905 Aurobindo Ghosh wrote the pamphlet **'Bhawani Mandir'**, describing in detail a centre for organising revolutionary work.
- Barindra Ghosh, with Bhupendra Nath Dutt, spread revolutionary ideas in Bengal; their activities led to the formation of Anushilan Samiti. {15,16,18}
- **Yugantar** (weekly newspaper) was published in 1906 under Barindra Ghosh and Bhupendra Nath Dutta.
- Jatindranath Mukherjee (1879-1915), a Bengali revolutionary called **Bagha Jatin**, led the reorganised Yugantar Party. {11}
- Jatindranath Mukherjee was a follower of Vivekananda and Aurobindo Ghosh.
- He was an active member of Yugantar, Anushilan Samiti and the Gadar Party.
- Howrah Conspiracy Case (1910) is linked with Jatindranath Mukherjee. {65}
- Aurobindo Ghosh gave the theory of **Passive Resistance** in his newspaper Bande Mataram. {83}
- Aurobindo held that the British must leave India at once and Indians must keep protesting until they leave.
## Barrah Dacoity
- **Barrah Dacoity** (1908) = first major venture of the revolutionary terrorists in East Bengal; at the Barrah zamindar's residence in Dhaka district. {20}
- It was planned and led by Pulin Behari Das, who founded the National School in Dhaka to develop a revolutionary army.
## Muzaffarpur bombing and Alipore case
- Kingsford was Chief Presidency Magistrate of Calcutta; Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki went to Muzaffarpur (Bihar) to kill him. {22}
- On the evening of 30 April 1908 they waited at the gate of the European Club and threw bombs at a carriage. {21}
- Kingsford was not in the carriage; Mrs. and Miss Kennedy, wife and daughter of barrister Pringle Kennedy, were killed.
- Mr. Kennedy was a sympathiser of the national movement.
- Prafulla Chaki committed suicide; Khudiram Bose was caught and hanged. {23}
- **Alipore Conspiracy Case** (1908): 49 persons, including Aurobindo Ghosh and his brother Barindra Kumar Ghosh, were accused of waging war, conspiracy and possessing illegal arms and bombs.
- 19 persons were sentenced; Aurobindo Ghosh was released, successfully defended by **Chittaranjan Das**. {24}
- Kanhai Lal Dutt and Satyendra Bose shot dead Narendra Gosain, a government witness in the Alipore case, inside the prison; they were sentenced to death.
## Hindustan Republican Association
- Revolutionaries of northern India were disillusioned by the sudden suspension of the Non-Cooperation Movement.
- A conference of young revolutionaries at **Kanpur** in October 1924 founded the **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA)**. {25,26}
- HRA founding members: Sachindra Nath Sanyal (President), Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee and Chandra Shekhar Azad.
- Shiv Verma was not a member of HRA; he joined its renewed form, HSRA. {27}
- Bhagat Singh, Chandra Shekhar Azad and Ram Prasad Bismil were earlier members of HRA; Bhagat Singh joined HRA in 1925.
- Shiv Verma was sentenced to life imprisonment in the Lahore Conspiracy.
## Kakori Train Action
- On 9 August 1925 HRA looted the government treasury from a train at **Kakori**, near Lucknow (Kakori Conspiracy / Kakori Train Action). {32,63}
- The train was the "Eight Down Saharanpur-Lucknow passenger train" of Northern Railway's Lucknow-Saharanpur section.
- The robbery was led by Ram Prasad Bismil; others involved: Chandra Shekhar Azad, Sachindra Sanyal, Manmath Nath Gupta, Rajendra Lahiri.
- About 40 (also given as 43) revolutionaries were arrested.
- Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqullah Khan, Roshan Singh (also printed Roshan Lal) and Rajendra Lahiri were hanged. {31,35}
- Chandra Shekhar Azad, key member of HRA, escaped and was never arrested by the British. {33,34}
- Keshab Chakravarthy and Murari Lal Gupta also escaped earlier.
- Surya Sen was not associated with the Kakori incident. {36}
- Bhagat Singh was not associated with the Kakori conspiracy. {37}
- The court appointed **Jagat Narayan Mulla** as public prosecutor in the Kakori case. {38}
- Jogesh (Yogesh) Chandra Chatterjee, a founder member of HRA, got life imprisonment in the Kakori case; he wrote the book 'In Search of Freedom'.
- His book says Pandit Motilal Nehru formed the Kakori Case Defence Committee under the leadership of **Govind Ballabh Pant**. {79}
! An article by Shankar Narayan Rao on the PIB website names Pandit Motilal Nehru as president of the Kakori defence committee; UPPSC first keyed Motilal Nehru, then revised to Govind Ballabh Pant.

- Bismil, an excellent poet, recited a sher just before the gallows: "Malik teri raza rahe, aur tu hi tu rahe; baki na main rahun, na meri aarzu rahe; jab tak tan mein saans, ragon mein lahu rahe; tera hi zikr-e-yaar aur teri hi justju rahe".
- **"Sarfaroshi ki tamanna ab hamare dil mein hai, dekhna hai zor kitna baju-e-qatil mein hai"** was written by Bismil Azimabadi, not Ram Prasad Bismil. {29,30}
- Bismil Azimabadi's original name was Saiyad Shah Muhammad Hasan; born 1901 in Binaha village, Patna.
- The ghazal was published in 1922 in the magazine "Sabah" of Qazi Abdul Gaffar.
- "Daro-deewar pe hasrat ki nazar karte hain, khush raho ahle-vatan ham to safar karte hain" was said by **Wazid Ali Shah**, last Nawab of Awadh. {40}
- Indian revolutionaries used to sing this poem during imprisonment and before capital punishment.
## Kanpur Conspiracy Case
- **Kanpur Conspiracy Case** (1924): some communist leaders were tried at Kanpur to repress the growing power of communists in India.
- 13 originally accused: M.N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shaukat Usmani, Ghulam Hussain, S.A. Dange, M. Singaravelu, R.L. Sharma, Nalini Gupta, Shamuddin Hassan, M.R.S. Velayndhun, Doctor Manilal, Sampurnananda, Satyabhakta. {39}
- M.A. Ansari was not associated with the Kanpur Conspiracy Case.
- Socialist ideology spread in India in the third decade of the 20th century; the government jailed socialist leaders to suppress it.
- Shripad Amrit Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Shaukat Usmani and Nalini Gupta were jailed on false charges in the **Kanpur Bolshevik Conspiracy Case**; each got four years' imprisonment. {66}
## HSRA
- **Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA)** was set up in September 1928 at Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi. {44,48}
- HSRA was founded under the leadership of **Chandra Shekhar Azad**, who changed the name of HRA to HSRA. {41,47}
- Those who reorganised HRA under Azad: Vijay Kumar Singh, Shiv Verma, Jai Dev Kapoor, Bhagat Singh, Bhagwati Charan Vohra and Sukhdev.
- Bhagat Singh was one of the founder members of HSRA. {46}
- HSRA operated more dangerously from 1928 to 1931.
- In December 1928 Bhagat Singh, Chandra Shekhar Azad and Rajguru killed Assistant Superintendent of Police John (J.P.) Saunders.
- Saunders was killed to avenge Lala Lajpat Rai's death: he had ordered the lathi charge on the procession against the Simon Commission.
- Association → founder: Revolt Group → Surya Sen; HRA → Ram Prasad Bismil; HSRA → Chandra Shekhar Azad; Punjab Naujawan Bharat Sabha → Bhagat Singh. {80}
- Ram Prasad Bismil was a founder member of HRA and had a special role in the Kakori conspiracy.
- No organisation named 'Hindustan Socialist Democratic Union' existed in history.
- Srikrishan Saral's book 'Indian Revolutionaries 1757-1961', Vol-3, gives that name for the 1928 body of Bhagat Singh, Chandra Shekhar Azad and Sachindra Sanyal; contemporary documents and authentic histories call it HSRA.
- Yatindranath was martyred in 1915 itself.
- Gopen Chakravarti and Dharani Goswami were members of the Calcutta Anushilan Samiti, linked with the communist movement and accused in the Meerut conspiracy; they were not members of HSRA.
- Womesh Chandra Banerjee was a moderate leader and the first president of the Indian National Congress.
! On the 'Hindustan Socialist Democratic Union' founder question no option is correct; UPPSC took "Yatindranath, Ajay Ghosh, Phanindranath Ghosh" as correct in its initial key.
## Assembly bomb and Lahore Conspiracy Case
- On 8 April 1929 HSRA members **Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt** threw a harmless bomb in the Central Legislative Assembly, "to make the deaf British Government hear". {42,43,45}
- The bomb was thrown while proceedings on the Trade Disputes and Public Safety Bills were going on.
- It was a low-intensity bomb used to protest against an Act, and they ensured there were no deaths.
- Right after the bombing Bhagat Singh and his friends surrendered voluntarily and were put on trial.
- Bhagat Singh was born in September 1907, so he was a little more than 21 years old at the Assembly bombing. {54}
- **Lahore Conspiracy Case** (1929-1930): Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru and others of HSRA were tried for killing ASP J.P. Saunders. {58,60}
- 18 members of HSRA were arrested and charged in the Lahore conspiracy.
- A special tribunal was constituted by the Governor-General; in October 1930 it gave capital punishment to Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev.
- Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged on **23 March 1931**. {55,59}
- Batukeshwar Dutt was not hanged; he was sent to Cellular Jail (Andaman) for Kala-Pani punishment. {57}
- The three were secretly cremated on the bank of the Sutlej at **Hussainiwala** in Firozpur district; Shaheed Bhagat Singh's memorial stands there. {56}
- Every year on 23 March thousands of people come to the memorial to pay homage.
- Their hanging was a major turning point in India's freedom struggle.
- The slogan **'Inquilab Zindabad'** ('Long Live Revolution') was written by Maulavi Hasrat Mohani; Bhagat Singh used it first and made it popular. {51,52,53}
- Bhagat Singh said: "Criticism and independent thinking are the two indispensable qualities of a revolutionary". {90}
- Bhagat Singh's words were not rigid or ideologically regimented; he insisted on a scientific and critical attitude towards all things.
- Chandra Shekhar Azad carried on his activities even after Bhagat Singh's arrest; he had dodged police since Kakori and also escaped in the Lahore Conspiracy matter.
- Azad was the only HSRA member never arrested by police.
- On 27 February 1931 police surrounded Azad in **Alfred Park** (Chandra Shekhar Azad Park), Allahabad (Prayagraj); he was martyred in the gunfight. {50}
## Jatin Das
- Jatindra (Jatin) Das took part in Gandhi's Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921.
- Later he joined the revolutionaries and made bombs to help Bhagat Singh and comrades.
- Jatin Das was arrested on 14 June 1929 under the supplementary **Lahore Conspiracy Case**. {75}
- He began a hunger strike for jail reforms and died on 13 September 1929 after 63 days of fasting. {76}

## Naujawan Bharat Sabha
- **Naujawan Bharat Sabha** was founded by Bhagat Singh in March 1926. {92}
- Early members with Bhagat Singh: Chhabil Das, Yaspal, Ramchandra, Bhagvati Charan Vohra.
- It was a left-wing association that sought revolution against the British Raj by gathering worker and peasant youths and spreading Marxist ideas.
- It was part of the Hindustan Republican Association.
- Ambika Chakravarti was not an early member; he joined the Naujawan Bharat Sabha at a later stage. {81}
- Ambika Chakravarti was a member of the Chittagong Jugantar Party and took part in the Chittagong Armoury Raid led by Surya Sen.
## Chittagong Armoury Raid
- **Chittagong Armoury Raid**: an attempt to raid the armoury of the police and auxiliary forces at Chittagong in Bengal.
- It was organised by **Surya Sen**, also famous as "Master Da". {67,68,69,70}
- Surya Sen set up the **Indian Republican Army (IRA)** in Bengal; he also established the Revolt Group at Chittagong.
- IRA members with Surya Sen: Ganesh Ghosh, Loknath, Nirmal Sen, Ambika Chakraborty, Naresh Roy, Kalpana Dutt, Ananda Gupta, Tegra (Tegrabal).
- The armoury was raided on 18 April 1930.
- On 22 April 1930 government forces and the IRA fought; 80 soldiers and 12 revolutionaries were killed.
- Surya Sen was arrested on 16 February 1933 and hanged on 12 January 1934.
- Kalpana Dutt was arrested with Surya Sen and got life imprisonment.
