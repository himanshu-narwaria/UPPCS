@ 88 | Constituent Assembly (1946)
## Growth of the idea
- The earliest non-official draft of the Constituent Assembly idea was in the **Swaraj Bill of 1895**, shaped by Bal Gangadhar Tilak's directions.
- In the 20th century Gandhiji first pointed to this idea in **1922**, saying the Indian Constitution should be made as per India's need.
- On **8 February 1924** Motilal Nehru moved the **"National Demand"** resolution in the Central Legislative Assembly.
- It called for a round table conference to set up a constitution-making body; a pivotal early demand for a Constituent Assembly.
- After this **M.N. Roy** proposed the demand; Jawaharlal Nehru restated its basic concepts.
- The **Swaraj Party** demanded a Constituent Assembly at Ranchi in **1934**.
- **Congress** officially stated the idea at its **Faizpur Session (December 1936)**, explaining it at length and stressing its importance. {1}
## Formation under the Cabinet Mission Plan
- The Constituent Assembly that framed India's Constitution was set up under the **Cabinet Mission Plan**. {2,3}
- The Cabinet Mission arrived at Delhi on 24 March 1946.
- Secretary of State Pethick Lawrence said its aim was a plan to form a Constituent Assembly for free India's constitution and to arrange an interim government.
- The Assembly was to be made of representatives of provincial legislative assemblies, Chief Commissioners' provinces and princely states.
- Each province got seats roughly at **one seat per ten lakh (10 lakh) population**, on a communal basis. {4}
- Three voter groups: **General, Muslims and Sikhs** (Sikhs only in Punjab).
- The Constituent Assembly was **elected by the Provincial Assemblies in 1946**; first-ever elections for it, for 296 British Indian province seats, completed by July 1946. {8}
- Results: **Congress 208**, **Muslim League 73**, others/independents 15.
- **Mohammad Ali Jinnah** was not a member of the Constituent Assembly of India.
- The National Executive of the **Socialist Party** (1946) called for dissolving the Constituent Assembly and re-electing it by universal adult suffrage. {9}
- Socialist leaders Jayaprakash Narayan and Ram Manohar Lohia argued that an Assembly indirectly elected by Provincial Legislatures did not properly represent common Indians.
## Meetings and chairmen
- The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly was held on **9 December 1946**. {5}
- **Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** was its first provisional (temporary) Chairman.
- Sachchidananda Sinha was associated with the **Quit India Movement**. {10}
- On **11 December 1946**, at Delhi, **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was elected permanent Chairman (President) of the Constituent Assembly. {6}
- At its formation the Constituent Assembly was not a sovereign body.
- At midnight of 14 August 1947 (15 August 1947) it was declared sovereign for the Dominion of India, with **Rajendra Prasad** as its President. {7}
- The Constituent Assembly was the first Parliament of independent India.
- Rajendra Prasad later became the first President of India.
- The Constitution of India was adopted on **26 November 1949**.

## G.V. Mavalankar
- **G.V. Mavalankar** was Speaker of both the Central Legislative Assembly (British era) and the Lok Sabha of free India. {11}
- Speaker of Central Legislative Assembly: 24 January 1946 to 14 August 1947.
- First Speaker of Lok Sabha after independence: 1952 to 1956.
- Speaker (President) of Bombay Legislative Assembly: 1937 to 1946.
