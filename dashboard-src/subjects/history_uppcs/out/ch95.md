## Bharat Ratna
- **Bharat Ratna**: highest civilian award of India, instituted in 1954; for exceptional service / performance of the highest order in any field of human endeavour.
- First President Dr. Rajendra Prasad ordered its establishment on 2 January 1954.
- First Bharat Ratna (1954) went to Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, C. Rajagopalachari and C.V. Raman (not Jawaharlal Nehru). {20,21,22,23}

- **Nelson Mandela** got the Bharat Ratna for his work to end apartheid in South Africa; his movement was based on Gandhian values.
- **Khan Abdul Gaffar Khan**: founder of 'Khudai Khidmatgar'; first non-Indian (Pakistani) to get the Bharat Ratna; called Bacha Khan and Frontier Gandhi.
- Gaffar Khan was born in 1890 in a prosperous family of Utmanzai (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan).
- In 1985 Gaffar Khan came to India for the centenary celebrations of the Indian National Congress; two years later he got the Bharat Ratna.
- Atal Bihari Vajpayee had not yet got the Bharat Ratna in 2005; he got it only in 2015. {24}

- Amjad Ali Khan, Dr. Kurien and Dr. Sarvepalli Gopal did not get the Bharat Ratna (as stated for a 2004 question).
- **Nargis Dutt** was the first Indian actress to get the Padma Shri, in 1958. {13}
## Nobel Prize
- By Alfred Nobel's last will: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences gives the Nobel in Physics, Chemistry and Economic Sciences.
- Nobel Assembly at the Karolinska Institute gives the Nobel in Physiology or Medicine.
- The **Swedish Academy** gives the Nobel in Literature; the Norwegian Nobel Committee gives the Nobel Peace Prize. {30}

## Magsaysay, Booker and Oscar
- First **Ramon Magsaysay Awards** ('Asian Nobel') were given in 1958 to five individuals working in India, Indonesia, Philippines, Republic of China (Taiwan) and Sri Lanka.
- **Vinoba Bhave** was the first Indian to get the Magsaysay Award (1958), for government service and community leadership. {3}
- The Magsaysay Award was founded by the Ramon Magsaysay Foundation, Philippines.
- Journalist **Arun Shourie** got the Ramon Magsaysay Award in 1982. {38}
- **V.S. Naipaul**: first person of Indian origin to win the Booker Prize, in 1971 for 'In a Free State'. {29}
- **Satyajit Ray** got a Special Oscar (Academy Honorary Award) in 1992 for his outstanding contribution to films. {33}
## Literary awards
- **Ashapurna Devi** was the first woman to get the Jnanpith Award, in 1976, for 'Pratham Pratishruti'. {16}
- Urdu poet **Firaq Gorakhpuri** got the Bharatiya Jnanpith Award in 1969 for 'Gule Nagma'. {34}
- Hindi fiction writer **Chitra Mudgal** got the Vyas Samman in 2003 for her novel 'Avaan'; first woman recipient. {27}
- **Moortidevi Award**: annual literary award of the Bharatiya Jnanpith for work stressing Indian philosophy and cultural heritage.
## Sports awards
- **Arjuna Award** is given for excellence in sports. {5}
- **Dronacharya Award**: given by the Government of India for excellence in sports coaching. {4}
- Dronacharya Award has a bronze statue of Dronacharya, a scroll of honour and cash.
- Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (now Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna) and Arjuna Awards are given yearly for good performance in sports over four years at international level.
- Arjuna Award prize money in the period of one 2003 question was Rs. 3.0 lakh. {6}
- In 2020 prize money was raised to Rs. 25 lakh for Khel Ratna and Rs. 15 lakh for Arjuna Award.
- **Maharana Pratap Award**: set up in 1982-83; for excellence in sports, only for players of Rajasthan. {32}

## Indian cinema firsts
- First Indian film nominated for an Oscar: **Mother India** (released 1957), directed by Mehboob Khan; not Mughal-e-Azam. {35}
- Mother India did not win in the Best Foreign Language Film (Best International Feature Film) category.
- **Keechak Vadham** (1918), directed by Nataraja Mudaliar: first full-length Tamil silent film.
- Playback singing was first used in 1935 in 'Dhoop Chhaon', directed by Nitin Bose.
- 'Dhoop Chhaon' was a remake of the Bengali film 'Bhagya Chakra', where playback singing was in fact first used.
- First Indian Cinemascope film: Kagaz Ke Phool.
