@ 75 | Round Table Conference
## First Round Table Conference
- Simon Commission's two-volume report was published on 10 June and 24 June 1930; political organisations rejected it.
- **First Round Table Conference** was held in London from November 1930 to January 1931 (summoned 1930), at St. James Palace. {4}
- It was held in an atmosphere of frustration; the main Congress leaders were in jail.
- 89 Indian delegates took part; Congress did not attend the First Conference. {2}
- Main objective of the Round Table Conference: discuss the Simon Commission Report and improve the political situation.
- Participants: Tej Bahadur Sapru, Srinivas Shastri, **Maulana Muhammad Ali** (Jauhar), Muhammad Shafi, Aga Khan III, Fazlul Haq, Muhammad Ali Jinnah, Homi Mody, M.R. Jayakar, Dr. B.S. Moonje, B.R. Ambedkar and Sundar Singh Majithia. {1}
- **K.T. Paul** represented Indian Christians at the First Conference. {3}
- It was inaugurated by the King Emperor (George V) and chaired by British PM **Ramsay MacDonald**.
- At the First Conference Dr. Ambedkar demanded separate electorates for the depressed classes; Poona Pact gave them representation in local bodies and civil services; Congress skipped the Third. {25}
## Second Round Table Conference
- **Second Round Table Conference**: 7 September 1931 to 1 December 1931, London.
- **Mahatma Gandhi** was the sole official representative of Congress at it, after the Gandhi-Irwin Pact. {8}
- Gandhi attended only the Second Round Table Conference. {9,11}
- Congress took part for the first time, and only, in the Second Conference. {14,15}
- Sarojini Naidu, Madan Mohan Malviya and Annie Besant also took part with Gandhi. {5}
- Others present: Mahadev Desai, B.R. Ambedkar; Pyarelal Nayyar came as Gandhi's private secretary; **Jawaharlal Nehru** did not attend. {6}
- Dr. Rajendra Prasad did not take part in the Second Conference. {7}
- False: that Nehru represented Congress at the Second Conference; true: the Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931) implied Congress would attend it. {10}
- Gandhi-Irwin Pact (5 March 1931) terms: stop Civil Disobedience, Congress to join the Round Table Conference, withdraw ordinances curbing Congress, withdraw prosecutions.
- Gandhi sailed from Bombay to London on the ship **S.S. Rajputana**. {13}
- In London Gandhi stayed at **Kingsley Hall**. {12}
- The Second Conference failed completely over communal issues (communal delegations). {17}
- Ambedkar demanded separate electorates for Dalits (depressed classes); Gandhi refused.
- The conference ended on 1 December 1931; Gandhi returned to India from London empty-handed in December 1931. {16}
- Gandhi said: "It is true that I returned barehanded but I am content that the Flag I had been handed was never let down by me and I never compromised with the honour of that flag".
## Third Conference and overall
- **Third Round Table Conference**: 17 November 1932 to 24 December 1932. {23}
- Congress did not send representatives to the First and Third Conferences. {24}
- **B.R. Ambedkar** took part in all three Round Table Conferences. {19,20,21}
- Ambedkar is given as the only Indian to take part in all three; it is also given that both Dr. Ambedkar and Tej Bahadur Sapru attended all three.
- The three were not separate conferences but three sessions of a single Conference, so calling them First, Second, Third Conferences is incorrect. {22}
