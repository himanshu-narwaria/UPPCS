@ 69 | Formation of Swaraj Party (1923)
## Background
- Government of India Act 1919 set up the Central Legislature (Council of States and Legislative Assembly) and Provincial Legislative Councils.
- Congress boycotted them on Gandhiji's direction and did not contest the 1920 elections.
- Withdrawal of Non-Cooperation and Gandhiji's arrest created an atmosphere of hopelessness.
- After Non-Cooperation, Congress split into pro-changers (enter legislatures) and no-changers (boycott legislatures).
- Pro-changer leaders: C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru and Vithalbhai Patel; no-changer leaders: Vallabhbhai Patel, C. Rajagopalachari and Rajendra Prasad.
- **C. Rajagopalachari** and **Rajendra Prasad** were not members of the Swaraj Party. {10,11}
- C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru proposed that Congress fight elections, enter the councils and continue the struggle from within; most Congressmen rejected it.
- The split came at the Gaya session of Congress, December 1922.
## Swaraj Party
- **Swaraj Party** was formed after the failure (withdrawal) of the Non-Cooperation Movement. {3,4}
- Founders: **C.R. Das** and **Motilal Nehru**, in January 1923, as a party within the Congress. {5,6,7,8,9}
- C.R. Das, then Congress President, resigned the presidency to form the Swaraj Party. {2}
- Reasons for founding: withdrawal of Non-Cooperation by Gandhi; entering councils to wreck the Government of India Act 1919 from within by obstruction. {1}
- C.R. Das = President of the party; Motilal Nehru = General Secretary.
- Other main leaders: S. Srinivasa Iyengar (founder of the Swaraj Party at Madras) and N.C. Kelkar.
- **Sri Krishna Singh** led the Swaraj Dal branch formed in Bihar. {12}
- Legislative Council elections were held in November 1923; Swarajists won 42 of 104 elected seats and a clear majority in the Central Provinces assembly.
- In the Central Legislative Assembly Swarajists joined hands with liberals and independents like **Jinnah** and Malviya. {14}
- The party demanded responsible Government in India with necessary changes in the Government of India Act 1919.
- **Vithalbhai Patel** became the first Indian Speaker (President) of the Central Legislative Assembly in 1925 (President in August 1925); biggest achievement of the Swaraj Party. {20,21,22}
- Vithalbhai Patel was also a co-founder of the Swaraj Party.
## C.R. Das
- **Chittaranjan Das** was called **'Deshbandhu'** = 'Friend of the Country'. {15}
- He studied law in London and became an advocate in India; a lawyer and revolutionary freedom fighter.
- He defended Aurobindo Ghose and won his acquittal in the Alipore Bomb Conspiracy Case.
- After Non-Cooperation failed he proposed a strategy to end dyarchy; Congress did not accept it.
- C.R. Das gave the slogan "Swaraj should be for common people, not only for classes" ("Swaraj for the masses, not for the classes"). {16}
- To him, "Swaraj is government by the people and for the people."
## Other parties
- Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms were framed at the end of World War I and embodied in the Government of India Act 1919.
- Surendranath Banerjee and other Liberal leaders welcomed the reforms; when Congress condemned the report in 1918, moderates left and formed the **Indian (National) Liberal Federation** (also called All India Liberal Federation). {17,18}
- In 1918 the moderates accepted the Montford Reforms report and the extremists opposed it; the old moderate remnants who broke away included Sapru, Jayakar and Chintamani.
- It was founded in 1918 by Surendra Nath Banerjee; T.B. Sapru, V.S. Srinivas Sastri and M.R. Jayakar were prominent leaders.
- **Madan Mohan Malviya** and **Motilal Nehru** decided to launch the Independent Party on 16 December 1922. {19}
- Madan Mohan Malviya was a founder member of the Hindu Mahasabha; he founded Banaras Hindu University in 1916.
