@ 83 | Individual Satyagraha (1940)
## Start and aims
- After completely rejecting the **August Offer**, Congress started Individual Satyagraha under Gandhiji's leadership.
- It was a symbolic moral protest against the British Government's policy towards India.
- Individual Satyagraha started on **17 October 1940**.
- In October 1940 Gandhiji called for a limited Satyagraha by a few selected individuals; it was kept limited so as not to disrupt Britain's war efforts. {8}
- Two aims: show Indian people's displeasure at British India's participation in the war, and give the British another chance to accept Indian demands.
- Gandhiji and Congress did not want a mass movement to disrupt the British war effort because of their anti-Nazi stand.
## The Satyagrahis
- **Acharya Vinoba Bhave** was chosen by Gandhi as the **first Satyagrahi**; he started the Individual Civil Disobedience. {1,3,4}
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** was the second Satyagrahi. {5}
- **Brahma Dutt** was the third Satyagrahi. {2}
- Vinoba Bhave started his Individual Satyagraha from **Pavnar, Maharashtra**. {6}
## Sarvodaya
- **Sarvodaya** means "Universal Uplift" or "Progress of all".
- The term was first coined by **Mahatma Gandhi** as the title of his **1908** translation of John Ruskin's tract on political economy, **"Unto This Last"**. {7}
- Gandhi later used the term for the ideal of his own political philosophy.
- Vinoba Bhave founded the **"Sarvodaya Samaj"** in **1948** to spread Gandhiji's ideals.
