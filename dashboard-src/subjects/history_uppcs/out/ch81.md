@ 81 | Second World War
## The war: dates and British Prime Ministers
- Second World War began on **1 September 1939** when Germany attacked Poland.
- It ended after six years, in September **1945**. {5}
- British Prime Ministers during the war (1939-45): **Neville Chamberlain** (1937-40) and **Winston Churchill** (1940-45). {6}
- Churchill's tenures as Prime Minister: 1940-45 and 1951-55.
## India dragged into the war
- On **3 September 1939**, Viceroy **Lord Linlithgow** announced that India stood against Germany in the war.
- Linlithgow put India into the war without consulting Indian people, leaders or elected councils, and declared an Emergency in the country; this angered Indian officials.
## Congress policy on the war
- Congress policy: cooperate with Britain only on assurance of **complete independence**; no support for the war until independence was granted. {1}
- Congress Working Committee asked Britain to announce its war objectives and demanded India's independence after the war.
- Congress resolution condemned the Nazi attack on Poland and criticized the ideology of **Nazism and Fascism**.
- The resolution said India could not join a war fought to protect democratic liberty while India itself was denied liberty under British colonial rule.
- So it is false that Congress assisted the British in the war, and false that it did so expecting complete independence. {2}
## Resignation of Congress ministries 1939
- Congress ministries in all provinces resigned in **1939** because the Viceroy declared war against Germany without the consent of the Congress ministries (Indians). {3,4}
- The ministries resigned after **28 months** in office.
- Not the causes of resignation: failure to form cabinets in the other four provinces, rise of the Congress left wing, or communal turbulence in provinces.
