@ 65 | Trade Union and Communist Party
## Early trade unions
- **Madras Labour Union** (1918), formed by **B.P. Wadia** and V. Kalyanasundaram = first modern trade union of India. {1}
- It mainly had textile workers of the Buckingham and Carnatic Mills.
- Soon after, two unions were formed in Bombay, one in Calcutta (the Indian Seamen's Union) and four in Madras.
- **Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association** set up 25 February 1920 by Anasuya Sarabhai (its president) and Mahatma Gandhi, after the 1918 Ahmedabad mill strike. {2}
## All India Trade Union Congress
- **All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)** founded by N.M. Joshi on 31 October 1920 at Bombay. {5,6}
- **Lala Lajpat Rai** = first President of AITUC; presided over its first (inaugural) session at Bombay, 1920. {3,4,7,8,10}
- Joseph Baptista = Vice President; Dewan Chaman Lal (Bajaj) = General Secretary; all were founding members.
- Motive for forming AITUC: establishment of the International Labour Organization in 1919; Indian workers decided to organise as unions.
- AITUC split for the first time in 1929 at Nagpur; **Jawaharlal Nehru** presided over that session. {9}
- Nagpur 1929 session showed a trial of strength between leftists and rightists (moderates or reformists).
- Moderates under N.M. Joshi, V.V. Giri and B. Shiva Rao set up a separate **Indian (All India) Trade Union Federation** in 1929; it was not founded by S.A. Dange.
- Revolutionary (militant) phase of the trade union movement = 1926–1939. {15}
- Main leaders of this phase: M.N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shripad Amrit Dange, Shaukat Usmani.
## Communist movement
- **Communist International** founded in March 1919 by Vladimir Lenin and the Bolshevik Party of Russia.
- **M.N. Roy** went to Russia (Moscow) on Lenin's invitation and became the first Indian member of the Communist International. {11}
- 17 October 1920: M.N. Roy headed the group that founded the Indian Communist Party at **Tashkent**. {12}
- Other Tashkent founders: Abani Mukherjee, Mohd. Ali, Mohd. Shafique, Evelyn Roy Trent, Rosa Fitingov and M.P.T. Acharya.
- **Kanpur Conspiracy Case** (1924) was against leaders of the Communist movement, to stop growing communist influence. {13}
- Kanpur accused: M.N. Roy, Shripad Amrit Dange, Ghulam Hussain, Ramcharan Lal Sharma, Muzaffar Ahmed, Shaukat Usmani, Singaravelu Chettiar, Nalini Gupta, Shamuddin Hassan, M.R.S. Velayndhun, Dr. Manilal, Sampurnanand and Satyabhakta.
- Kanpur charge: conspiracy to set up a revolutionary organisation; "to deprive the Emperor of his sovereignty over British India" by complete separation through violent revolution.
- Meerut and Peshawar conspiracy cases were also against Communist leaders.
- **Meerut Conspiracy Case** (1929–33) drew world-wide publicity; Einstein, H.G. Wells, Harold Laski and Roosevelt criticised it and sympathised with the convicts. {14}
- In the Meerut case 32 labour leaders were charged; 31 were arrested, including two Britishers.
## Parties
- **Labour Swaraj Party** formed in Bengal in 1925 under Congress; became Workers and Peasants Party of Bengal in 1926.
- Workers and Peasants Party of Bombay Province formed in 1927 from the Congress Labour Party (which arose within Congress in Bombay Province in 1926).
- Workers and Peasants Party formed in United Province in 1928; in Punjab it was named **Kirti Kisan Party**.
- **All India Workers and Peasants Party** was formed at the Calcutta conference of these provincial parties in December 1928 (not 1927).
- Aim of the Workers and Peasants Party: work within Congress to make it more revolutionary and an organisation of common people. {18}
- **M.N. Roy** founded the **Radical Democratic Party** in 1940. {16}
- 'Bandi Jeevan' is a masterpiece written by Shachindranath Sanyal.
- **Saumyendranath Tagore** founded the **Revolutionary Communist Party of India (RCPI)** in 1934, breaking away from the CPI. {17}
